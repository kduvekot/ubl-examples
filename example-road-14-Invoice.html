Invoice: Trasvia Haulage to Lumeway Distribution

Example 14 of the flow "Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)" · Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification

The carrier invoices the transport to the party that booked it; under FCA that is the buyer, not the seller. A transport service is invoiced with the ordinary Invoice; the Freight Invoice is deprecated in UBL 2.5.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
