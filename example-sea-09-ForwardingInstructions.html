Forwarding Instructions: Lumeway Distribution to Bayforth Forwarding

Example 9 of the flow "Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)" · Forwarding Instructions in the UBL 2.5 specification

The distributor instructs its forwarder: book the sea freight to the island and insure the cargo (CIF obliges the seller to insure).

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
