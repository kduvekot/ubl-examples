Billing rules for public bodies of Bayvarra

National specification in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:XB:public-billing:1.0

Bayvarra's rules for every invoice to one of its public bodies, whatever the seller's country: the withholding of 10 % of the price of a service supplied by a business not established in Bayvarra, which the public body pays to the state, and the supplier's tax representative in Bayvarra. The tax on the invoice is the seller's own: a Bayvarran supplier applies the state sales tax's exemption for public bodies, a foreign supplier its own country's treatment.

Owner: Government of Bayvarra (federal procurement and tax). It applies to Invoice, CreditNote to a public body of Bayvarra, on top of the Trade Accord's transaction, and is chained after it.

Document types: Invoice, CreditNote.

Examples that claim it:

  • furniture 17-Invoice
  • tender 32-Invoice
  • tender 33-CreditNote
  • tender 39-Invoice
