Billing rules for public bodies of Bayvarra

National specification in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:XB:public-billing:1.0

Bayvarra's rules for invoices to its public bodies: the exemption from the state sales tax, the withholding of 10 % of the price of a service by a business not established in Bayvarra, and the supplier's tax representative. A public body's own rules come first: an invoice to it follows them whatever the seller's country, as a national rule for public buyers does.

Owner: Government of Bayvarra (federal procurement and tax). It applies to Invoice, CreditNote to a public body of Bayvarra, on top of the Trade Accord's transaction, and is chained after it.

Document types: Invoice, CreditNote.

Examples that claim it:

  • furniture 17-Invoice
  • tender 32-Invoice
  • tender 33-CreditNote
