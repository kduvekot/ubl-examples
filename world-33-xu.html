How UBL helps

In an unbundled market the retailer bills, but the grid operator owns and reads the meters. UBL keeps the two apart: the grid operator's Utility Statement carries the readings to the retailer, and the retailer's Invoice and Utility Statement carry them on to the customer.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Sends

  • Utility Statement to Valdara Industries: The factory's monthly electricity consumption: meter readings and consumption per connection, in the energy and water supply structure. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: The electricity bill, with Urvania's VAT; it refers to the utility statement for the consumption. See the example document.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Ferrano Grid: The grid operator reads the factory's gas meter and sends the retail company the consumption: the volume at line pressure, converted to standard cubic metres and to energy. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, CustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SubscriberParty.

  • Invoice from Ferrano Grid: The grid operator charges the retail company for the use of the networks at the factory's and the warehouse's connections. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Invoice from Montebrio Power: The power station invoices the electricity it fed in for the retail company in March. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Invoice from Kelpstone Gas: The gas producer invoices the gas it fed into the grid for the retail company's customers. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.
