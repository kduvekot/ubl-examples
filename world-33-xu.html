How UBL helps

In an unbundled market the retailer bills, but the grid operator owns and reads the meters. UBL keeps the two apart: the grid operator's Utility Statement carries the readings to the retailer, and the retailer's Invoice and Utility Statement carry them on to the customer.

In this world it sends 4 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays for electricity, water and the telephone. A business with a meter read every month is billed every month on its reading. A household pays on account: a twelfth of the year's estimate every month by direct debit, the meter read once a year, and an annual statement and a settlement bill for the actual year. In Urvania the electricity bill carries the grid operator's network charges beside the retail company's energy; in Lumeport the water company also runs the district heating, and its bill carries the city's sewage levy; the island's telephone company bills its business customers every month. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with the meter readings.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, treated at the producer's plant on the shore and sent by pipeline across the border. The producer sells the month's gas to the power station and to the retail company for its gas customers; it declares the export in one declaration, with a consignment for each buyer, and each buyer declares its own import. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.
