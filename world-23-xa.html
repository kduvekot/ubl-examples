How UBL helps

The seller's bank receives payments from many countries. Because UBL invoices carry the payee account and a payment reference, and remittance advices repeat that reference, incoming payments can be matched to the right invoices automatically.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 9 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Country A)

The manufacturer buys ore free carrier (FCA) at the mine: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so the manufacturer, not the mine, books the ore train with the rail carrier.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Country A → Country B)

The distributor buys free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Containers by rail under transit (Country A → Country B)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Valdara Industries to Lumeway Distribution: Under DAP the price includes the transport, so the invoice covers goods and freight.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

Bulk by river barge (Country A → Country B)

Bulk lots go down the river. The distributor buys carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay in Country B: the manufacturer books the barge and pays the freight; the risk passes when the barge is loaded.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Valdara Industries to Lumeway Distribution: Under CPT the seller has paid the barge, so the price includes carriage to the quay.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Ferrano Energy to Valdara Industries: The electricity bill, with Country A's VAT; it refers to the utility statement for the consumption.
