#23 Seller's Bank

Country A (XA) · Finance

Holds the manufacturer's account and receives the payments from abroad.

How UBL helps

The seller's bank receives payments from many countries. Because UBL invoices carry the payee account and a payment reference, and remittance advices repeat that reference, incoming payments can be matched to the right invoices automatically.

Documents it exchanges

  • Invoice (PayeeFinancialAccount)
  • RemittanceAdvice

UBL roles

The party elements in which this party appears:

  • PayeeParty (account holder)
