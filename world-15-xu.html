How UBL helps

Inland waterway transport moves large volumes slowly. UBL transport execution plans and waybills carry the barge, the loading and discharge quays and the cargo per hold, so shippers and terminals can plan around long transit times.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The waste processor recovers plastic from production waste and sells it as granulate to the packaging maker on the coast of Bayvarra, carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the barge is loaded. The packaging maker turns it into packaging for the benches and the clothing.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan to Rinova Recovery: The barge operator confirms the vessel and the sailing.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill to Rinova Recovery: The inland waterway consignment note, issued to the shipper.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice to Rinova Recovery: Under CPT the seller pays the carriage: the barge operator invoices the river freight to the waste processor.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Rinova Recovery: Under CPT the waste processor is the transport user; it asks the barge operator for capacity down the river.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Export Customs Declaration from Rinova Recovery to Customs Administration of Urvania: The granulate leaves Urvania; the waste processor declares the export.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Rinova Recovery to Rivermouth Packaging: The waste processor announces the lot loaded on the barge.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).
