Example documents

Real UBL 2.5 documents from the example world. Every document is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas of the endorsed document types and against the world's code lists, and every value in it comes with the reason why it is that value. The documents are generated from the world's layers, so the parties, items, currencies and taxes in them agree with the rest of the world.

These examples are kept apart from the examples of the OASIS UBL Technical Committee, whose history is in this repository's xml/ folder.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

The manufacturer buys ore free carrier (FCA) at the mine: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so the manufacturer, not the mine, books the ore train with the rail carrier.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.
