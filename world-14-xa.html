#14 Rail Carrier

Country A (XA) · Logistics

Runs the ore trains from the mine to the factory and container trains to the border.

How UBL helps

A rail carrier plans capacity on a fixed timetable. UBL transport documents let its customers request and confirm transport plans and receive status updates per train and wagon, with the same consignment references the forwarder and the receiving terminal use.

Documents it exchanges

  • Waybill
  • TransportExecutionPlan
  • TransportationStatus

UBL roles

The party elements in which this party appears:

  • TransportServiceProviderParty
