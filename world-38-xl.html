How UBL helps

A farm does not write invoices: the ginnery weighs and grades the cotton and bills itself on the farm's behalf. UBL's self-billed invoice and credit note are made for exactly this, and the farm receives a structured statement of what it was paid for.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

  • Delivery Note to Selanu Cotton Ginnery: The farm delivers a trailer of seed cotton at the gin and confirms what it delivered. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

Receives

  • Self Billed Invoice from Selanu Cotton Ginnery: Under the self-billing agreement the ginnery writes the farm's invoice from the weight and grade it measured. No tax on the island, stated as such. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Self Billed Credit Note from Selanu Cotton Ginnery: Classing after ginning puts part of the delivery a grade lower; the ginnery credits the difference on the farm's behalf. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Remittance Advice from Selanu Cotton Ginnery: The ginnery pays the farm the net amount. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.
