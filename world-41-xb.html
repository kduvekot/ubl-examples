How UBL helps

Recycled raw material comes in bulk lots by river; packaging goes out in many small orders. UBL handles both with the same order, despatch and invoice structure, across the border and at home.

In this world it sends 5 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 4 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The waste processor recovers plastic from production waste and sells it as granulate to the packaging maker on the coast of Bayvarra, carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the barge is loaded. The packaging maker turns it into packaging for the benches and the clothing.

Sends

  • Order to Rinova Recovery: A bulk lot of recycled granulate. Delivery terms CPT river quay: the seller books the barge. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The packaging maker declares the import in Bayvarra. See the example document.

    In it as ImporterParty, ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as ConsignorParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Rinova Recovery: The packaging maker pays from Bayvarra to the waste processor's account in Urvania. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order Response from Rinova Recovery: The waste processor confirms the lot and the loading date. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Rinova Recovery: The waste processor announces the lot loaded on the barge. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Fiumella Barges as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice from Rinova Recovery: Granulate and barge freight (CPT). An export from Urvania: no VAT charged, with the exemption stated. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Rinova Recovery to Fiumella Barges: Under CPT the waste processor is the transport user; it asks the barge operator for capacity down the river. See the example document.

    In it as Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Transport Execution Plan from Fiumella Barges to Rinova Recovery: The barge operator confirms the vessel and the sailing. See the example document.

    In it as Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Export Customs Declaration from Rinova Recovery to Customs Administration of Urvania: The granulate leaves Urvania; the waste processor declares the export. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: Fiumella Barges as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Waybill from Fiumella Barges to Rinova Recovery: The inland waterway consignment note, issued to the shipper. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

Packaging for the benches, clothing for the store

The packaging maker supplies the manufacturer in Urvania and the textile mill at home; the textile mill sells clothing to the department store, delivered duty paid (DDP) at the store.

Sends

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: Packaging exported to Urvania: no sales tax in Bayvarra, stated. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Invoice to Lumeport Textile Mill: Packaging for manufacture, exempt from sales tax. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from Valdara Industries: Bench packaging, collected by the road carrier the manufacturer books. Delivery terms FCA packaging maker. See the example document.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty.

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Order from Lumeport Textile Mill: Garment bags for the clothing. Delivery terms DAP mill. See the example document.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.
