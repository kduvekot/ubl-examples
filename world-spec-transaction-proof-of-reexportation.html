Proof of re-exportation

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:proof-of-reexportation:1.0

The guarantor of a carnet asks, and reminds, for the proof that goods were re-exported, and the other guarantor sends it.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: ProofOfReexportationRequest, ProofOfReexportationReminder, ProofOfReexportation.
