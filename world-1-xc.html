#1 Commercial Buyer

Country C (XC) · Commerce

Needs the goods: orders from the distributor overseas and pays the invoice.

How UBL helps

The commercial buyer orders overseas and pays across borders. UBL lets it order from the distributor's catalogue, follow the goods through despatch and transport, match the invoice to the order and the receipt, and send a remittance advice with its payment.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 6 documents, and is named in 13 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Country B → island Country C)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Order to #6 Distributor: Delivery terms CIF port of the island: the order names the buyer's 3PL as delivery party; freight and insurance are the seller's business.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) as Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Remittance Advice to #24 Factor: The buyer pays the factor from its account at the buyer's bank and says which invoices it settles.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as AccountingSupplierParty; #22 Buyer's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Catalogue from #6 Distributor: The export catalogue for the island customer.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #31 Identifier Issuing Agency as CatalogueLine/Item/StandardItemIdentification/IssuerParty.

  • Digital Agreement from #6 Distributor: Before trading, the two parties join the multilateral digital agreement that fixes which UBL processes they use.

    In it as ParticipantParty.

    Also mentions: #32 Digital Agreement Governor as GovernorParty.

  • Digital Capability from #32 Digital Agreement Governor: The agreement's governor tells the buyer which documents the distributor can send and receive.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as BusinessParty.

  • Order Response from #6 Distributor: The distributor accepts and confirms the sailing it plans to use.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Invoice from #6 Distributor: Goods, sea freight and insurance (CIF) in one invoice; the receivable is sold to the factor, who is the payee.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #24 Factor as PayeeParty; #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) as Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Statement from #24 Factor: The factor, who now owns the receivables, sends the statement of account on behalf of the distributor.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as AccountingSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Forwarding Instructions from #6 Distributor to #10 Freight Forwarder: The distributor instructs its forwarder: book the sea freight to the island and insure the cargo (CIF obliges the seller to insure).

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #29 Insurer as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Packing List from #6 Distributor to #10 Freight Forwarder: How the goods are packed in the container, for the forwarder's booking and the export declaration.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Export Customs Declaration from #10 Freight Forwarder to #19 Customs (XB): The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the distributor at the seaport.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ExporterParty.

  • Forwarding Instructions from #10 Freight Forwarder to #11 Ocean Carrier: The forwarder, now acting as shipper, books the container with the ocean carrier.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty.

  • Bill of Lading from #11 Ocean Carrier to #10 Freight Forwarder: The negotiable bill of lading, issued to the forwarder that gave the instructions; it names the insurer of the cargo and the buyer's 3PL as party to notify on arrival.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) as Shipment/Consignment/NotifyParty; #29 Insurer as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Manifest from #11 Ocean Carrier to #19 Customs (XC): Before arrival the carrier lists the cargo on board for the island's customs.

    In it as ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty.

  • Transportation Status from #16 Seaport (XC) to #10 Freight Forwarder: The port of discharge reports the container discharged and available.

    In it as Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #11 Ocean Carrier as Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration from #3 Customs Declarant / Broker to #19 Customs (XC): Under CIF the buyer clears the import; its licensed customs broker files the declaration on its behalf.

    In it as ImporterParty, ConsigneeParty (The Party who makes the import declaration, or on whose behalf the import declaration is made, and who is the owner of the goods or has similar right of disposal over them at the time when the declaration is accepted) (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #10 Freight Forwarder as FreightForwarderParty.

  • Despatch Advice from #6 Distributor to #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL): The distributor announces the shipment to the delivery party named in the order, the buyer's 3PL.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty (The buyer).

    Also mentions: #11 Ocean Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Receipt Advice from #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) to #6 Distributor: The buyer's 3PL confirms what arrived, for the buyer.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty (The buyer).

An urgent part of the order by air (Country B → island Country C)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Sends

  • Order Change to #6 Distributor: Part of the quantity is needed urgently: delivery by air, CPT island airport, at an extra charge.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) as Delivery/DeliveryParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Forwarding Instructions from #6 Distributor to #10 Freight Forwarder: The distributor asks its forwarder to book air freight for the urgent part.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Waybill from #12 Air Carrier to #10 Freight Forwarder: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #13 Road Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty.

  • Transportation Status from #17 Airport (XC) to #10 Freight Forwarder: The island's air cargo terminal reports the cargo arrived and ready for customs clearance.

    In it as Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: #12 Air Carrier as Consignment/CarrierParty.
