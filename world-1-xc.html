#1 Commercial Buyer

Country C (XC) · Commerce

Needs the goods: orders from the distributor overseas and pays the invoice.

How UBL helps

The commercial buyer orders overseas and pays across borders. UBL lets it order from the distributor's catalogue, follow the goods through despatch and transport, match the invoice to the order and the receipt, and send a remittance advice with its payment.
