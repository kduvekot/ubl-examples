Valdara Industries

#5 Manufacturer · Urvania (XU) · Commerce

Makes park benches and street furniture: frames from steel made from the mine's ore, slats from the sawmill in Bayvarra, packed in recycled packaging from Bayvarra. Sells to the distributor abroad and tenders in public procurement.

How UBL helps

The manufacturer sits in the middle of the chain: it buys, produces, sells, exports and tenders. UBL gives it one vocabulary for all of that, from the order it places with the mine to the certificate of origin and the tender it submits to a public buyer, so the same item, party and price data are reused instead of re-entered for every partner.

In this world it sends 21 and receives 22 documents, and is named in 15 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

The manufacturer buys ore free carrier (FCA) at the mine: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so the manufacturer, not the mine, books the ore train with the rail carrier.

Sends

  • Order to Serravona Mining: Monthly ore call-off. Delivery terms FCA mine: the buyer books the train, so the order names the rail carrier that will collect. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

    Also mentions: Ferrano Rail Cargo as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to Ferrano Rail Cargo: The manufacturer, as transport user, asks the rail carrier for wagons and a slot to move the ore from the mine to its siding. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Serravona Mining: The manufacturer tells the mine which invoices the transfer pays. Both companies bank at the same domestic bank. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan from Ferrano Rail Cargo: The rail carrier confirms the train, the wagons and the schedule; this is the agreed transport plan. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Serravona Mining: The mine announces what was loaded into which wagons, so the factory can plan the unloading. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Ferrano Rail Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Waybill from Ferrano Rail Cargo: The rail consignment note goes to the party that contracted the carriage, the manufacturer; the mine is named as consignor because the ore is loaded there. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as ConsignorParty.

  • Invoice from Ferrano Rail Cargo: Under FCA the buyer pays the carriage: the rail carrier invoices the ore freight to the manufacturer, per tonne. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Invoice from Serravona Mining: The mine invoices the ore; transport is not on this invoice because under FCA the buyer pays it. Payment goes to the mine's account at the seller's bank. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Order Response to Lumeway Distribution: The manufacturer accepts the order and confirms the collection date at its warehouse.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration to Customs Administration of Urvania: Goods leave Urvania: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Certificate of Origin to Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: The goods qualify for preferential duty in Bayvarra; the manufacturer applies to its chamber of commerce for a certificate of origin.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ImporterParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: The manufacturer invoices the goods only (no freight under FCA) and asks for payment to its account at the seller's bank.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from Lumeway Distribution: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Castrano Logistics as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty; Trasvia Haulage as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Remittance Advice from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor announces the cross-border payment and which invoices it settles.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Lumeway Distribution to Trasvia Haulage: The distributor, as transport user, books the truck: collection at the seller's 3PL, delivery at its own distribution centre, across the border.

    In it as Consignment/ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Despatch Advice from Castrano Logistics to Lumeway Distribution: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty (The seller).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Certificate of Origin from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce to Lumeway Distribution: The chamber endorses the certificate and makes it available to the importer, who needs it for the import declaration.

    In it as ExporterParty (The Party who makes the export declaration, or on whose behalf the export declaration is made, and who is the owner of the goods or has similar right of disposal over them at the time when the declaration is accepted).

  • Waybill from Trasvia Haulage to Lumeway Distribution: The road consignment note goes to the party that booked the truck, the distributor; it names the manufacturer as consignor.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeway Distribution to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The distributor, as importer, declares the goods in Bayvarra and claims the preferential duty rate with the certificate of origin.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Receipt Advice from Lumeway Distribution to Castrano Logistics: The distributor confirms what arrived to the party that despatched it, the 3PL, naming the manufacturer as seller.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty (The seller).

Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to Ferrano Rail Cargo: Under DAP the manufacturer is the transport user; it books a container train to the distributor's siding.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transit Customs Declaration to Customs Administration of Urvania: The goods travel under customs transit from Urvania to the destination in Bayvarra; the manufacturer, as holder of the procedure, lodges the declaration at the office of departure.

    In it as TransitExporterParty, ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsigneeParty; Ferrano Rail Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: Under DAP the price includes the transport, so the invoice covers goods and freight.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from Lumeway Distribution: Delivery terms DAP distributor's siding: no carrier in the order, because the seller chooses and pays the transport.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan from Ferrano Rail Cargo: The rail carrier confirms the train and the slot at the intermodal hub.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportUserParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill from Ferrano Rail Cargo: The rail consignment note goes to the transport user, now the seller.

    In it as ReceiverParty, ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice from Ferrano Rail Cargo: Under DAP the seller pays the carriage: the rail carrier invoices the container slots to the manufacturer.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Sends

  • Tenderer Qualification to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer declares its qualifications and those of its consortium partner.

    In it as TendererPartyQualification/MainQualifyingParty.

    Also mentions: Saltmere Engineering as TendererPartyQualification/AdditionalQualifyingParty.

  • Tender to Lumeport Public Works Department: The tender, with the consortium partner as subcontractor.

    In it as TendererParty.

    Also mentions: Saltmere Engineering as SubcontractorParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration to Customs Administration of Urvania: The call-off leaves Urvania; the manufacturer declares the export.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Public Works Department as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer invoices the public buyer under the contract; payment to its account at the seller's bank.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Call For Tenders from Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer retrieves the procurement documents.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.

  • Awarded Notification from Lumeport Public Works Department: The winner is told directly that its tender won.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

  • Order from Lumeport Public Works Department: The first call-off under the awarded contract. Delivery terms DAP the authority's site: the manufacturer delivers, by the road carrier it chooses.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Business Information from Bayvarra Information Service to Lumeport Public Works Department: The authority checks the tenderer's registration with the business registry.

    In it as BusinessParty (The Party who owns this Business Registration Information).

  • Guarantee Certificate from Bank of Lumeport to Lumeport Public Works Department: The guarantor notifies the authority of the bid bond deposited for the manufacturer's tender.

    In it as InterestedParty (The Party who deposits the bid bond guarantee).

  • Contract Award Notice from Lumeport Public Works Department to Bayvarra Information Service: The award is published; the review body is named for appeals.

    In it as TenderResult/WinningParty (A party that is identified as the awarded by a tender result).

    Also mentions: Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Castrano Logistics to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer's 3PL despatches the call-off and names the carrier.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty (The seller).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeport Public Works Department to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: Under DAP the buyer clears the import, so the contracting authority declares the goods in Bayvarra.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Receives

  • Goods Item Passport from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel.

    In it as HolderParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Chamber of Commerce as ImportingGuarantorParty; Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty; Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Urvania one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Sends

  • Waste Notification to Customs Administration of Urvania: The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority.

    In it as SenderParty, NotifierParty, WasteProducerParty.

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as RecoveryFacilityParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Invoice from Rinova Recovery: The waste processor invoices collection and recovery.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Movement from Rinova Recovery to Customs Administration of Urvania: The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority.

    In it as NotifierParty, WasteProducerParty (The Party that acts as the notifier in relation to the Waste Movements covered by this document) (The Party producing the waste material).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Sends

  • Remittance Advice to Ferrano Energy: The manufacturer pays both electricity bills in one transfer.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Ferrano Energy: The factory's monthly electricity consumption: meter readings and consumption per connection, in the energy and water supply structure.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SubscriberParty.

  • Invoice from Ferrano Energy: The electricity bill, with Urvania's VAT; it refers to the utility statement for the consumption.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Utility Statement from Ferrano Energy to Castrano Logistics: The 3PL's warehouse is the manufacturer's building: the 3PL is the subscriber at the connection and receives the statement, but the manufacturer pays. The statement therefore names the manufacturer as customer, the buyer that differs from the receiver.

    In it as CustomerParty (The buyer, if different from the receiver of the document).

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

The sawmill in Upland State saws timber to size. The manufacturer in Urvania asks for a quotation for bench slats to its own dimensions and orders them delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill arranges the truck across the border. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Sends

  • Request For Quotation to Marrowdale Sawmill: Slats to the manufacturer's own dimensions and wood species: it asks the sawmill to quote before ordering.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Order to Marrowdale Sawmill: The manufacturer accepts the quotation and orders. Delivery terms DAP factory: the sawmill delivers and the manufacturer clears the import.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration to Customs Administration of Urvania: The manufacturer declares the import into Urvania, where import VAT is due.

    In it as ImporterParty, ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Marrowdale Sawmill as ConsignorParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Marrowdale Sawmill: The manufacturer pays across the border.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Quotation from Marrowdale Sawmill: The sawmill quotes per thousand slats, delivered at the factory.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Marrowdale Sawmill: The sawmill announces the slats per lot, so the factory can trace the wood of every bench.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice from Marrowdale Sawmill: Slats and delivery. An export from Bayvarra: no sales tax, with the exemption stated.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Marrowdale Sawmill to Trasvia Haulage: Under DAP the sawmill is the transport user; it books the road carrier across the border.

    In it as Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Export Customs Declaration from Marrowdale Sawmill to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The slats leave Bayvarra; the sawmill declares the export at the land border.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.
