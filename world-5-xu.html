Valdara Industries

Manufacturer · Urvania (XU) · Commerce

Makes park benches and street furniture: frames of ductile iron made from the mine's ore and about 35 % steel and iron scrap and cast in its own foundry, slats from the sawmill in Bayvarra, packed in recycled packaging from Bayvarra. Sells to the distributor abroad and tenders in public procurement.

How UBL helps

The manufacturer sits in the middle of the chain: it buys, produces, sells, exports and tenders. UBL gives it one vocabulary for all of that, from the order it places with the mine to the certificate of origin and the tender it submits to a public buyer, so the same item, party and price data are reused instead of re-entered for every partner.

In this world it sends 27 and receives 33 documents, and is named in 20 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

Under a supply agreement for 2026, which sets the price basis, one train a month and a moisture clause, Valdara calls off one train of iron ore a month from Serravona Mining, free carrier (FCA) at Fucinalta mine siding: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so Valdara, not the mine, books Ferrano Rail Cargo's ore train under their framework transport agreement, within the standing plan for the year. On Tuesday 10 March, the first day of the loading window, the mine loads 30 wagons, which are weighed at the siding: 1,788.4 t of lump ore with 62 % iron. The train runs about 160 km on the line past Ferrano into the factory's siding and arrives the same afternoon, which earns the carrier the 2 % bonus of the plan, so that the factory can start the blend a day early. The mine deducts 1.5 % of the value for the 7.5 % moisture measured at loading, as the agreement's moisture clause says.

Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads Valdara sells Lumeway 192 benches delivered at place (DAP) Lumeway's rail siding in Marrowdale. The seller arranges and pays the transport: it books Ferrano Rail Cargo under their framework contract for container slots. The carrier's tractor brings the two packed 40-foot containers from Castrano's warehouse to the intermodal hub at Ferrano, a pre-carriage by road of the same contract, and the hub lifts them onto the container train FRC 4417; the consignment note follows the Accord's uniform rules for international carriage by rail, modelled on the CIM. Urvania's rail infrastructure manager, which runs the tracks and the train paths since the state separated them from the train operators, reports the train's progress to the carrier. The goods travel under the Accord's common transit, which Valdara lodges the evening before departure, to Bayvarra customs' inland office at Marrowdale, where Lumeway declares the import on 15 April and claims the Accord preference. Lumeway empties the first container the next day; the second waits until 22 April, because the siding's fork-lift is in repair, and runs two days past the five days' free time. Ferrano Rail Cargo charges the demurrage to Valdara in a debit note to its freight invoice, and Valdara passes it on to Lumeway, which bears a delay in unloading under DAP, in a debit note to its goods invoice: the world's debit notes.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

Valdara bales the polyethylene film its factory throws out, the stretch film of the pallets it receives and the offcuts of its own wrapping, about 275 t a year, and sends it to Rinova Recovery across Ferrano, which recycles it into granulate. The waste is not hazardous and does not leave Urvania, so Urvania's waste law applies, not the Basel procedure: the producer notifies the waste stream for the year to the environment agency, which acknowledges it with a waste-stream number, and the recovery facility reports each load it receives. Trasvia, registered with the agency as a waste carrier, brings about 54 bales a load; Rinova's weighbridge weighs the truck full and empty, about 38.2 t and 15.3 t, and Rinova invoices the 22.9 t of waste.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays for electricity, water and the telephone. A business with a meter read every month is billed every month on its reading. A household pays on account: a twelfth of the year's estimate every month by direct debit, the meter read once a year, and an annual statement and a settlement bill for the actual year. In Urvania the electricity bill carries the grid operator's network charges beside the retail company's energy; in Lumeport the water company also runs the district heating, and its bill carries the city's sewage levy; the island's telephone company bills its business customers every month. Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

Marrowdale Sawmill in Upland State makes Valdara's bench slats to Valdara's drawing: it saws and kiln-dries ash from the managed forest, modifies it at about 212 °C, planes it to 1800 × 95 × 45 mm and sells the slats by the bundle of 50. It modifies the ash in a thermal modification kiln of its own; in the southern summer, when that kiln is full with its own decking and cladding, it has the slats modified under contract at Tostara Wood Modification in Conciaro, near Ferrano, and names that processor to Valdara, whose approval of the supplier covers it. Valdara asks for a quotation, which the sawmill gives under Valdara's purchasing terms, for the modified ash with kiln-dried oak of the same size as a priced alternative, and orders 96 bundles of the ash delivered at its factory (DAP). The February delivery is modified at Conciaro, so the sawmill books the truck from the plant to the factory: 4,800 slats on 24 heat-treated pallets, about 21.0 t, a quarter's supply for 960 benches, which the plant despatches for the sawmill. The slats are sold while they are still under inward processing in Urvania; Valdara releases them into free circulation and pays Urvania's import VAT. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, against the furniture maker's exemption certificate, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Modification: bench slats modified under contract (Bayvarra → Urvania)

From October to March, the southern summer, Marrowdale Sawmill's thermal modification kiln is full with its own decking and cladding, so it buys kiln slots for the park-bench slats from Tostara Wood Modification at Conciaro, east of Ferrano, under a season agreement that gives each slot a job number in advance. For the February delivery to Valdara it orders the modification, planing, end sealing and bundling of 96 bundles and sends its own kiln-dried ash blanks, 4,800 of 1,830 × 101 × 51 mm, across the border in two loads of its timber truck. The blanks remain the sawmill's, and the sawmill has no Urvanian VAT number, so they do not enter free circulation: the plant holds an authorisation for inward processing and enters them under it, and Urvania's import VAT waits until the finished slats are released. Bayvarra records the blanks as a permanent export for processing. The plant reports what it holds for the sawmill and when the second charge is ready, stores the bundles until the sawmill calls them off, and hands them to the carrier the sawmill books, against the sawmill's pick-up release. It invoices the processing in ferra, a service to a customer established abroad and so outside Urvania's VAT, and the sawmill pays within the discount period.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, treated at the producer's plant on the shore and sent by pipeline across the border. The producer sells the month's gas to the power station and to the retail company for its gas customers; it declares the export in one declaration, with a consignment for each buyer, and each buyer declares its own import. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.
