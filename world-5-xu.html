How UBL helps

The manufacturer sits in the middle of the chain: it buys, produces, sells, exports and tenders. UBL gives it one vocabulary for all of that, from the order it places with the mine to the certificate of origin and the tender it submits to a public buyer, so the same item, party and price data are reused instead of re-entered for every partner.

In this world it sends 25 and receives 31 documents, and is named in 19 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Is named in documents between others

  • Utility Statement from Ferrano Grid to Ferrano Energy: The grid operator reads the factory's gas meter and sends the retail company the consumption: the volume at line pressure, converted to standard cubic metres and to energy. See the example document.

    In it as SubscriberParty (The Party that is the subscriber of the utility).
