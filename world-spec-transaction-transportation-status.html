Transportation status

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:transportation-status:1.0

A party asks for the status of a consignment, or a party in the transport chain reports it: a carrier, a port, an airport, a terminal or a forwarder.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: TransportationStatusRequest, TransportationStatus.
