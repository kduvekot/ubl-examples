#8 Seller's Warehouse (3PL)

Country A (XA) · Commerce

Picks, packs and hands the goods to the carrier for the manufacturer; knows nothing about prices.

How UBL helps

A third-party warehouse acts on instructions from its client but never sees the commercial terms. UBL separates the two: the warehouse sends despatch advices and inventory reports with exactly the logistic details (packages, weights, serial numbers) while prices stay in the documents between seller and buyer.

Documents it exchanges

  • DespatchAdvice
  • InventoryReport
