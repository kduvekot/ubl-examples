How UBL helps

The seaport loads the ship. UBL transport reports and status messages tell the port which containers are coming, and tell carriers and authorities what was loaded, on which vessel and when.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Transportation Status to Bayforth Forwarding: The port of loading reports the container loaded on board. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/MainCarriageShipmentStage/TerminalOperatorParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Consignment/CarrierParty.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Receives

  • Common Transportation Report from Tidewell Ocean Lines: Before entering Lumeport the carrier reports its second feeder to the port. The ship came under Bayvarra's flag in January, so it sails with an interim security certificate, and it carries a sanitation control exemption certificate, as the inspection at Lumeport in January found no control measures needed. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, AuthorityParty.
