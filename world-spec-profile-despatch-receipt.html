Despatch and receipt

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:despatch-receipt:1.0

The supplier announces what it despatches or did, the customer reports what it received, and a despatch is cancelled or goods are returned.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: DespatchAdvice, ReceiptAdvice, DeliveryNote, FulfilmentCancellation, InstructionForReturns, WorkReport.
