How UBL helps

A third-party warehouse acts on instructions from its client but never sees the commercial terms. UBL separates the two: the warehouse sends despatch advices and inventory reports with exactly the logistic details (packages, weights, serial numbers) while prices stay in the documents between seller and buyer.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Despatch Advice to Lumeway Distribution: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Receipt Advice from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor confirms what arrived to the party that despatched it, the 3PL, naming the manufacturer as seller. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order from Lumeway Distribution to Valdara Industries: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them. See the example document.

    In it as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty (The Party who despatches the goods).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Sends

  • Despatch Advice to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer's 3PL despatches the call-off and names the carrier.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Ferrano Energy: The 3PL's warehouse is the manufacturer's building: the 3PL is the subscriber at the connection and receives the statement, but the manufacturer pays. The statement therefore names the manufacturer as customer, the buyer that differs from the receiver.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SubscriberParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as CustomerParty.
