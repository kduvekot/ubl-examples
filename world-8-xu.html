How UBL helps

A third-party warehouse acts on instructions from its client but never sees the commercial terms. UBL separates the two: the warehouse sends despatch advices and inventory reports with exactly the logistic details (packages, weights, serial numbers) while prices stay in the documents between seller and buyer.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor in Bayvarra buys 96 park benches and 12 spare side frames from the manufacturer in Urvania, free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's 3PL warehouse, and books the road carrier itself. The manufacturer confirms the order with the new casting of the side frame, at the same price; Urvania's chamber of commerce certifies the goods' preferential origin; the manufacturer clears them for export, the distributor for import. At goods-in a bench seems to be short and a carton of frames over; the next morning the recount finds the bench's carton on the pallet of spare frames, and the distributor withdraws its first receipt advice and sends a corrected one: everything ordered arrived. The carrier invoices the transport to the distributor, the manufacturer invoices the goods, and the distributor pays across the border.

  • Despatch Advice to Lumeway Distribution: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices. See the example document.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches for the city's parks under procurement law, in two lots: the benches and their installation. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders alone, with an engineering firm as its subcontractor for the installation, backed by a bank guarantee; a furniture maker tenders wooden benches, backed by pledged government bonds. After an electronic auction and the award the authority orders the first release under the framework contract; the manufacturer delivers and invoices the benches, the subcontractor installs them, the authority accepts the release, and the installation is invoiced once accepted.

Sends

  • Despatch Advice to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer's 3PL despatches the call-off and names the carrier. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays for electricity, water and the telephone. A business with a meter read every month is billed every month on its reading. A household pays on account: a twelfth of the year's estimate every month by direct debit, the meter read once a year, and an annual statement and a settlement bill for the actual year. In Urvania the electricity bill carries the grid operator's network charges beside the retail company's energy; in Lumeport the water company also runs the district heating, and its bill carries the city's sewage levy; the island's telephone company bills its business customers every month. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with the meter readings.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Ferrano Energy: The warehouse is the manufacturer's building: the manufacturer owns it and holds the subscription, the 3PL operates the warehouse and receives the statement of its consumption point; the statement names the manufacturer as subscriber and customer. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SubscriberParty; Valdara Industries as CustomerParty.
