How UBL helps

Waste movements must be announced and tracked before the waste leaves the site. UBL's waste notification and waste movement documents describe the waste, its producer and the facility that recovers or disposes of it, and the same parties then appear on the invoice for the removal service.

In this world it sends 7 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The waste processor recovers plastic from production waste and sells it as granulate to the packaging maker on the coast of Bayvarra, carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the barge is loaded. The packaging maker turns it into packaging for the benches and the clothing.

Sends

  • Order Response to Rivermouth Packaging: The waste processor confirms the lot and the loading date. See the example document.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to Fiumella Barges: Under CPT the waste processor is the transport user; it asks the barge operator for capacity down the river. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration to Customs Administration of Urvania: The granulate leaves Urvania; the waste processor declares the export. See the example document.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Fiumella Barges as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Despatch Advice to Rivermouth Packaging: The waste processor announces the lot loaded on the barge. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Fiumella Barges as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Rivermouth Packaging: Granulate and barge freight (CPT). An export from Urvania: no VAT charged, with the exemption stated. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from Rivermouth Packaging: A bulk lot of recycled granulate. Delivery terms CPT river quay: the seller books the barge. See the example document.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan from Fiumella Barges: The barge operator confirms the vessel and the sailing. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportUserParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill from Fiumella Barges: The inland waterway consignment note, issued to the shipper. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice from Fiumella Barges: Under CPT the seller pays the carriage: the barge operator invoices the river freight to the waste processor. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Remittance Advice from Rivermouth Packaging: The packaging maker pays from Bayvarra to the waste processor's account in Urvania. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Urvania one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Sends

  • Waste Movement to Customs Administration of Urvania: The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, RecoveryFacilityParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as NotifierParty; Valdara Industries as WasteProducerParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: The waste processor invoices collection and recovery. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Urvania: The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority. See the example document.

    In it as RecoveryFacilityParty (The Party recovering the waste material).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.
