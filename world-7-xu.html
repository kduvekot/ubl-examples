Rinova Recovery

Waste Processor · Urvania (XU) · Commerce

Recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories, Valdara's among them: it collects the baled film, weighs each load on its weighbridge, reports it to the environment agency and invoices the net tonnes; it sells the granulate it makes, certified for its recycled content.

How UBL helps

Waste movements must be announced and tracked before the waste leaves the site. UBL's waste notification and waste movement documents describe the waste, its producer and the facility that recovers or disposes of it, and the same parties then appear on the invoice for the removal service.

In this world it sends 9 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

Rinova Recovery in Ferrano recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories, Valdara's among them (the waste flow), and sells 120 tonnes of granulate in 120 jumbo bags of one tonne to Rivermouth Packaging on Bayvarra's coast, carriage paid to (CPT) Rivermouth's river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the bags are on board. Before the order Rinova sends its product information for the quarter, with the recycled-content certificate of an accredited certification body. The bags are loaded on Monday 20 April and sail down the river by day; the freight of exported goods is exempt from Urvania's value added tax. Rivermouth turns the granulate into stretch film and garment bags (the packaging flow).

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

Valdara bales the polyethylene film its factory throws out, the stretch film of the pallets it receives and the offcuts of its own wrapping, about 275 t a year, and sends it to Rinova Recovery across Ferrano, which recycles it into granulate. The waste is not hazardous and does not leave Urvania, so Urvania's waste law applies, not the Basel procedure: the producer notifies the waste stream for the year to the environment agency, which acknowledges it with a waste-stream number, and the recovery facility reports each load it receives. Trasvia, registered with the agency as a waste carrier, brings about 54 bales a load; Rinova's weighbridge weighs the truck full and empty, about 38.2 t and 15.3 t, and Rinova invoices the 22.9 t of waste.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries to Environment Agency of Urvania: Before the year's movements start, the manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies its waste stream to Urvania's environment agency under the national waste law: the baled polyethylene film it sends to Rinova for recycling, about 275 tonnes in twelve loads from April to March, by Trasvia. The agency acknowledges it with a waste-stream number; no consent is needed for a domestic stream of non-hazardous waste for recovery. UBL requires a CustomsParty in a waste notification, "the competent authority granting a permit to export or import the waste"; a domestic stream crosses no border, so it names the environment agency, the only competent authority there is. See the example document.
