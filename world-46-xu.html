Environment Agency of Urvania

#46 Environment Agency (XU) · Urvania (XU) · Government

Urvania's environment agency, the competent authority of its waste law: it issues the permits of waste treatment plants, registers waste carriers, and receives the producers' notifications of their waste streams, which it acknowledges with a waste-stream number, and the facilities' reports of each load received (decision D42).

How UBL helps

A waste authority must know which waste moves, from whom and to which facility. UBL's waste notification tells it the waste stream before the movements start, and the waste movement reports each load as it is received, so the authority can follow the waste from the producer to its recovery without collecting the figures again.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

Valdara bales the polyethylene film its factory throws out, the stretch film of the pallets it receives and the offcuts of its own wrapping, about 275 t a year, and sends it to Rinova Recovery across Ferrano, which recycles it into granulate. The waste is not hazardous and does not leave Urvania, so Urvania's waste law applies, not the Basel procedure: the producer notifies the waste stream for the year to the environment agency, which acknowledges it with a waste-stream number, and the recovery facility reports each load it receives. Trasvia, registered with the agency as a waste carrier, brings about 54 bales a load; Rinova's weighbridge weighs the truck full and empty, about 38.2 t and 15.3 t, and Rinova invoices the 22.9 t of waste (decision D42).

Receives

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries: Before the year's movements start, the manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies its waste stream to Urvania's environment agency under the national waste law: the baled polyethylene film it sends to Rinova for recycling, about 275 tonnes in twelve loads from April to March, by Trasvia. The agency acknowledges it with a waste-stream number; no consent is needed for a domestic stream of non-hazardous waste for recovery (decision D42). UBL requires a CustomsParty in a waste notification, "the competent authority granting a permit to export or import the waste"; a domestic stream crosses no border, so it names the environment agency, the only competent authority there is.
