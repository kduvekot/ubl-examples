Customs

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:customs:1.0

Goods are declared to customs for export, transit and import under the Accord's customs data model, with the certificates customs asks for.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: ExportCustomsDeclaration, ImportCustomsDeclaration, TransitCustomsDeclaration, CertificateOfOrigin, GoodsCertificate.
