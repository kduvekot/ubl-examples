Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment. The store renovates the home and garden department of its Lumeport store and reopens it on Monday 11 May, with two weeks at 10 % off; to set up the range it asks the distributor for a catalogue, which the distributor deletes when its winter catalogue takes over on 20 June. The store orders 24 benches, delivered in a slot on the morning of 30 April; it refuses one in a torn carton, and the distributor credits it. The distributor has sold its receivables to a factor, which the store pays. A consumer buys in the shop, and another orders a dining set from the store's web shop.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays for electricity, water and the telephone. A business with a meter read every month is billed every month on its reading. A household pays on account: a twelfth of the year's estimate every month by direct debit, the meter read once a year, and an annual statement and a settlement bill for the actual year. In Urvania the electricity bill carries the grid operator's network charges beside the retail company's energy; in Lumeport the water company also runs the district heating, and its bill carries the city's sewage levy; the island's telephone company bills its business customers every month. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with the meter readings.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Coastal State Water Company: The household's annual statement for water and district heat, April 2025 to March 2026: the annual readings, the year's water with the city's levy, the heat with its cooling correction, and the new payments on account from April.
