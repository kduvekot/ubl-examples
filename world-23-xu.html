How UBL helps

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

Under a supply agreement for 2026, which sets the price basis, one train a month and a moisture clause, Valdara calls off one train of iron ore a month from Serravona Mining, free carrier (FCA) at Fucinalta mine siding: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so Valdara, not the mine, books Ferrano Rail Cargo's ore train under their framework transport agreement, within the standing plan for the year. On Tuesday 10 March, the first day of the loading window, the mine loads 30 wagons, which are weighed at the siding: 1,788.4 t of lump ore with 62 % iron. The train runs about 160 km on the line past Ferrano into the factory's siding and arrives the same afternoon, which earns the carrier the 2 % bonus of the plan, so that the factory can start the blend a day early. The mine deducts 1.5 % of the value for the 7.5 % moisture measured at loading, as the agreement's moisture clause says (decision D45).

Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads Valdara sells Lumeway 192 benches delivered at place (DAP) Lumeway's rail siding in Marrowdale. The seller arranges and pays the transport: it books Ferrano Rail Cargo under their framework contract for container slots. The carrier's tractor brings the two packed 40-foot containers from Castrano's warehouse to the intermodal hub at Ferrano, a pre-carriage by road of the same contract, and the hub lifts them onto the container train FRC 4417; the consignment note follows the Accord's uniform rules for international carriage by rail, modelled on the CIM. Urvania's rail infrastructure manager, which runs the tracks and the train paths since the state separated them from the train operators, reports the train's progress to the carrier. The goods travel under the Accord's common transit, which Valdara lodges the evening before departure, to Bayvarra customs' inland office at Marrowdale, where Lumeway declares the import on 15 April and claims the Accord preference. Lumeway empties the first container the next day; the second waits until 22 April, because the siding's fork-lift is in repair, and runs two days past the five days' free time. Ferrano Rail Cargo charges the demurrage to Valdara in a debit note to its freight invoice, and Valdara passes it on to Lumeway, which bears a delay in unloading under DAP, in a debit note to its goods invoice: the world's debit notes (decision D37).

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

Rinova Recovery in Ferrano recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories, Valdara's among them (the waste flow), and sells 120 tonnes of granulate in 120 jumbo bags of one tonne to Rivermouth Packaging on Bayvarra's coast, carriage paid to (CPT) Rivermouth's river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the bags are on board. Before the order Rinova sends its product information for the quarter, with the recycled-content certificate of an accredited certification body (plastics/12). The bags are loaded on Monday 20 April and sail down the river by day; the freight of exported goods is exempt from Urvania's value added tax. Rivermouth turns the granulate into stretch film and garment bags (the packaging flow) (decision D41).

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

Marrowdale Sawmill in Upland State saws Valdara's bench slats to Valdara's drawing, 1800 × 95 × 38 mm, kiln dried, and sells them by the bundle of 50. Valdara asks for a quotation, which the sawmill gives under Valdara's purchasing terms, for oak with ash as a priced alternative, and orders 96 bundles delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill books the truck across the border: 4,800 slats on 24 heat-treated pallets, about 22.2 t, a quarter's supply for 960 benches. This delivery is oak; from May the sawmill saws ash, as its felling in the managed forest moves to an ash stand. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, against the furniture maker's exemption certificate, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why (decision D43).
