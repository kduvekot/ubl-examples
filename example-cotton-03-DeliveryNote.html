Delivery Note: Lisavu Cotton Farm to Selanu Cotton Ginnery

Document 3 of 18 in the flow Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra) · Delivery Note in the UBL 2.5 specification

From Lisavu Cotton Farm to Selanu Cotton Ginnery, under the Trade Accord's transaction Delivery note.

← Forecast · all documents of the flow · Self Billed Invoice →

The farm delivers a trailer of seed cotton at the gin and confirms what it delivered.
