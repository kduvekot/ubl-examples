# The place list (O91)

Prepared 2026-11-18 for the owner's approval. Nothing in the world data changes until the owner approves.

The place list applies the story line "Geography: the map is a diagram" (O82, `analysis/worklist-decisions.md`):

- every place has one position;
- every party belongs to a place, a few kilometres from its centre;
- distances come from a table, not from counting tiles;
- coordinates in the documents come from the place and the party's offset in it.

It keeps D19's scale (one map square is 14 km) and its long distances, and the two answers settled under O91:

- Lumeway is in Marrowdale;
- the airports lie 10 to 15 km from their towns.

Positions are given as WGS 84 latitude and longitude, because that is what the documents state. Distances are great-circle distances (straight lines); route lengths are those distances times a usual detour factor:

- road about 1.2 to 1.3;
- rail about 1.2 to 1.3;
- river about 1.3.

## 1. One conflict in D19, and the proposal

D19 fixes two road distances, Ferrano–Marrowdale 182 km and Marrowdale–Lumeport 84 km, and two positions, Ferrano at 30.6° S 16.4° W and Lumeport at 31.4° S 13.8° W. These do not fit together:

- From Ferrano to Lumeport is 263 km in a straight line, but the road through Marrowdale is only 266 km. That would be a road almost as straight as a ruler.
- A real road is about a quarter longer than the straight line.

**Proposal:** Ferrano moves 0.5° east, to 30.60° S 15.90° W, about 48 km. With that change:

- the straight line Ferrano–Lumeport is 219 km, and the road through Marrowdale (182 + 84 = 266 km) is 1.21 times as long;
- Lumeport, the island and all of D19's long distances stay as decided;
- the mainland still lies within 28.1–31.9° S and 17.7–12.3° W.

Moving Lumeport west instead would lengthen the crossing to the island and the flight, so this proposal leaves Lumeport where it is.

**A second correction follows from D19's own figures.** D19 gives the flight as about 1,100 km, and the positions confirm it: 1,107 km airport to airport. The same positions give 1,103 km from port to port in a straight line. A ship that rounds the island's cape to reach Selanu sails about 1,150 km (about 620 nautical miles), which at 13.5 knots is about 46 hours, not D19's 1,050 km and 42 hours. The sea flow (D30, stage 6) would use the new figures.

## 5. What changes if the list is adopted

**World data:**

- a place list, either in `world/map.json` (positions, districts, offsets) or as a new file;
- every party gets its place and offset;
- `world/countries.json`'s tile-based coordinates (`origin`, `degrees_per_tile`) give way to the place positions;
- the legs in `world/documents.json` get their distances.

**Documents:**

- every coordinate a document states: 25 coordinates (`LocationCoordinate`) in 18 documents, for parties, places, the platform and the pipelines;
- road/14's freight bill moves from 186 km to 182 km;
- retail/16's delivery bill moves from 38 km to 84 km;
- the platform's "four kilometres" becomes about 36 km;
- the sea flow's passage time follows the 1,150 km (with D30, stage 6);
- the addresses of the customs border office, the cotton farm and the mast site.

Most of this is done with C56 (the map scale) and the flows of stage 6.

**The drawn map:** redrawn so that it tells the truth in everything but distance (O92):

- Lumeport on the coast at the river mouth;
- the customs posts facing each other;
- the airports outside their towns;
- Rinova's own quay;
- the island as an inset.

## 6. Questions for the owner (asked one at a time)

1. **Ferrano 0.5° east** (section 1), or another way to make the road through Marrowdale longer than the straight line?
2. **The river east of the border crossing:** in this proposal both banks are Bayvarran, so the river runs through Bayvarra to Lumeport. The map today keeps all land south of the river Urvanian, which would put Urvania on the opposite bank at Lumeport.
3. **The border crossing:** a name for the place, with the Urvanian customs border office there (the street "Valico di Confine" moves to it from Ferrano).
4. **The cotton farm:** a place of its own on the north coast, with an address there instead of Selanu.
5. **The sea crossing:** about 1,150 km and 46 hours instead of D19's 1,050 km and 42 hours, following D19's own flight distance.
