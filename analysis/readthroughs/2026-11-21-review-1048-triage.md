## (1) Decisions for the owner (most important first)

### Q1. reefer/07: a bill of lading for which no original is issued (reefer/07#F1)
**Situation.** Tidewell's carrier document in the reefer flow is a BillOfLading with `ToOrderIndicator` false. Its instruction says: "Express release: no original issued; the reefer is released at Selanu to the consignee named." UBL defines a bill of lading as the document against whose surrender the carrier delivers. A document with no original, released to a named consignee, is a sea waybill. In practice, a "telex" or "express" release means the originals were issued and then surrendered at origin. The O137 build chose the "express release" and nothing has decided it since.

**Options.**
- **(a)** Keep the BillOfLading, issued in originals to the forwarder Bayforth. Bayforth surrenders them to Tidewell at Lumeport, and Tidewell releases the reefer at Selanu by message (a telex release). The instruction text changes. The coverage of the reefer's bill of lading stays.
- **(b)** Make the carrier document a Waybill, a sea waybill to the named consignee. This fits a straight two-day sailing to a known buyer. The document type changes, and with it the elements the bill of lading carries there (coverage to check: `coverage_register.py`).
- **(c)** Keep it as it is and record the gap as a semantic difference.

**Recommendation: (a).** It removes the contradiction with one sentence and loses no coverage.

### Q2. reefer/02: the counter-offer that nobody accepts (reefer/02#F1)
**Situation.** In reefer/02 the cooperative answers with `OrderResponseCode` CA (conditionally accepted). It offers 280 Granny Smith instead of 224 and 168 pears instead of 224, open until Friday 17 April at noon. No document of the flow shows Hafefo accepting. Every later document still uses 280/168, and the remittance speaks of "the mix of your order response". The forwarding instructions book the reefer at 10:40 on 17 April.

**Options.**
- **(a)** A new document: an OrderChange from Hafefo carrying 280/168, under the next free number of the flow (D28). This makes the contract formation explicit.
- **(b)** No new document: the despatch advice (reefer/06), or the forwarding instructions, states "accepted by the buyer by e-mail on Thursday 16 April". The booking then follows the acceptance.
- **(c)** Change the response to "accepted with amendment" and add a world rule that silence by the deadline is acceptance.

**Recommendation: (b).** One sentence makes the story whole. A buyer's e-mail acceptance of a short-lived fruit offer is normal practice, and (a) adds a document only for this.

### Q3. lpg/04: Coastal State's sales tax charged under a Northern Territory permit (lpg/04#F1)
**Situation.** O204 and `note/src/03-world.md` already decide the rate: propane burned by the water company carries the tax of Coastal State, where it is delivered, at 6.5 %. `examples_kit.tax_rule` builds exactly that. The world's sales tax numbers are permits per state, though (`countries.json` XB `tax_id`: "state code …, sales tax permit of the state or territory"). Kelpstone's only permit is `NT-1238631`, and its PartyTaxScheme jurisdiction is the Northern Territory. The invoice names no state for the 6.5 %.

**Options.**
- **(a)** Kelpstone also holds a Coastal State permit (for example a `CO-` number, held like `tax_abroad`). The invoice shows that permit and the Coastal State jurisdiction. This is consistent with per-state permits, as in US sales taxes.
- **(b)** The home-state permit is enough: a seller files every state's tax with its own state, as with the EU's one-stop shop. The invoice keeps NT and only names Coastal State as the jurisdiction of the tax charged. This needs a new rule in the world's tax story.
- **(c)** No change.

**Recommendation: (a).** It follows from the permit format the world already has, and it is the only case (propane is the world's only `business_use: consumed` item). The mechanical part, naming the jurisdiction of the tax charged, is in the table below either way.

### Q4. Tax numbers of the two foreign makers (medicines/02#F2, medicines/07#F2)
**Situation.** The Indian vaccine maker zero-rates its export under India's GST, and the Dutch isotope maker states "Export outside the European Union". Neither shows a tax registration (no PartyTaxScheme). In real practice both invoices carry the supplier's GSTIN or VAT number. O229 says the makers' identifiers are "only what the documents need, in a form that cannot collide with real registers (no real-looking KvK or GSTIN)".

**Options.**
- **(a)** A PartyTaxScheme with the scheme (GST, VAT) and no CompanyID. It states the registration the zero rate rests on and invents no number.
- **(b)** A number in the real format whose check character fails, so it cannot be anyone's. Recorded as such in the source verification.
- **(c)** Leave it out and record why (the world does not model foreign tax numbers).

**Recommendation: (a).** It meets the condition without a number that could pass for a real one, in line with O229. (The missing payment means, medicines/02#F1 and 07#F3, is mechanical; see below.)

### Q5. Gullhaven's telephone area code 38522 beside Lumeport's 38 (lpg/01#F2, 02#F2, 03#F2, 04#F4)
**Situation.** Bayvarra's plan (`countries.json` XB `phone_plan`) is a deliberately British-style closed plan: "Lumeport 38, Upland State 7121, Gullhaven 38522". The reviewer calls the codes not prefix-free. Britain's mixed area codes work the same way (Dumfries 01387 beside Langholm 013873; Lancaster 01524 beside Hornby 015242): the shorter code's subscriber numbers never start with the longer code's extra digits. No Lumeport number in the world starts with `38 522` (grep over world/ and tools/). The bank branch code 385220 is a sort code and has nothing to do with the telephone plan.

**Options.**
- **(a)** Keep it, and state the rule in the plan's note: "Lumeport's numbers never begin with 522, which belongs to Gullhaven, as in Britain's mixed area codes". Add a check in `world_parties.py` that no `+999 2 38 522…` number exists.
- **(b)** Give Gullhaven a code of its own outside 38 (for example 1563), and renumber the three Gullhaven numbers in `world/parties.json`.

**Recommendation: (a).** The plan is intended and real-world-plausible. A note and a check answer the finding without renumbering.
