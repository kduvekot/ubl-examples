# Encyclopedia: gaps in the story

Facts the encyclopedia's articles would need that the world's data and story do not settle yet, found while the articles were written (2026-11-20). Open point OW002 in `analysis/worklist-decisions.md` points here.

**How this list is used.** A gap is filled by a decision of the owner, recorded like the other decisions; the fact then goes into the world's data (the record it is about, and its `wiki` texts), never only into an article. Strike a gap through (`~~…~~`) with the date when it is filled. The encyclopedia names no persons (owner, 2026-11-20), so a gap about who founded a company asks how and why it was founded, not for a person's name.
