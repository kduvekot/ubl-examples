# UBL findings: what looks wrong inside UBL 2.5

**Purpose.** This is the one list of everything the work on the example world found that looks wrong inside UBL itself (owner, 2026-11-20): a missing element, a definition that is wrong or copied from elsewhere, a cardinality that does not fit, a default code list or CVA association that cannot be used as published, and the places where established practice (the EN 16931 and Peppol bindings, eForms) uses an element against its UBL 2.5 definition. The owner brings these to the OASIS UBL TC, and the kind C points also to CEN/TC 434, **at a slow pace and with nothing guaranteed**: an entry here is a point to raise, not a promise that UBL will change.

**How the world deals with them meanwhile.** The world follows UBL's definitions, not its element names and not the practice of other bindings (semantic conformance, UBL 2.5 §4.7 and §7.2; the general rule at the top of `analysis/codelist-decisions.md`). Where a definition leaves no correct place for a fact, the world either leaves the element out (and the fact goes into a description or note), or uses the nearest element and says why in the example's `.md`, and records the gap here. Each entry says which of the two the world does now.

**Kinds.**

- **A:** defects in UBL 2.5 itself: a missing element, a wrong or copied definition, a name that contradicts its definition, a cardinality.
- **B:** UBL's default code lists and the default CVA (`UBL-DefaultDTQ-2.5.cva`).
- **C:** conflicts between established practice (EN 16931 / Peppol BIS, eForms) and UBL 2.5 semantics. The EN 16931 points go to CEN/TC 434 as well, to raise when EN 16931 moves to UBL 2.5.

**Sources and checks.** Every entry names its source: a decision (D-number), an open point (O-number), a row of `analysis/codelist-decisions.md` (Q-, P- or the numbered items of its section "For the OASIS UBL TC"), or a review finding (`flow/NN#Fn`, in the merged reviews next to the examples; the merge decisions in `analysis/review-merge-decisions.md`). The UBL 2.5 definitions quoted here were checked on 2026-11-20 against `.ubl/mod/UBL-Entities-2.5.gc` (the UBL 2.5 OS business entity summary), the CVA points against `.ubl/cva/UBL-DefaultDTQ-2.5.cva`, and the default-list contents against `world/codelists/ubl-default-codes.json` (the lists as read from the UBL 2.5 CVA's value lists). Quotes of EN 16931 and Peppol are given as `analysis/codelist-decisions.md` records them (Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 checked there on 2026-11-11); they were not re-read for this list. "Where the world meets it" names the examples as they are built today (`world/examples/<flow>/NN-DocType.xml`).

This file is internal (the workshop layer): its IDs, decision numbers and finding IDs are not published in world texts.

**Status** is "collected" for every entry: gathered here, not yet gone over with the owner, not yet written up for the TC.

## Considered and left out, or still to look at

Not entries, but noted so that nothing is lost:

- **A week number** (worklist-decisions, "The calendar", 2026-11-16): UBL has no element for one; a week is stated as a period with start and end dates and the code `WEEK`. Nothing looks wrong: a period carries a week fully. Left out.
- **`PublishAwardIndicator`** (D28 point 9): dropped because the world has no "certain service categories"; the definition is narrow but not wrong. Left out.
- **`LocaleCode` bound to the Language list** (noticed while collecting, 2026-11-20; not recorded as a TC point in any source): `Language/LocaleCode` is "A code signifying the locale in which this language is used", but the UBL 2.5 CVA binds `cbc:LocaleCode` (with `NoticeLanguageCode`, `PreferredLanguageLocaleCode`, `TenderLanguageLocaleCode`) to the Language list, which holds languages, not locales, so a locale such as `en-XB` cannot pass. The world binds `LocaleCode` to its own locale list (D1). To go over with the owner before it becomes an entry (kind B).
- **The older `AllowanceChargeReason-2.1` list** (noticed while collecting): the UBL 2.5 CVA still offers it, with listID `UN/ECE 3155` (the communication channel list). Only of interest if the TC tidies the older value lists. To go over with the owner.
- **`analysis/codelist-classification.md`, "UBL lists not bound by UBL's CVA"**: besides ReceiptAdviceTypeCode (U-B07), `DefaultLanguageCode`, `NationalityCode`, `MimeCode` and `TransportHandlingUnitTypeCode` have no default-list context in the UBL CVA although fitting UBL lists exist. Whether that is a defect or a deliberate choice is for the owner to judge.

### U-A23: amounts collected for a third party and the line total

- **Element:** `LegalMonetaryTotal/LineExtensionAmount` with `CollectionInvoiceLine` (and similar collected amounts).
- **What looks wrong:** UBL defines `LineExtensionAmount` as the sum of the line amounts, but gives no rule for lines that are not the seller's supply (an amount collected on behalf of a third party, such as a public levy or a fund's contribution). Implementers total them two ways: in the line total, or only in the amount payable.
- **Where the world meets it:** the utilities bills (the city's sewage levy, utilities/06, 09, 11) and the cotton self-billing (the cotton fund's levy, cotton/04, 05).
- **How the world handles it:** collected amounts stay out of `LineExtensionAmount` and are added only to `PayableAmount` (owner, 2026-11-21, O274); the build checks it.
- **Suggested change:** state in the definition of `LineExtensionAmount` (and of the collection lines) whether amounts collected for a third party are included, or add a separate total for them.
- **Source:** O274. **Status:** collected.
