#28 Review Body

Country B (XB) · Public procurement · proposed addition to the 26+C set

Independent body that handles appeals and mediation in public procurement.

How UBL helps

A review body handles appeals against procurement decisions. The tendering documents name it, with the deadlines for an appeal, so every tenderer knows where and when to go.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond.

Is named in documents between others

  • Contract Notice from #2 Contracting Authority to #21 Government Information Service: The call for competition is published; it names the review body for appeals and mediation.

    In it as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty, TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/MediationParty (The Party who receives the appeal) (The Party who mediates any appeal).

  • Call For Tenders from #2 Contracting Authority to #5 Manufacturer: The manufacturer retrieves the procurement documents.

    In it as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty (The Party who receives the appeal).

  • Contract Award Notice from #2 Contracting Authority to #21 Government Information Service: The award is published; the review body is named for appeals.

    In it as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty (The Party who receives the appeal).

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as TenderResult/WinningParty.
