In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer. Sales to businesses that resell, exports and sales to public bodies are exempt, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

A review body handles appeals against procurement decisions. The tendering documents name it, with the deadlines for an appeal, so every tenderer knows where and when to go.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Is named in documents between others

  • Contract Notice from Lumeport Public Works Department to Bayvarra Information Service: The call for competition is published; it names the review body for appeals and mediation. See the example document.

    In it as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty, TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/MediationParty (The Party who receives the appeal) (The Party who mediates any appeal).

  • Call For Tenders from Lumeport Public Works Department to Valdara Industries: The manufacturer retrieves the procurement documents. See the example document.

    In it as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty (The Party who receives the appeal).

  • Contract Award Notice from Lumeport Public Works Department to Bayvarra Information Service: The award is published; the review body is named for appeals. See the example document.

    In it as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty (The Party who receives the appeal).

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as TenderResult/WinningParty.
