Marrowdale Sawmill

#36 Sawmill · Bayvarra (XB) · Commerce · proposed addition to the 26+C set

Fells timber in the forests of Upland State and saws it into planks, slats and beams; sells slats for the benches across the border and timber to the furniture maker.

How UBL helps

Wood is sold by dimensions and volume, in lots. UBL carries the measurements and the lot of every item in quotations, orders and despatch advices, so a buyer that orders slats to size gets exactly what it ordered and can trace each lot.

In this world it sends 6 and receives 4 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

The sawmill in Upland State saws timber to size. The manufacturer in Urvania asks for a quotation for bench slats to its own dimensions and orders them delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill arranges the truck across the border. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Sends

  • Quotation to Valdara Industries: The sawmill quotes per thousand slats, delivered at the factory.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to Trasvia Haulage: Under DAP the sawmill is the transport user; it books the road carrier across the border.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The slats leave Bayvarra; the sawmill declares the export at the land border.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Despatch Advice to Valdara Industries: The sawmill announces the slats per lot, so the factory can trace the wood of every bench.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: Slats and delivery. An export from Bayvarra: no sales tax, with the exemption stated.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Invoice to Brackenmere Furniture: Timber sold for manufacture: exempt from the state sales tax, and the invoice says so.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Request For Quotation from Valdara Industries: Slats to the manufacturer's own dimensions and wood species: it asks the sawmill to quote before ordering.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Order from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer accepts the quotation and orders. Delivery terms DAP factory: the sawmill delivers and the manufacturer clears the import.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Remittance Advice from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer pays across the border.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Order from Brackenmere Furniture: Timber for furniture, collected with the furniture maker's own van at the sawmill. Delivery terms EXW sawmill.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Import Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Urvania: The manufacturer declares the import into Urvania, where import VAT is due.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).
