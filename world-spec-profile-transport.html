Transport

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:transport:1.0

A shipper books transport, instructs the forwarder and carrier, and the carrier documents and reports the carriage.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.
