Goods item itinerary

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:goods-item-itinerary:1.0

One document type carries the transaction, the Goods Item Itinerary, which UBL 2.5 defines as follows: “A document providing details relating to a transport service, such as transport movement, identification of equipment and goods, subcontracted service providers, etc.”

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.
