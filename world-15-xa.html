How UBL helps

Inland waterway transport moves large volumes slowly. UBL transport execution plans and waybills carry the barge, the loading and discharge quays and the cargo per hold, so shippers and terminals can plan around long transit times.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Bulk by river barge (Country A → Country B)

Bulk lots go down the river. The distributor buys carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay in Country B: the manufacturer books the barge and pays the freight; the risk passes when the barge is loaded.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan to Valdara Industries: The barge operator confirms the vessel and the sailing.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill to Valdara Industries: The inland waterway consignment note, issued to the shipper.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer, as transport user under CPT, asks the inland waterway carrier for barge capacity.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Despatch Advice from Valdara Industries to Lumeway Distribution: The manufacturer announces the lot loaded on the barge, so the distributor can arrange collection at the quay.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).
