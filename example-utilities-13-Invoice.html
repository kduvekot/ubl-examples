Invoice: Selanu Telecom to Velumi Trading

Document 2 of 14 in the flow Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country) · Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification

From Selanu Telecom to Velumi Trading, under the Trade Accord's transaction Billing.

← Invoice · all documents of the flow · Utility Statement →

The telecom bill. The island has no sales tax or VAT, so no tax is due and the bill has no tax section.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.

The document itself

    The collaboration this document belongs to: Selanu Telecom's bill for March to Velumi. Selanu Telecom started it and gave it this identifier, which every document of it repeats.
