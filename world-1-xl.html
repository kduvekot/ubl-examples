Velumi Trading

#1 Commercial Buyer · Lisavu (XL) · Commerce

Needs the goods: orders from the distributor overseas and pays the invoice. Sells garden and park furniture to island customers from its shop in Selanu.

How UBL helps

The commercial buyer orders overseas and pays across borders. UBL lets it order from the distributor's catalogue, follow the goods through despatch and transport, match the invoice to the order and the receipt, and send a remittance advice with its payment.

In this world it sends 7 and receives 14 documents, and is named in 15 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Sends

  • Remittance Advice to Selanu Telecom: The buyer pays the telecom bill from its account at the island's bank. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Selanu Telecom: Calls, data and subscriptions per telephone line of the buyer, in the telecommunications supply structure. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SubscriberParty.

  • Invoice from Selanu Telecom: The telecom bill. The island has no sales tax or VAT, and the invoice says that no tax applies rather than leaving it out. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.
