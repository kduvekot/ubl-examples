How UBL helps

The commercial buyer orders overseas and pays across borders. UBL lets it order from the distributor's catalogue, follow the goods through despatch and transport, match the invoice to the order and the receipt, and send a remittance advice with its payment.

In this world it sends 6 and receives 15 documents, and is named in 15 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys 104 benches, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock, since it buys by the pallet and two pallets add little to the freight of a container already booked. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, are CIF prices: they hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor pays to its freight forwarder together with the forwarder's truck to the port, and the invoice bills the goods alone. CIF stays as common practice and as the flow's theme, although the ICC's guidance note to CIF says that where goods are handed over to a carrier at a container terminal, the appropriate rule is CIP rather than CIF (Incoterms 2020; D30, O105). The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Urgent spare frames by air (Urvania → Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

On 11 May the buyer asks for eight more side frames by air, spares for the Port of Selanu's harbour benches, on top of the twelve on the ship: it changes its order with a line carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The distributor has none left: Valdara casts them on 12 May and packs them on 13 May, and Trasvia trucks them under the Trade Accord's common transit to the distributor's dispatch area in Marrowdale, where the forwarder takes them straight on to Lumeport airport, a cross-dock. They are booked on the afternoon flight KXL209, but the truck from Ferrano is late and the cargo reaches the terminal after the cut-off; the island's airline flies it on the next morning's KXL207 under a direct air waybill. The distributor cancels its despatch advice and replaces it, and the 3PL collects the skid at Selanu airport on 14 May. The distributor pays the air freight and recharges it to the buyer at cost, as a surcharge on its invoice for the frames.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.
