Harrowmead is a small town in Bayvarra, with about 5,000 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies on the plateau near the border, on a confluence in the wetter western part of Bayvarra.

History

It was founded by British settlers in the 1850s.

Economy

It lives from the market of the western wheat land.
