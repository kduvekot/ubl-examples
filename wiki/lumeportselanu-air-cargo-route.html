Lumeport–Selanu air cargo route

Air cargo route between Lumeport and Lisavu

The Lumeport–Selanu air cargo route links the airports of Lumeport and Selanu, about 1,080 km apart. Kelaro Air Cargo, the island's airline, flies it with a freighter.

Operation

The flight takes about 2½ hours from gate to gate in still air; the timetable allows 2 h 45 eastbound and 3 h 00 westbound, for the west-north-westerly winds of winter and for delays.
