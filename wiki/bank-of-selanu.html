Bank of Selanu is the bank of Lisavu, at Reef House on Reef Street in Selanu. Founded in 1965, it holds the accounts of the island's buyers, producers, carriers and brokers and makes and receives their payments; it employs about 760 people.

Operations

It has a main branch and a market branch in Selanu. Its customers include Velumi Trading, Selanu Cotton Ginnery, Lisavu Cotton Farm, Selanu Telecom, Tevaro Customs Brokers, Nalevi Warehousing and Hafefo Fresh Produce. The island's clearing, Coral net, settles tokenised deposits on the bank's ledger. Payments to and from the mainland run over the Trade Accord's payment link; when an island customer pays in lume, the bank changes its coral at the fixed parity. One of the payments that brings money to the island is the Lumeport Textile Mill's payment for the ginnery's cotton. The island's banks run the Coralcard, the debit card in coral.
