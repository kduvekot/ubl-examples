the Litakima

The old meeting ground of Lisavu's rulers, in Selanu

the Litakima
KindMeeting ground
CountryLisavu
NamesLitakima (Lisavan) · the Litakima (English)

The Litakima (Lisavan, gathering) is the old meeting ground of the island's rulers, in the Lisavan village at the heart of Selanu, Lisavu.

The ground

The Litakima is an open ground walled with dressed lava stone, shaded by old trees, with a raised stone platform at its head where the elders sit. It is still used on Elders' Day.

History

By about the 14th century the island stood under one line of rulers, seated at the village on the east bay. At the first new moon of spring the elders of every village gathered on the Litakima to open the planting and fishing season, and the young brought them the year's first gifts. The island keeps the custom as Elders' Day, a public holiday on 21 September since self-government in 1962.
