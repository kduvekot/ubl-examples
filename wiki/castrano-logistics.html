Castrano Logistics

Urvanian warehouse and logistics company of Valdara Industries

Castrano Logistics
TypeSocietà a responsabilità urvaniana (private limited company)
Founded4 March 1998
SeatConciaro, Urvania
Employees140
Websitehttps://www.castrano.example
ParentValdara Industries (wholly owned)

Castrano Logistics runs the finished-goods warehouse of Valdara Industries at Conciaro, near Ferrano, Urvania. Founded in 1998 and wholly owned by Valdara, it picks, packs and loads the bench maker's goods for the carriers and employs about 140 people.[1][2]

History

Valdara set up Castrano in 1998, when its exports grew after the Lumeport Trade Accord came into force, and handed it its logistics. Castrano registered with the Codana Identification Agency in March 2000, among the agency's early members.

Operations

The warehouse stands on Via del Magazzino at Conciaro, a village east of Ferrano on the main road to the border. Valdara owns the building; Castrano operates it every day from 06:00 to 22:00. It handles the goods but not their prices: it makes up the pallets, marks each with a licence plate of its own numbering, which ends in a check character, and hands them to the carrier the buyer or the seller has booked, among them Trasvia Haulage for the road across the border and Ferrano Rail Cargo for containers to the rail hub. It keeps one box truck of its own. Its warehouse at Conciaro, built in 1998, has about 18,000 m² of floor, twelve dock bays and about 9,000 pallet places; it works for Valdara alone. The company takes its name from Monte Castrano, the hill above Conciaro.
