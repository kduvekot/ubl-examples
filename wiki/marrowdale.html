Marrowdale

Inland town in Upland State, Bayvarra

Marrowdale

Marrowdale is an inland town in Upland State, Bayvarra, with about 40,000 people, near the border with Urvania. It grew from a drovers' market and is now a town of the railway and the timber trade.[1][2]

Geography

The town lies on the Upland near the border with Urvania, in the dale of the Marrow, at the stream crossing of the road down from the plateau; the Upland State Forest covers the slope north of it.

History

The town began in the 1850s as the drovers' market and wool sales at the stream crossing on the road down from the plateau; on 12 April 1858 the plateau became a colony of its own, Upland, and since 1899 it has been Upland State of Bayvarra. The railway came in the 1880s, and with it the station yard and the timber yards. After the Trade Accord entered into force, a distribution centre and the trades of the Accord settled by the railway east of the town.

Economy

Marrowdale lives from the railway and the timber of the Upland State Forest, which lies on the slope north of the town: the sawmill, a furniture maker, an engineering works and a distribution centre with its own siding.
