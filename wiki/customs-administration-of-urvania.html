Customs Administration of Urvania

Urvania's customs service, at the land border crossing

Customs Administration of Urvania
SeatValico Euridice, Urvania
What it doesCustoms (XU)

The Customs Administration of Urvania is the customs service of Urvania, a public body with its border office at Valico Euridice, the road crossing into Bayvarra. It clears the country's exports and imports, the gas that arrives by pipeline among them, and supervises goods in transit.

History

The administration grew from the Italian customs post at the Euridice crossing, set up after the boundary convention of 1857 fixed the border. It has been Urvania's own service since independence in 1962.

The border office

The office stands on Strada del Confine at Valico Euridice (Urvanian Menbaz), facing Bayvarra's post at Euridice Crossing. Both take their name from Forte Euridice, 8 km down the road. The office works around the clock, with export, import and transit offices.

Operations

Goods of Accord origin cross free of duty on proof of origin; on imports Urvania levies its value added tax of 20 % on the value with freight and insurance to the border, which a business deducts again. The office clears the exports of Valdara Industries and Rinova Recovery, and each month the gas that Montebrio Power and Ferrano Energy buy from Kelpstone Gas. It enters goods worked for foreign owners under inward processing, as at Tostara Wood Modification, and releases them into free circulation when they are sold in Urvania. Goods for Bayvarra can travel under the Accord's common transit from the office of departure at the rail hub in Ferrano.
