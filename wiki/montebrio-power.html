Montebrio Power

Gas-fired power station near Serravona, Urvania

Montebrio Power runs a gas-fired power station of about 40 MW near Serravona, Urvania. It was founded in 1974 and employs about 34 people. It burns gas from Bayvarra's offshore field and sells the electricity through the grid.

History

The state built the station in 1974, after the dry winter of 1973 had left the reservoirs of the hydro plants low, to cover the peaks and the dry years, below Monte Brio, the hill near Serravona that gave it its name. It burned heavy fuel oil, which came up the river by barge to Ferrano and on by rail. In 1998, as Urvania opened its economy, the state sold the company to Urvanian pension funds, which own it still. Since 2011 it has burned gas from the offshore field of Kelpstone Gas, brought by pipeline across the border, under the energy protocol of the Lumeport Trade Accord, which opened the wholesale trade in gas and electricity in 2007. In 1999 it was among the first members of the Codana Identification Agency.

Operations

The gas reaches the station by a pipeline of about 207 km from Kelpstone's plant at Gullhaven, metered at a station on Bayvarra's side of the border. Montebrio declares its own import of the gas and pays for it in ferra. It is one of Urvania's several power stations; it feeds its electricity into the network of Ferrano Grid and sells it to Ferrano Energy, the retail company that bills the customers. Urvania's energy market is unbundled, so production, network and retail supply are run by separate companies.
