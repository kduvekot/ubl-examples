Hafefo Fresh Produce

Fresh fruit and vegetable importer in Selanu, Lisavu

Hafefo Fresh Produce is a wholesaler of fresh fruit and vegetables on Wharf Road in Selanu, Lisavu, founded in 1997. It imports produce the island does not grow, or not in the season, and employs about 32 people.

Operations

Hafefo keeps its own cold store by the port and supplies the island's shops, hotels and the kitchen of Selanu General Hospital. In the southern autumn it buys reefers of apples and pears from Harrowmead Fruit Growers in Bayvarra's Upland State, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu; they come on the weekly service of Tidewell Ocean Lines. It lodges its import declarations itself, while the ship is at sea, and Nalevi Warehousing takes the reefer from the port to the cold store. Imported food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import. Hafefo pays Harrowmead in lume from its account at Bank of Selanu. Its purchasing desk works from early morning, Monday to Saturday.
