Hafefo Fresh Produce

Fresh fruit and vegetable importer in Selanu, Lisavu

Hafefo Fresh Produce is a wholesaler of fresh fruit and vegetables on Wharf Road in Selanu, Lisavu, founded in 1997. It imports produce the island does not grow, or not in the season, and employs about 32 people.[1][2]

History

The island's grocers set up Hafefo together in 1997 as their own import company, so that they no longer depended on the mainland wholesalers once trade within the Accord was free; they own it still. Its name is Lisavan and means fresh.

Operations

Hafefo keeps its own cold store by the port and supplies the island's shops, hotels and the kitchen of Selanu General Hospital. In the southern autumn it buys reefers of apples and pears from Harrowmead Fruit Growers in Bayvarra's Upland State, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu; they come on the weekly service of Tidewell Ocean Lines. It lodges its import declarations itself, while the ship is at sea, and Nalevi Warehousing takes the reefer from the port to the cold store. Imported food pays the island's import consumption tax like most imports. Hafefo pays Harrowmead in lume from its account at Bank of Selanu. Its purchasing desk works from early morning, Monday to Saturday. Besides the fruit of Harrowmead it buys from growers and importers in Lumeport, and from the island's own gardens in season. Its cold store has about 1,500 m² of floor.
