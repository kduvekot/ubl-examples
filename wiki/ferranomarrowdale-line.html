Ferrano–Marrowdale line

Railway from Ferrano across the border to Marrowdale

Ferrano–Marrowdale line
KindRailway
CountriesUrvania, Bayvarra

The Ferrano–Marrowdale line is the railway from Ferrano across the border to Marrowdale, the main rail link between Urvania and Bayvarra.

Operation

Container trains run from the intermodal hub at Ferrano past the border to the distribution centre's siding at Marrowdale. On the Urvanian side the Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania owns the tracks up to the border station.
