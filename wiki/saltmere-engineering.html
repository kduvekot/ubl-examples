Saltmere Engineering

Bayvarran engineering firm for telecom mast sites

Saltmere Engineering

Founded1994-10-03
SeatMarrowdale, Bayvarra

Founded byengineers from Bayvarra's telephone service

Saltmere Engineering is an engineering firm in Marrowdale, Bayvarra, founded in 1994. It designs telecom mast sites, obtains their permits and installs back-up power and safety equipment, and employs about 36 people.

History

The firm was founded in 1994 in Saltmere, the east-coast town it is named after, by engineers who left Bayvarra's telephone service to build the masts of the first mobile networks. In 2001 it moved to Marrowdale, nearer the masts inland and on the road and rail to Urvania.

Operations

Saltmere designs mast sites with a registered architect on its staff and installs back-up batteries, smoke detectors and other safety equipment at masts across Bayvarra, dangerous goods included; a duty engineer answers its dangerous goods emergency line around the clock. It also sells such equipment abroad: Selanu Telecom buys battery modules, smoke detectors and coating stripper from it for a mast on the island. Its crews and anchoring gear for ground foundations also suit other work: it installs and anchors park benches in Lumeport as the subcontractor of Valdara Industries for the Lumeport Public Works Department.

Design contest

For the bench of Lumeport's harbour promenade Saltmere teamed with Brackenmere Furniture, which took the design lead while Saltmere did the structure and the anchoring. The jury gave the team the first prize, ahead of Valdara.
