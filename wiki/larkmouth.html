Larkmouth

Port town on the east coast of Bayvarra, its first capital

Larkmouth

Larkmouth is a port town in Coastal State, Bayvarra, with about 80,000 people, at the mouth of the Lark, the largest river of the east coast. It was the British settlers' first town and the colony's first capital.[1][2]

Geography

The town lies at the mouth of the Lark, behind Lark Head, which shelters its harbour from the open sea.

History

The landing

The first settlers landed here on 16 February 1824, behind Lark Head at the mouth of the Lark, where they found a sheltered harbour, fresh water and dry grazing land behind it. Their Government House, church and court stood on the rise above the landing until the capital moved to Lumeport in 1852.

Wool and meat port

From the 1880s wool stores and sale rooms lined the wool line's last stretch to the harbour, and after 1914 a freezing works by the river mouth served the frozen meat trade.

Economy

Larkmouth is the wool and meat port of the dry east, at the end of the railway down from Marrowdale.
