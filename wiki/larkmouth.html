Larkmouth

Port town on the east coast of Bayvarra, its first capital

Larkmouth
CountryBayvarra
Population80,000
Built-up areaabout 6 km across
Districts5

Larkmouth is a port town in Coastal State, Bayvarra, with about 80,000 people, at the mouth of the Lark, the largest river of the east coast. It was the British settlers' first town and the colony's first capital.

History

The first settlers landed here on 16 February 1824, behind the headland at the mouth of the east river, where they found a sheltered harbour, fresh water and dry grazing land behind it. Their Government House, church and court stood on the rise above the landing until the capital moved to Lumeport in 1852. From the 1880s wool stores and sale rooms lined the wool line's last stretch to the harbour, and after 1914 a freezing works by the river mouth served the frozen meat trade.

Economy

Larkmouth is the wool and meat port of the dry east, at the end of the railway down from Marrowdale.
