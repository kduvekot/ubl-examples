Lumeport Trade Accord Council

International body seated in Lumeport that governs the Trade Accord's electronic business documents

Lumeport Trade Accord Council
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
Reports tothe governments of the three members

The Lumeport Trade Accord Council is an international body of the three members of the Lumeport Trade Accord, seated in the Old Town of Lumeport, Bayvarra. It governs the Accord's multilateral agreement on the exchange of electronic business documents, which trading parties join before they trade with each other.[1]

History

The Council was set up when the Accord entered into force in 1996. Each member sends its delegates, the Council decides by consensus, and its chair passes from member to member each year. Since the Accord's protocol of 2014 on electronic business documents it also governs the agreement on their exchange.

Status

The Council is entered in Bayvarra's federal list of public bodies under its seat agreement with Bayvarra, so that it can deal with Bayvarran bodies, banks and the exchange network. Like other public bodies it carries no commercial register entry. Its secretariat works from Exchange Street in the Old Town. As an international body under its seat agreement it answers to the governments of the three members, not to a ministry of any one of them. Besides the agreement on electronic documents it keeps the Accord's shared code lists and its uniform rules for carriage, reviews the parities of the three currencies every five years, next on 1 January 2031 (no review has yet changed a rate), and hears disputes between the members in a panel. Its secretariat has a staff of about 45.
