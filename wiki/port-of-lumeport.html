Port of Lumeport

Seaport of Lumeport, Bayvarra

Port of Lumeport
TypeLimited company (private)
Founded1991-10-01
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra

The Port of Lumeport runs the seaport of Lumeport, Bayvarra: its berths, its container yard and its vessel traffic. It was formed as a company in 1991 and employs about 640 people.

Operations

The port's deep-water quays lie at the inner basin behind the narrows of the estuary, linked to the railway. Its ships sail to Lisavu and beyond, among them the weekly service of Tidewell Ocean Lines to Selanu.
