Port of Lumeport

Seaport of Lumeport, Bayvarra

The Port of Lumeport runs the seaport of Lumeport, Bayvarra: its berths, its container yard and its vessel traffic. It was formed as a company in 1991 and employs about 640 people.[1][2]

History

A federal harbour board ran the port until 1991, when it was made a company owned jointly by the federation and Coastal State.

Operations

The port's deep-water quays lie at the inner basin behind the narrows of the estuary, linked to the railway. Its ships sail to Lisavu and beyond, among them the weekly service of Tidewell Ocean Lines to Selanu. The port has eight deep-water berths, 12 m deep, in the inner basin, three of them for containers, with three ship-to-shore cranes, and a container yard for about 5,000 TEU. It handles about 180,000 TEU and 3.5 million tonnes a year, and its own pilots and tugs bring ships through the narrows.
