Canneto (river)

River of Urvania

Canneto (river)
CountryUrvania
Length71 km
Basin1,304 km²
Sourceabout 395 m
MouthRunmek
NamesCanneto (Italian) · Mersul (Urvanian)

The Canneto rises at about 395 m in Urvania and joins the Runmek after 71 km; its basin covers 1,304 km². Its Urvanian name, Mersul, means reed.
