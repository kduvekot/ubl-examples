Bayvarra Information Service

Bayvarra's federal publication service and business registry

Bayvarra Information Service
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
Formerlythe federal register office (until 1998)
Reports tothe Federal Department of Commerce

The Bayvarra Information Service is a federal public body in Lumeport, Bayvarra. It publishes the notices of public procurement and keeps the country's business registry and its list of public bodies.[1]

  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. Seat
  4. See also
  5. References

History

The service began in 1901 as the register office of the new federation. It took its present name in 1998, when it began to publish the notices of public procurement online. It reports to the Federal Department of Commerce.

Operations

Contracting authorities publish their procurement notices through the service: prior information, calls for competition, design contests, corrections and award results. Lumeport Public Works Department publishes its tenders for park benches and its design contest for the harbour promenade bench this way. As the business registry, the service keeps the entries of Bayvarra's companies and issues extracts of them, which authorities use to check tenderers such as Brackenmere Furniture and Saltmere Engineering; it reissues an extract when a company files new accounts. It also keeps the federal list of public bodies, which gives each public body in Bayvarra a ten-digit number; the Lumeport Trade Accord Council is in the list under its seat agreement.

Seat

Its offices are at Civic Square 27, in the government quarter on Government Hill: one building in one style, its wings round a court that opens on the square, which it shares with the Lumeport Public Works Department and the Federal Department of Agriculture of Bayvarra. The service has a staff of about 120.
