Bayvarra Information Service

Bayvarra's federal publication service and business registry

Bayvarra Information Service
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
What it doesGovernment Information Service

The Bayvarra Information Service is a federal public body in Lumeport, Bayvarra. It publishes the notices of public procurement and keeps the country's business registry and its list of public bodies.

Contents
  1. Operations
  2. Seat
  3. See also

Operations

Contracting authorities publish their procurement notices through the service: prior information, calls for competition, design contests, corrections and award results. Lumeport Public Works Department publishes its tenders for park benches and its design contest for the harbour promenade bench this way. As the business registry, the service keeps the entries of Bayvarra's companies and issues extracts of them, which authorities use to check tenderers such as Brackenmere Furniture and Saltmere Engineering; it reissues an extract when a company files new accounts. It also keeps the federal list of public bodies, which gives each public body in Bayvarra a ten-digit number; the Lumeport Trade Accord Council is in the list under its seat agreement.

Seat

Its offices are at Civic Square 27, in the government quarter on Government Hill: one building in one style, its wings round a court that opens on the square, which it shares with the Lumeport Public Works Department and the Federal Department of Agriculture of Bayvarra.
