Ferrano Grid

Electricity and gas network operator of the Ferrano region, Urvania

Ferrano Grid runs the electricity and gas networks of the Province of Ferrano, Urvania (the other provinces have network companies of their own), from its offices on the Via della Rete in Ferrano. It was founded in 2007 and employs about 620 people.

History

Ferrano Grid was split off from Ferrano Energy as a company of its own in 2007, when the energy protocol of the Lumeport Trade Accord asked for networks to be run apart from supply and Urvania chose the full split. The networks are a natural monopoly, and the state holds the company directly, apart from the partly listed retail company. It has run gas mains as well since the pipeline from Bayvarra opened in 2011, and its gas network has grown since then.

Timeline

  • 2007: split off from Ferrano Energy under the energy protocol of the Lumeport Trade Accord
  • 15 January 2007: registers with the Codana Identification Agency
  • 2011: takes on gas mains as the pipeline from Bayvarra opens

Operations

The company connects Montebrio Power and the gas pipeline from Bayvarra, whose branch reaches its city gate after about 97 km. It owns the meters, reads them and corrects the gas readings. It charges Ferrano Energy for the use of its networks per kilowatt hour taken at each connection, and Ferrano Energy collects the network charges with its bills to customers, as a part of its tariff. The Province of Ferrano takes in the city and the valley up to Serravona and Fucinalta, about 4,800 km² with about 700,000 people. The company's power lines run to about 8,500 km, with about 300,000 connections, and its gas mains to about 1,500 km, with about 90,000 connections. Its own crews run and maintain the networks and read the meters; line contractors build most of the new lines.
