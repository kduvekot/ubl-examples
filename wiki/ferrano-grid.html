Ferrano Grid

Electricity and gas network operator of the Ferrano region, Urvania

Ferrano Grid runs the electricity and gas networks of the Ferrano region of Urvania, from its offices on the Via della Rete in Ferrano. It was founded in 2007 and employs about 91 people.

History

Ferrano Grid was split off as a company of its own in 2007, when the energy protocol of the Lumeport Trade Accord asked for networks to be run apart from supply and Urvania chose the full split. It was an electricity company until 2011, when Kelpstone Gas began producing and the pipeline brought gas to Urvania; it then took up gas as a second business.

Operations

The company connects Montebrio Power and the gas pipeline from Bayvarra, whose branch reaches its city gate after about 97 km. It owns the meters, reads them and corrects the gas readings. It charges Ferrano Energy for the use of its networks per kilowatt hour taken at each connection, and Ferrano Energy collects the network charges with its bills to customers: of a tariff of 27.3 ferra a kilowatt hour, 6.2 are for the network.
