Porto Riparo

Country town in Urvania

Porto Riparo
CountryUrvania
Population20,000

NamesPorto Riparo (English) · Metir (Urvanian)

Porto Riparo is a country town in Urvania, with about 20,000 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies at the head of a long inlet on the north-west coast, the most sheltered anchorage of Urvania's coast, where a river comes in.

History

It was founded by Urvanians before 1600.

Economy

It lives from the north-west's harbour: fishing, fish processing, the coastal shipping, the market of the inlet's farms.
