Environment Agency of Urvania

Urvania's environment agency, the authority for waste

Environment Agency of Urvania
SeatFerrano, Urvania
What it doesEnvironment Agency (XU)

The Environment Agency of Urvania is the competent authority of Urvania's waste law, on the Via delle Acque in Ferrano. It issues the permits of waste treatment plants, registers waste carriers and keeps track of the country's waste streams.

Contents
  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. See also

History

Urvania set up the agency in 2001, after a fish kill in the Runmek below Ferrano's works the year before. Among its first rules was the ban of 2003 on sending the factories' plastic waste to landfill.

Operations

A producer notifies each waste stream for a year to the agency before the first load, with the waste, the planned total and number of loads, the period and the facility, and the agency acknowledges it with a waste-stream number. A domestic stream of non-hazardous waste for recovery needs no consent. The facility then reports to the agency each load it receives, with its net weight. The agency has issued the operating permit of Rinova Recovery and registered Trasvia Haulage as a waste carrier, and it receives the notification of Valdara Industries for its baled film waste. Besides waste it watches the quality of the rivers' water, the Runmek first among them, issues the permits for emissions to the air and looks after the protected areas. The agency reports to Urvania's Ministry of the Environment.
