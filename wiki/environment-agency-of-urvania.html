Environment Agency of Urvania

Urvania's environment agency, the authority for waste

Environment Agency of Urvania
SeatFerrano, Urvania
What it doesEnvironment Agency (XU)

The Environment Agency of Urvania is the competent authority of Urvania's waste law, on the Via delle Acque in Ferrano. It issues the permits of waste treatment plants, registers waste carriers and keeps track of the country's waste streams.

Operations

A producer notifies each waste stream for a year to the agency before the first load, with the waste, the planned total and number of loads, the period and the facility, and the agency acknowledges it with a waste-stream number. A domestic stream of non-hazardous waste for recovery needs no consent. The facility then reports to the agency each load it receives, with its net weight. The agency has issued the operating permit of Rinova Recovery and registered Trasvia Haulage as a waste carrier, and it receives the notification of Valdara Industries for its baled film waste.
