Nevola dam

Dam and hydroelectric power station in the Mukrir massif, Urvania

Nevola dam
KindDam and power station
CountryUrvania
Capacityabout 300 MW
In service1961
Ownerthe state generating company of Urvania
NamesNevola dam (English) · Diga della Nevola (Italian)

The Nevola dam (Italian Diga della Nevola) holds the Nevola in the Mukrir massif of Urvania in the Lago della Nevola, whose water drives the country's largest power station, about 200 m lower down the valley.[1][2]

History

Ferrano Energy built the dam and its power station from 1956 to 1961, its first great work, to bring the valley the electricity its works and towns needed. When Urvania split its energy market in 2007, the scheme passed to the state generating company. The dry winter of 1973, which left the lake low, led the state to build Montebrio Power as a reserve.

Timeline

  • 1956: Ferrano Energy begins the dam and the power station
  • 1961: the power station goes into service
  • 1974: Montebrio Power built as a reserve after the dry winter of 1973
  • 2007: the scheme passes to the state generating company

Operation

The dam stands where the valley narrows below a wide upland hollow, and the lake lies at about 1,190 m. A tunnel brings its water to the power house below. In a normal year the scheme gives about half of Urvania's electricity; on sunny days the lake holds back its water, which the power house turns into electricity in the evening.
