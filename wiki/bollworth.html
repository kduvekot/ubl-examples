Bollworth

Village in Lisavu

Bollworth
CountryLisavu
Population1,000
FoundedEnglish settlers, 1860s
NamesBollworth (English) · Pakesa (Lisavan)

Bollworth (Lisavan Pakesa) is a village in Lisavu, with about 1,000 people.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also
  5. References

Geography

It lies on the coast of the cotton plain north of Selanu, where the estates landed their bales for the ships.

History

It was founded by English settlers in the 1860s.

Economy

It lives from cotton and fishing.
