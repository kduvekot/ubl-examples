Ferrano Intermodal Hub

Intermodal rail terminal at Ferrano, Urvania

Ferrano Intermodal Hub is the intermodal terminal of Ferrano, Urvania, on the container line that runs on across the border to Marrowdale. It was founded in 1997 and employs about 60 people.[1][2]

History

The city of Ferrano built the hub in 1997 on the state railway's old goods yard, so that the container traffic of the Lumeport Trade Accord, in force since 1996, would stop at the town rather than pass it by. The city still holds the majority; Ferrano Rail Cargo, whose container trains start here, took a minority share in 2004.

Operations

The terminal stands in Ferrano's industrial zone along the railway east of the centre. It takes containers in by road, stacks them and lifts them onto the container trains of Ferrano Rail Cargo, to which it reports what it has loaded. The tracks and the train paths belong to the Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania, since Urvania separated its rail infrastructure from its train operators. Among the goods that pass through it are the park benches of Valdara Industries for Lumeway Distribution, brought in containers from the warehouse of Castrano Logistics. The terminal has four sidings of 600 m, two reach stackers and room to stack about 1,200 containers, and handles about 40,000 a year. It works around the clock.
