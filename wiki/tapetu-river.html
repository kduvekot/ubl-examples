The Tapetu is a river of Lisavu, 51 km long, with a basin of 600 km². It rises at about 1,770 m and reaches the sea on the south-west coast at Nihekemo. Its Lisavan name means mountain.[1][2]

Hydro plant

High in its valley the river is taken into a tunnel that brings it down to a power house of about 8 MW above Nihekemo. Built in 1968, the plant is the island's oldest source of electricity from water; a small reservoir lets it run in the evening and at night, when the island's solar park is idle.
