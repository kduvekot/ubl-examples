Maritime Administration of Lisavu

Flag state administration of Lisavu

Maritime Administration of Lisavu
SeatSelanu, Lisavu
Reports tothe Department of Marine Affairs

The Maritime Administration of Lisavu is the flag state administration of Lisavu, on Wharf Road in Selanu. It keeps the island's register of ships and issues ship security certificates.[1]

Contents
  1. History
  2. Organisation
  3. See also
  4. References

History

The administration was set up at self-government in 1962, when the island took over its foreign affairs and its register of ships. Lisavu's flag is flown by the island's fishing boats and the port's tugs; the feeders that serve the island fly Bayvarra's.

Organisation

It is a public body of the island, set up by law and entered in the government list of public bodies of Lisavu, which the Government Secretariat keeps. Its ship registry is open on weekdays. It reports to the island's Department of Marine Affairs. Besides the register it inspects the foreign ships that call at Selanu, keeps the island's lights and buoys, among them those of Cape Sihato and Signal Hill, and issues the certificates of seafarers and fishers. The administration has a staff of about 20.
