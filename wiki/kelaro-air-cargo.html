Kelaro Air Cargo

Lisavu's airline, flying passengers and cargo between Lumeport and Selanu

Kelaro Air Cargo is the airline of Lisavu, founded in 1987, with its office on Airfield Road in Lintwell. It flies passengers and cargo between Lumeport and Selanu, with an ATR 72-600 and an ATR 72-600F freighter, and employs about 38 people.

History

The island's state founded Kelaro in 1987 as its own airline, to fly the route to Lumeport that a Bayvarran airline had served since the island's airfield at Lintwell opened in 1982. Its ATR 72-600F freighter came new in 2023, beside the ATR 72-600 that carries its passengers. The two aircraft are of the same series: one maintenance department looks after both, its pilots are rated on both, and they share the same spare parts.

Operations

The freighter flies the route twice a day: KXL207 leaves Lumeport at 07:00 and KXL209 at 15:00. The flight takes about 2½ hours over about 1,080 km; the timetable allows 2 h 45 eastbound and 3 h 00 westbound. As a freighter it can carry dangerous goods that are for cargo aircraft only, such as lithium batteries, and it carries medicines for Selanu General Hospital in an active temperature-controlled container. Its cargo sales and operations work every day. It flies no other scheduled route, since no other airport lies within reach of the island; with a stretcher kit fitted, its passenger aircraft also flies patients to Lumeport when the hospital needs it.

Fleet

The ATR 72-600F, registered QL-KXA, has seven LD3 positions on its main deck, an aft bulk compartment, a large cargo door of 2.94 × 1.80 m and about 9,200 kg of payload in 75 m³.
