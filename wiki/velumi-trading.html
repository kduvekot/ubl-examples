Velumi Trading

Importer and shop for garden and park furniture in Selanu

Velumi Trading is an importer of garden and park furniture with a shop on Reef Street in Selanu, Lisavu, founded in 2002. It employs about 34 people.[1][2]

History

Velumi began on Wharf Road as a ship chandler, selling hardware and boat fittings to the ships' crews and the island's fishermen. In 2002 it became a company and turned to garden and park furniture, when the town began to furnish its parks and the hotels their gardens, and moved to its shop on Reef Street.

Operations

Velumi buys from Lumeway Distribution in Bayvarra and sells to customers on the island, from its shop and for larger projects. Its largest customer is the Port of Selanu, whose harbour parks it furnishes with the Serra park bench of Valdara Industries and keeps in spare frames. The benches come by sea, by the pallet; parts needed at short notice come by air with Kelaro Air Cargo. Like Lumeway's other customers, it pays its invoices to Harbourline Factoring. In its shop it sells the Serra bench to the island's hotels and households. The shop is open Monday to Saturday. Besides park benches it sells outdoor lighting, playground equipment and garden tools.
