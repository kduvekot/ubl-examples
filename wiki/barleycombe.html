Barleycombe is a small town in Bayvarra, with about 6,000 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies in a valley of the western wheat land between Marrowdale and the border, where two streams meet.

History

British settlers founded it in the 1850s.

Economy

The market of the western wheat land's barley, wheat and sheep farms.
