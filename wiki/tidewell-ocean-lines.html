Tidewell Ocean Lines

Bayvarran shipping line between Lumeport and Selanu

Tidewell Ocean Lines is a shipping line in Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 1974 to run the regular service between Bayvarra and Lisavu. It sails container feeders between Lumeport and Selanu and employs about 776 people.

History

Tidewell was founded in 1974 under the Lumeport Agreement between Bayvarra and Lisavu, which called for a regular shipping service to tie the island to the mainland. In December 2025 it bought a second feeder second-hand, the Tidewell Reef, which came under Bayvarra's flag on 15 January 2026.

Operations

The line sails weekly from Lumeport, out through the estuary and round the island's south-eastern cape, about 1,200 km to Selanu in about 48 hours. Its ships carry the island's imports, among them benches for Velumi Trading and chilled fruit in reefers, and bring back the island's export, the cotton bales of Selanu Cotton Ginnery for Lumeport Textile Mill. Its booking office in Lumeport's Dockside works every day from 06:00 to 22:00.

Fleet

Both ships are registered in Lumeport. The Tidewell Harbour, of 9,940 gross tonnes, takes about 1,000 TEU with a crew of 14; the Tidewell Reef, of 7,480 gross tonnes, about 700 TEU with a crew of 12. Until the flag state verifies its security system, the Tidewell Reef sails with an interim security certificate.
