Lumeway Distribution

Bayvarran wholesale distributor in Marrowdale

Lumeway Distribution is a wholesale distributor in Marrowdale, Bayvarra, founded in 1988. It is the distributor of Valdara Industries for Bayvarra and Lisavu and employs about 78 people.[1][2]

History

Lumeway began in 1988 as the wholesale arm of a timber merchant of Marrowdale, distributing garden furniture and tools to Bayvarra's shops. When the Lumeport Trade Accord entered into force in 1996, Lumeway became Valdara's distributor under a distribution agreement, which is why Valdara's benches reach the island through Lumeport. The agreement reserves public tenders to Valdara itself.

Operations

Lumeway buys the Serra park bench and its spare side frames from Valdara, stocks them in its distribution centre at Marrowdale Business Park and publishes a catalogue for its customers. Goods arrive by truck from Ferrano, with Trasvia Haulage, and by container train on the distribution centre's own rail siding. Its goods-in takes deliveries in the morning only, in slots booked a week ahead, and its customs desk clears its imports on weekdays. Besides Valdara's benches it distributes garden furniture and tools from Bayvarran makers.

Trade

It sells at home to Brightmarket Stores and on the island to Velumi Trading, to the island at prices that include sea freight and insurance, which it buys through Bayforth Forwarding. It sells its receivables to Harbourline Factoring, so its customers pay the factor.
