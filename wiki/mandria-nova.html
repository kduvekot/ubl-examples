Mandria Nova

Small town in Urvania

Mandria Nova
CountryUrvania
Population4,420
FoundedItalian, 1880s
NamesMandria Nova (English) · Belzhan (Urvanian)

Mandria Nova (Urvanian Belzhan) is a small town in Urvania, with about 4,420 people.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also
  5. References

Geography

It lies on the plain of a large river near the north coast, where an Italian land company settled farmers from the valley on a cattle estate.

History

Italians founded it in the 1880s.

Economy

It lives from cattle and hay meadows, wheat.
