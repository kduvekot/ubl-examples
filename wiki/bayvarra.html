Federal country on the plateau and the east coast of the mainland

Bayvarra is a federal country on the mainland, east of Urvania, with about 800,000 people. It holds most of the plateau, the dry east and the low north coast. Its capital and port city is Lumeport, at the narrows of the estuary. Its currency is the lume (XBL).[1][2]

Geography

Bayvarra covers about 70,100 km². The plateau of Upland State falls to the dry east of Coastal State, a land of grazing and wheat; the north coast is low. The Lark, the largest river of the east coast, reaches the sea at Larkmouth; the Tallow and the Marrow drain the plateau south to the Runmek. On the slope north of Marrowdale lies the Upland State Forest, planted and felled by compartment.

Bayvarra shares the mainland's climate: dry, sunny summers with light north-east winds, and winters of westerly winds and fronts. The winter rain falls on the western high ground, so Bayvarra's east and north-east, in its rain shadow, are the driest land of the mainland, with about 500 mm a year, and the sunniest.

The settlers named the plateau the Upland, which gave Upland State its name; the grazing land of the dry east, drained by the Sallow, is the Sallow Downs; the low, thinly peopled north coast is the Gull Coast, after Gullhaven; and the headland behind which the first settlers landed at Larkmouth is Lark Head.

Government

Bayvarra is a federal parliamentary monarchy under the British Crown, represented by a governor-general. The federal parliament has a House of Representatives and a Senate in which the two states have equal seats; the Northern Territory sits in the House only. Each state has its own parliament and government. Bayvarra has two states and a federal territory. Upland State lies inland on the plateau, with Marrowdale; Coastal State lies along the east coast, with Lumeport and Larkmouth; the thinly peopled Northern Territory holds the low north coast, with the fishing village of Gullhaven. Each levies its own rate of the retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer: 5 % in Upland State, 6.5 % in Coastal State and 4 % in the Northern Territory.

History

British settlers landed at Larkmouth on 16 February 1824, at the mouth of the largest east-coast river, and spread over the dry east and the plateau as graziers and wheat farmers; Bayvarra keeps the landing as Settlers' Day, on the third Monday of February. In the late 1830s their company kept a trading station at the narrows of the estuary. After the Urvanian rulers' treaty with Sardinia in 1840, Britain annexed the coast and the dry east on 29 November 1841, fortified the station and laid out Lumeport there as the colony's seat; Coastal State keeps the day as Coastal State Day, on the last Monday of November. As the river trade grew, Lumeport took over from Larkmouth as capital in 1852. Few people lived on the dry east and the plateau; the Urvanian peoples lived in the valley, which stayed theirs under Sardinian protection. A boundary convention of 1857 set the border with Urvania along the Runmek and the watershed, and on 12 April 1858 the plateau became a colony of its own, Upland; Upland State keeps the day as Upland State Day, on the second Monday of April.

In the late 19th century the two colonies built their railways, and villages grew at the stations. On 16 October 1899 they joined as the federation of Bayvarra, a self-governing dominion under the British Crown, with the low north coast as federal territory; Bayvarra keeps the day as Federation Day, on the third Monday of October. It took over its own foreign affairs and its last ties to the British Parliament step by step and was fully sovereign by the early 1930s. In 1974 it signed the Lumeport Agreement with Lisavu, and in 1994 the Lumeport Trade Accord with both its neighbours.

Economy

Bayvarra's economy is a trading one: distribution, retail, the seaport and the airport of Lumeport, and the wool, wheat and timber of the plateau. Since 2011 gas from an offshore field off the north coast is sold by pipeline to Urvania. In the dry north-east, the sunniest part of the mainland, solar farms on the Sallow Downs sell their power into the Accord's wholesale market, much of it across the border to Urvania. They stand on grazing land, where the sheep still graze between the panels, and belong to a company the graziers own together with the Bank of Lumeport, which financed them.

Languages

Bayvarra speaks English, the settlers' English, which is also the working language of the Lumeport Trade Accord. Its places carry the English names the settlers gave them.
