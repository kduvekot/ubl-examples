Bayvarra

Federal country on the plateau and the east coast of the mainland

Bayvarra is a federal country on the mainland, east of Urvania, with about 800,000 people. It holds most of the plateau, the dry east and the low north coast. Its capital and port city is Lumeport, at the narrows of the estuary. Its currency is the Lume (XBL).

Geography

Bayvarra covers about 70,100 km². The plateau of Upland State falls to the dry east of Coastal State, a land of grazing and wheat; the north coast is low. The largest river of the east coast reaches the sea at Larkmouth. On the slope north of Marrowdale lies the Upland State Forest, planted and felled by compartment.

Government

Bayvarra has two states and a federal territory. Upland State lies inland on the plateau, with Marrowdale; Coastal State lies along the east coast, with Lumeport and Larkmouth; the thinly peopled Northern Territory holds the low north coast, with the fishing village of Gullhaven. Each levies its own rate of the retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer: 5 % in Upland State, 6.5 % in Coastal State and 4 % in the Northern Territory.

History

British settlers landed at Larkmouth in the 1820s, at the mouth of the largest east-coast river, and spread over the dry east and the plateau as graziers and wheat farmers; Bayvarra keeps the landing as Settlers' Day. In the late 1830s their company kept a trading station at the narrows of the estuary. About 1841 Britain annexed the coast and the dry east, fortified the station and laid out Lumeport there as the colony's seat; as the river trade grew it took over from Larkmouth as capital. Few people lived on the dry east and the plateau; the Urvanian peoples lived in the valley, which stayed theirs under Sardinian protection. A boundary convention of about 1857 set the border with Urvania along the main river and the watershed, and in 1858 the plateau became a colony of its own, Upland.

In the late 19th century the two colonies built their railways, and villages grew at the stations. On 16 October 1899 they joined as the federation of Bayvarra, a self-governing dominion under the British Crown, with the low north coast as federal territory; Bayvarra keeps the day as Federation Day, on the third Monday of October. It took over its own foreign affairs and its last ties to the British Parliament step by step and was fully sovereign by the early 1930s. In 1974 it signed the Lumeport Agreement with Lisavu, and in 1994 the Lumeport Trade Accord with both its neighbours.

Economy

Bayvarra's economy is a trading one: distribution, retail, the seaport and the airport of Lumeport, and the wool, wheat and timber of the plateau. Since 2011 gas from an offshore field off the north coast is sold by pipeline to Urvania.
