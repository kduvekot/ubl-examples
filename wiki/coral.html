Coral

Currency of Lisavu

Coral
CodeXLC
CountryLisavu
Minor unit1/1000

The coral is the currency of Lisavu, divided into a thousand parts.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Exchange
  2. See also
  3. References

Exchange

The coral was tied to the lume by the Lumeport Agreement of 1974, at two and a half lume to the coral. The Lumeport Trade Accord kept that rate when it brought the ferra into the parity in 1996.
