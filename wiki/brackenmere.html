Brackenmere

Small town in Bayvarra

Brackenmere
CountryBayvarra

Brackenmere is a small town in Bayvarra, with about 8,000 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies between Marrowdale and Lumeport on the road and the railway, by a spring-fed pond (the mere) in bracken-covered ground: a stage on the road down to the capital.

History

It was founded by British settlers in the 1840s.

Economy

It lives from its station and the road, the farms round it, joinery for Marrowdale's furniture trade.
