Crevalto Bank

Crevalto Bank is a bank in Ferrano, Urvania, founded in 1921, before the country's independence. It is listed on the Ferrano Stock Exchange and employs about 520 people.

History

The bank was founded in 1921, under Italian rule, as the savings and credit bank of the Province of Ferrano, in the Italian manner of a provincial savings bank, to take the savings of the valley's farmers and craftsmen and lend to them. In 1993, as Urvania opened its economy, it became a public limited company and was listed on the Ferrano Stock Exchange.

Operations

Crevalto Bank holds the accounts of most of Urvania's trading companies around Ferrano: Serravona Mining, Valdara Industries, Castrano Logistics, Rinova Recovery, the carriers Ferrano Rail Cargo, Trasvia Haulage and Fiumella Barges, the energy companies Ferrano Energy, Montebrio Power and Ferrano Grid, and Tostara Wood Modification. It receives their payments from home and abroad. When Valdara tendered for park benches in Lumeport, Crevalto gave the counter-guarantee on which Bank of Lumeport issued Valdara's tender guarantee.

Branches

Its head office and main branch are in the Palazzo Crevalto on the Piazza della Banca in Ferrano. It has a second branch in Ferrano, Porta Nord, and a branch in the mining village of Serravona.
