Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra

Customs and border service of Bayvarra

The Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra is Bayvarra's federal customs and border authority. It clears imports and exports at the land border crossing with Urvania, at the seaport and at the airport of Lumeport.

History

The service was formed at federation in 1899 from the customs services of the two colonies.

Operations

Its border office stands at Euridice Crossing in Upland State, facing the Urvanian customs post of Valico Euridice across the road; it is open around the clock. It also has a post at the rail border station, offices at the seaport and the airport of Lumeport, and an inland office at Marrowdale, where goods arriving by rail under the Accord's common transit are declared. Goods of Accord origin enter free of duty on proof of origin. Bayvarra values imports as they left the exporting country, without freight, and charges nothing further at the border, because its sales tax falls once at the final retail sale.

Organisation

As a public body set up by law, the service is entered in Bayvarra's federal list of public bodies, not in the business register. It gives each trader a trader number built from the trader's number in its register. It reports to the Federal Treasury. Its head office is in Lumeport, and it keeps five posts: the border crossing, the seaport, the airport, Marrowdale and the harbour of Larkmouth. The service has a staff of about 700.
