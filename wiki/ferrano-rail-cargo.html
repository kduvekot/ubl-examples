Ferrano Rail Cargo

Urvanian rail freight company running ore and container trains

Ferrano Rail Cargo is a rail freight company of Urvania, with its depot at Serravona in the Province of Ferrano. It was founded in 1964 and runs the ore trains from the mine to the bench works at Ferrano and the container trains across the border to Marrowdale. It employs about 380 people.[1][2]

History

The company was set up in 1964, two years after independence, as the freight arm of Urvania's state railway, to run the ore trains from the mine to the new bench works at Ferrano; its depot is still at Serravona, where its oldest trains start. In 2004 Urvania separated its rail infrastructure from its train operators: the tracks, the train paths and the traffic control went to the Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania, and the company's managers and staff bought the freight business, with a loan from Crevalto Bank. It has run its trains on the paths the agency gives it since.

Operations

Its ore trains carry the ore of Serravona Mining about 159 km down the ore line from the mine siding to the works of Valdara Industries in Ferrano, priced per tonne. Its container trains run from the Ferrano Intermodal Hub across the border to the rail siding of Lumeway Distribution in Marrowdale, under the Accord's uniform rules for international carriage by rail. For these it sells container slots under a framework contract with Valdara, payable 30 days net, and its own tractor brings the packed containers from the warehouse to the hub. A container gets five free days at the consignee's siding; after that the company charges demurrage per container day. Its customers are Valdara and Serravona Mining; the spare container slots on the Marrowdale train it sells to other makers of Ferrano.

Fleet

The company runs the container train FRC 4417, the ore train FRC 2208 with its bulk ore wagons, and a road tractor for its container haulage to the hub.
