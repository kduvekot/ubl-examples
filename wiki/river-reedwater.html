River Reedwater

River of Bayvarra

River Reedwater
CountryBayvarra
Length73 km
Basin1,517 km²
Sourceabout 140 m
MouthSallow

The Reedwater is a river of Bayvarra, 73 km long, with a basin of 1,517 km². It rises at about 140 m and joins the Sallow.
