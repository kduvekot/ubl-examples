Marrowdale Sawmill

Sawmill and timber modifier in Marrowdale, Bayvarra

Marrowdale Sawmill is a sawmill on the north bank of the river at Marrowdale, in Upland State, Bayvarra, founded in 2004. It saws logs from the Upland State Forest into planks, slats and beams, modifies ash in a kiln of its own and employs about 85 people.

History

The mill was built in 2004 by the Marrowdale timber merchant whose wholesale arm is Lumeway Distribution, when Upland State put the timber of its planted forest up for sale on long contracts.

Operations

Its logs come from the Upland State Forest, a managed forest on the slope north of Marrowdale. The mill makes the slats of the Serra park bench to the drawing of Valdara Industries, thermally modified ash of 1,800 × 95 × 45 mm, and sells them by the bundle of 50; once a quarter 96 bundles go across the border to Valdara's factory in Ferrano. It also sells sawn oak and ash by the cubic metre to Brackenmere Furniture in Marrowdale. Its own timber truck with a crane carries logs and loads.

Contract modification

From October to March, the southern summer, the mill's kiln is full with its own decking and cladding. For those months it buys kiln slots from Tostara Wood Modification near Ferrano under a season agreement: it sends its kiln-dried ash blanks across the border, the plant modifies and stores them, and the mill sells the finished slats to Valdara from that stock in Urvania. For this trade it is registered for Urvania's value added tax.
