Lumeport–Selanu shipping service

Weekly container service between Lumeport and Lisavu

The Lumeport–Selanu shipping service links Lumeport and Selanu by container ship. Tidewell Ocean Lines was founded in 1974 to run it, under the Lumeport Agreement between Bayvarra and Lisavu.

Route

The ships sail out through the estuary and the bay and round Cape Lavase, the island's southern cape, to the lee side, about 1,200 km, in about 48 hours at 13.5 knots.
