Hartsedge

Small town in Bayvarra

Hartsedge
CountryBayvarra
Population5,000
FoundedBritish, 1880s (the station)

Hartsedge is a small town in Bayvarra, with about 5,000 people.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also
  5. References

Geography

It lies at a station on the wool line between Marrowdale and Larkmouth, on the edge of a rise.

History

It was founded by the British in the 1880s (the station).

Economy

The station's stock yards and grain store, the sheep and cattle runs.
