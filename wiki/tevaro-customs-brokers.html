Tevaro Customs Brokers

Licensed customs broker in Selanu, Lisavu

Tevaro Customs Brokers is a licensed customs broker in Selanu, Lisavu, founded in 1975. It lodges import declarations for the island's buyers and acts as tax representative. It employs about 13 people.[1][2]

History

Clerks of the island's customs house founded Tevaro in 1975, the year after the Lumeport Agreement, when the new regular shipping service from Lumeport brought more import declarations than the island's merchants could lodge themselves.

Operations

Its office is on Wharf Road by the port. Tevaro clears goods that arrive at the Port of Selanu for importers such as Velumi Trading, which brings in park benches from Lumeway Distribution. At the airport it declares the medicines that Selanu General Hospital receives by air from Quillmoor Pharma Distribution, as the hospital's direct representative, in the hospital's name. Its declarations go to the Island Customs Service, which collects the island's import consumption tax. As tax representative it acts for about 20 firms of the mainland that install or service machines on the island without an establishment there, such as the engineers of the gin makers and the suppliers of the port's cranes.
