Bayforth Forwarding

Freight forwarder in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Bayforth Forwarding is a freight forwarder in the Dockside quarter of Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 1976. It books sea and air freight, clears exports, arranges cargo insurance and runs its own trucks within Bayvarra. It employs about 95 people.

History

A Lumeport wool-broking house set up Bayforth in 1976, to forward the island trade that grew after the Lumeport Agreement of 1974 linked Bayvarra and Lisavu by a regular shipping service. It added air freight in 1983, when the cargo terminal at Lumeport airport opened, and reefer haulage in 1998, when Hafefo Fresh Produce began to bring the fruit of Harrowmead Fruit Growers to the island in refrigerated containers; its truck with a clip-on generator set came in 2019.

Operations

Bayforth books containers with Tidewell Ocean Lines and air freight with Kelaro Air Cargo, lodges export declarations as customs agent and arranges cargo insurance with Wavesure Cargo Insurance. Its customers include Lumeway Distribution, whose benches it ships to the island, the Lumeport Textile Mill, whose cotton it brings from the port, Harrowmead Fruit Growers, for whom it ships apples and pears to Selanu in reefers, and Quillmoor Pharma Distribution, whose medicines it takes to the airport. Its trucks run between Marrowdale, the Port of Lumeport, Lumeport airport and the shops and works of the city. Besides its office in Dockside it has a branch at the rail yard in Marrowdale. It has no office in Urvania, and on Lisavu Tevaro Customs Brokers acts as its agent.

Fleet

The fleet has a truck with a curtain-side trailer for 33 pallets, a container chassis truck, and a second container chassis truck with a clip-on generator set, which keeps a reefer's refrigeration running on the road.
