Lisavu Cotton Farm is a cotton farm near Lintwell on the dry north-eastern plain of Lisavu, run by its owner since 2009.

Operations

The farm grows cotton on the plain where the island's rain shadow keeps the land dry and sunny, land that has grown cotton since the estates of the 1860s. After the harvest, from March to May, it delivers its seed cotton to Selanu Cotton Ginnery, which weighs and grades it and settles each delivery for the farm itself.
