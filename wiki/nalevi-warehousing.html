Nalevi Warehousing

Warehousing and haulage company in Selanu, Lisavu

Nalevi Warehousing

Nalevi Warehousing is a warehousing company on Lagoon Road in Selanu, Lisavu, founded in 1998. It receives and stores imported goods for island businesses and collects containers and cargo at the island's port and airport with its own truck. It employs about 17 people.

History

Nalevi began as a lighterage firm at the wharf of Selanu, unloading ships by boat. When containers came to the island after the Lumeport Trade Accord entered into force, it built a warehouse on Lagoon Road and bought a truck, and in 1998 it became a warehousing company under its present name. Its name is the Lisavans' old name for the beach by the wharf of Selanu where the lighters were drawn up; the elders' stories give no meaning for it.

Operations

Nalevi receives goods for Velumi Trading and confirms what arrived. Its container truck collects containers at the Port of Selanu and air cargo at Selanu Airport Cargo, and carries for others on the island: the bales of Selanu Cotton Ginnery to the port, batteries to the mast site of Selanu Telecom on Mast Hill, reefers of fruit to the cold store of Hafefo Fresh Produce, and sample benches between the airport and the trade fair of the Selanu Chamber of Commerce. Its warehouse has about 4,000 m² of floor; its goods-inwards desk is open Monday to Saturday. Its other customers include the island's hardware and building merchants and the port's depot for empty containers. It runs one truck.
