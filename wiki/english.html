English

Working language of the Lumeport Trade Accord

English
Own nameEnglish

English is the working language of the Lumeport Trade Accord, the language of Bayvarra and an official language of Urvania and Lisavu, beside Urvanian and Lisavan.[1][2]

Varieties

The three countries write English in the British manner: colour, harbour, centre, tonne; dates as 15 April or Monday 11 May; the 24-hour clock; a comma for thousands and a point for decimals. Each has its own variety.

Bayvarran English, the settlers' English, keeps plain words of its own, a truck rather than a lorry and a store for a shop, and ends an address with the town, the state and the postcode.

Urvanian English uses the Italian words of its streets and places as English, via, piazza, corso, strada, banchina, and gives amounts in whole ferra.

Lisavan English stands beside Lisavan on the island, calls the capital Selanu rather than Port Wrenfield, takes a few Lisavan words into its English, and gives amounts in coral to three decimals.
