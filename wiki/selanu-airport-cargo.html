Selanu Airport Cargo

Selanu Airport Cargo handles the air cargo at Lisavu's airport, on the plain about 10 km north of Selanu, within the locality of Lintwell. It was founded in 1989 and employs about 15 people.

History

The Port of Selanu opened it as a branch in 1989, two years after the island had founded its own airline, Kelaro Air Cargo; the airfield itself opened in 1982. The airfield lies in Lintwell but bears the name of the capital it serves.

Operations

Its import desk is open around the clock. It receives the freighter flights of Kelaro Air Cargo from Lumeport and holds the cargo until it is cleared and collected: spare parts for park benches, sample benches for the trade fair of the Selanu Chamber of Commerce, batteries for the mast of Selanu Telecom, and medicines in temperature-controlled containers for Selanu General Hospital, whose temperature readings it records on arrival. Most of the cargo is collected by Nalevi Warehousing. It also handles the island's outbound air cargo: mail, cotton samples and chilled fish for Lumeport. The runway, the control tower and the passenger terminal are run by the civil aviation office of the island's government, as they have been since the airfield opened, and Kelaro's own ground staff handle the passengers' baggage.
