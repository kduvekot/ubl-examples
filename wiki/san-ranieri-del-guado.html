San Ranieri del Guado is a village in Urvania, with about 1,680 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies at an old ford of the upper river, a day's walk above the rapids: friars of the Tuscan voyage of 1609-10 built a chapel to Pisa's saint beside the Urvanian hamlet at the ford; the chapel outlasted the trading post.

History

It was founded by Tuscans about 1611 (the friars' chapel); the Urvanian hamlet is older.

Economy

It lives from fields on the valley floor, vineyards and orchards, a little pilgrimage to the chapel.
