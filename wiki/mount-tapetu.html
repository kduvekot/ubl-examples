Mount Tapetu

Highest mountain of Lisavu

Mount Tapetu
KindMountain
CountryLisavu
Heightabout 1,810 m
NamesTapetu (Lisavan) · Mount Tapetu (English)

Mount Tapetu (Lisavan Tapetu, mountain) is the highest mountain of Lisavu, about 1,810 m, the summit of the old volcano that forms the west of the island. Its top holds the caldera called the Cauldron, and the river Tapetu, named after it, rises just below the summit.

History

By the Lisavans' tradition the first canoes, about 2,000 years ago, steered for its peak, seen far out at sea, and landed in the bay of Natahoma. The settlers kept its Lisavan name.

The Cauldron

The caldera at the top, in Lisavan Vukaka (cauldron), is a basin about 7 km across and about 650 m deep; the settlers translated its name as the Cauldron. The elders' stories say that the island's fire sleeps there. The summit, about 12 °C colder than the coast, has snow in some winters.
