Park bench Serra 180

Park bench made by Valdara Industries

Park bench Serra 180
MakerValdara Industries
Dimensions1800 × 620 × 780 mm
Weight44.1 kg

The Park bench Serra 180 is a park bench of Valdara Industries, 1,800 mm long, for bolting to the ground. Its frame is of galvanised and powder-coated ductile cast iron, its five slats of thermally modified ash.[1]

Design

The two side frames are cast in Valdara's foundry at Ferrano from iron ore and about 35 % steel and iron scrap. The slats come from Marrowdale Sawmill.
