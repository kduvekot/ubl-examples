Forte Euridice

Country town in Urvania

Forte Euridice
CountryUrvania
Population14,000

NamesForte Euridice (English) · Girkul (Urvanian)

Forte Euridice is a country town in Urvania, with about 14,000 people.[1][2]

  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also
  5. References

Geography

It lies on the north bank in the angle between the Runmek and the Bruna, the largest plateau river, just above their confluence, where the land border meets the river: the Sardinian protectorate built a fort here to watch the river and the British settlers beyond the plateau river, and named it after the corvette Euridice of its station on the Plata, which had first shown the flag on the river; the boundary convention of 1857 made the Runmek below the confluence, and the watershed north of it, the border.

History

Sardinians founded it in 1842 (the fort); the village grew up after it.

Economy

The border crossing: customs agents, hauliers and warehouses, the railway, the valley's farms.
