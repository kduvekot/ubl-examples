Fierabruna

Country town in Urvania

Fierabruna
CountryUrvania
Population26,000

NamesFierabruna (English) · Hihek (Urvanian)

Fierabruna is a country town in Urvania, with about 26,000 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies where two large rivers of the plateau meet north-east of Ferrano, on the drovers' way down from the hills: the old cattle fair of the region.

History

Urvanians founded it before 1600.

Economy

The cattle fair and the meat works, the market of the plateau's farms, schools and the hospital of the hills.
