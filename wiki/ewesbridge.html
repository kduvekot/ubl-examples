Ewesbridge is a village in Bayvarra, with about 1,000 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies on the road north from Larkmouth over the dry east, at a bridge over a creek.

History

It was founded by British settlers in the 1830s.

Economy

It lives from a sheep station's village: wool, the road.
