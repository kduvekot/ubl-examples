Robin Ashdale

Household in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Robin Ashdale
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
What it doesConsumer

Robin Ashdale is a private person who lives in the Riverside quarter of Lumeport, Bayvarra.

In the world

Robin Ashdale shops at Brightmarket Stores, both in its Lumeport store and in its web shop, and has bought furniture and a shirt there. The household takes its water and district heat from the Coastal State Water Company and pays on account, a monthly amount collected by direct debit and settled once a year after the meters are read.
