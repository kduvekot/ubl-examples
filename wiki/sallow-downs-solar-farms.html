Sallow Downs solar farms

Solar farms on the grazing land of Bayvarra's dry north-east

Sallow Downs solar farms
KindSolar farms
CountryBayvarra
Capacityabout 120 MW
In service2017
Ownerthe graziers of the Sallow Downs with the Bank of Lumeport (25-xb)

The Sallow Downs solar farms are solar power stations on the Sallow Downs, the grazing land of Bayvarra's dry north-east and the sunniest part of the mainland.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Ownership
  2. Trade
  3. See also
  4. References

Ownership

The farms belong to a company the graziers of the Downs own together with the Bank of Lumeport, which financed them. The panels stand on the grazing land, and the sheep graze between the rows.

Trade

The farms sell their power on the Lumeport Trade Accord's wholesale market, much of it across the border to Urvania.
