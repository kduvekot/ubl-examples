Department of Agriculture of Lisavu

Lisavu's department of agriculture and plant protection

Department of Agriculture of Lisavu
SeatSelanu, Lisavu
Reports toits own minister in the island's government

The Department of Agriculture of Lisavu is the island's government department for agriculture, on Reef Street in Selanu. Its Plant Protection Service is Lisavu's plant protection organisation, and the department keeps the island's farm register.[1]

History

The department was set up in 1921, after a cotton pest had reached the estates, to keep pests off the island; it still certifies the island's lint free of pests.

Operations

The Plant Protection Service issues the phytosanitary certificates of plants and plant products that leave the island, such as the cotton lint that Selanu Cotton Ginnery ships to Lumeport Textile Mill, certifying it free of pests. The farm register records the island's growers, such as Lisavu Cotton Farm; a grower's registration is renewed each season, so each entry carries an expiry date. It also inspects the plants and food that come into the island and advises the island's farms. The department answers to its own minister in the island's government.
