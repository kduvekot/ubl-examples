NamesPastora (Italian) · Nekskir (Urvanian)

The Pastora is a river of Urvania, 138 km long, with a basin of 6,127 km². It rises at about 1,460 m and reaches the sea on the north-west coast at Porto Riparo. Its Urvanian name, Nekskir, means herd.[1][2]
