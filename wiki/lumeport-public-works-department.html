Lumeport Public Works Department

Public works department of the city of Lumeport

Lumeport Public Works Department
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
What it doesContracting Authority

The Lumeport Public Works Department is the public works department of the city of Lumeport, Bayvarra, on Civic Square in the Government Hill quarter. It buys goods, works and services for the city under procurement law.

  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. Taxes
  4. See also

History

The department has been the city's works department since 1872, when the deep-water quays and the streets and drains behind them were built.

Operations

Its procurement office publishes the city's notices through the Bayvarra Information Service and receives and evaluates tenders. Under its parks renewal programme it holds a framework contract with Valdara Industries for park benches for the city's parks, with their installation by Saltmere Engineering as a second lot; it pays Valdara in lume, 30 days after each accepted release. It runs a design contest for the bench of Lumeport's harbour promenade, and buys office furniture with installation from Brackenmere Furniture for the fit-out of its planning office. Its parks depot receives the goods. Besides buying for the city it builds and keeps Lumeport's streets, drains, parks, public buildings and the harbour promenade. It is a department of the city of Lumeport, in four divisions under the city council.

Taxes

As a public body it buys free of Coastal State's sales tax. It withholds 10 % of the price of a service bought from a business not established in Bayvarra and pays it to the state.
