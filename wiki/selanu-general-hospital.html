Selanu General Hospital

General hospital of Lisavu, in Selanu

Selanu General Hospital
SeatSelanu, Lisavu
What it doesHospital (XL)
Websitehttps://www.selanu-hospital.example

Selanu General Hospital is the general hospital of Lisavu, on Reef Street in Selanu, a public body of the island's health service. It has wards, an operating theatre, the island's vaccination service and a small nuclear medicine unit.

History

The island's rulers and the estates built the hospital in 1891 as the island's cottage hospital. It is still the island's only hospital.

Operations

The nuclear medicine unit makes its technetium-99m every morning from a generator that is replaced once a week. The hospital pharmacy buys medicines, vaccines and the generator from Quillmoor Pharma Distribution, a wholesaler beside Lumeport airport, among them the vaccines for the island's winter influenza campaign. They come by air on the freighter of Kelaro Air Cargo; Tevaro Customs Brokers clears them for the hospital, free of duty and of the import consumption tax, and the pharmacy's van fetches them from the airport.

Fleet

The pharmacy's van has a compartment kept at 2 to 8 °C, a freezer box and a locked, lined box for radioactive packages; its drivers are trained to carry radioactive material.
