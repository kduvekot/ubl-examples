Selanu Chamber of Commerce

Chamber of commerce of Lisavu, in Selanu

Selanu Chamber of Commerce
SeatSelanu, Lisavu
What it doesChamber of Commerce (XL)

The Selanu Chamber of Commerce is the chamber of commerce of Lisavu, seated in Selanu. It is a public body that guarantees ATA carnets on the island, classes the island's cotton and holds a trade fair.

Contents
  1. Operations
  2. Seat
  3. See also

Operations

As the island's guarantor of ATA carnets, the chamber answers for goods that enter Lisavu for a while and leave again, such as samples for a fair. When the proof that such goods left is missing after their time limit, it asks the chamber that issued the carnet for it, as it asked the Ferrano Chamber of Commerce for two sample benches of Valdara Industries. The chamber also classes the cotton lint of Selanu Cotton Ginnery, and its cotton fund is fed by a levy of 1 % that farms collect on their seed cotton. In early June it holds a trade fair in Selanu.

Seat

Its offices are on Reef Street in Selanu, with a carnet office and a certificates office.
