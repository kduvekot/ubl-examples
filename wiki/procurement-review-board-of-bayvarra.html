Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra

Bayvarra's independent body for public procurement appeals

Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
Reports tothe federal parliament

The Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra is an independent public body in Lumeport, Bayvarra, that hears appeals in public procurement.[1]

Contents
  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. Seat
  4. See also
  5. References

History

The board was set up under the Trade Accord's procurement protocol of 1999, which opened the members' public tenders to suppliers from the partners and asked each member for an independent body to hear appeals.

Operations

A tenderer or a candidate who disputes a decision of a contracting authority appeals to the board. Contracting authorities name it as the place for appeals, as the Lumeport Public Works Department does in its tender for the city's park benches and in its design contest for the harbour promenade bench. A candidate excluded from a contest is told how to appeal to it. It hears about 25 appeals a year and upholds about a third of them. It is independent of the government and reports to the federal parliament.

Seat

The board has its registry at Justice Row 20 on Government Hill, in a building of its own, apart from the government quarter where the contracting authorities have their offices: its independence from the authorities whose awards it reviews. The registry is open on weekdays from 08:00 to 17:00. The board has a chair and four members, with a staff of about 40.
