Fernhollow Studio

Small industrial design studio in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Fernhollow Studio

Founded2019-04-01
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra

What it doesDesign Studio

Fernhollow Studio is a small industrial design studio in Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 2019 by two designers. It employs about 4 people.

Operations

The studio works from Ropewalk Lane in Lumeport. It asked to take part in the city's design contest for the bench of its harbour promenade, held by the Lumeport Public Works Department, and was excluded at the selection: the certificate of the social security fund showed contributions due and unpaid, above the minor amounts the procurement act tolerates. The first prize went to the team of Brackenmere Furniture and Saltmere Engineering.
