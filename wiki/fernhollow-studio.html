Fernhollow Studio

Small industrial design studio in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Fernhollow Studio is a small industrial design studio in Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 2019 by two designers. It employs about 4 people.[1]

Contents
  1. Operations
  2. See also
  3. References

Operations

The studio works from Ropewalk Lane in Lumeport. It designs furniture and fittings for public spaces and for makers without a designer of their own, and it enters the design contests that public buyers such as the Lumeport Public Works Department hold for street furniture. Like every candidate in Bayvarra's public procurement, it must show at the selection a certificate of the social security fund that no contributions are due and unpaid beyond the minor amounts the procurement act tolerates.
