Vallerna

River of Urvania

Vallerna
CountryUrvania
Length177 km
Basin8,067 km²
Sourceabout 907 m
Mouththe sea
TributariesGemella
NamesVallerna (Italian) · Binskaz (Urvanian)

The Vallerna is a river of Urvania, 177 km long, with a basin of 8,067 km². It rises at about 907 m and reaches the sea on the west coast. Its Urvanian name, Binskaz, means west.
