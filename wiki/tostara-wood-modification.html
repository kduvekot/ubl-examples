Tostara Wood Modification

Urvanian thermal wood modification plant at Conciaro

Tostara Wood Modification is an independent plant that thermally modifies wood in two steam kilns at Conciaro, east of Ferrano, Urvania. Founded in 2011, it works for Urvanian timber merchants and, under contract, for sawmills across the border, and employs about 22 people.[1][2]

History

A group of Urvanian timber merchants set up Tostara in 2011 beside the leather works at Conciaro, whose boiler house sells it the steam for its kilns. Its name comes from the Italian tostare, to roast, as the trade calls wood modified by heat.

Operations

The plant stands on Via delle Concerie in Conciaro, the village where the town once put its tanneries, downwind. It modifies beech, ash and poplar at about 212 °C in steam, then planes, end-seals and bundles the wood, stores it for its owner in covered bays and hands it to the carrier the owner names. It holds an authorisation for inward processing, so wood of a foreign owner is worked without Urvania's import tax until the owner releases it. Its two kilns hold about 40 m³ each and modify about 6,000 m³ of wood a year.

Customers

Its main customer across the border is Marrowdale Sawmill, which buys kiln slots under a season agreement for the months when its own kiln is busy. Tostara modifies the sawmill's kiln-dried ash blanks into park-bench slats for Valdara Industries and keeps them until the sawmill calls them off for delivery to Valdara's works.

Products

  • Thermal modification and finishing, bundle of 50 bench slats
  • Storage of a finished job, bay of 24 pallets
