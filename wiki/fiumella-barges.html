Fiumella Barges

Urvanian river barge operator at Ferrano

Fiumella Barges
TypeSocietà a responsabilità urvaniana (private limited company)
Founded15 April 1953
SeatFerrano, Urvania

Founded byLigurian boatmen from Marina Ligure

Fiumella Barges carries bulk cargo by barge on the Runmek, from Ferrano in Urvania down to the coast of Bayvarra. It has worked the river since 1953 and employs about 30 people.

History

Ligurian boatmen from Marina Ligure, on Urvania's south coast, founded Fiumella in 1953, under the British administration, and brought motor barges to the river, which until then had carried its bulk goods to the sea under sail and oar. Its name is Italian for little river.

Operations

Its office and quay are on Banchina del Fiume in Ferrano. The barges work below the rapids above the town, which end navigation, and sail about 250 km down the river and the estuary towards Lumeport without locks. They sail by daylight only, and the receiver's morning shift unloads them. Fiumella carries, among other loads, the recycled plastic granulate of Rinova Recovery in one-tonne bags from Rinova's own quay to the river quay of Rivermouth Packaging; the freight of goods going abroad is free of Urvania's value added tax.

Fleet

Fiumella runs six dry-cargo barges, each with a crew of three; besides recycled granulate they carry sand and gravel, grain and fertiliser. Its barge Fiumella IV, of 1,210 gross tonnes, is registered at Ferrano.
