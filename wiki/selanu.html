Selanu

Capital and only town of Lisavu

Selanu (English Port Wrenfield) is the capital and only town of Lisavu, with about 30,000 people, on a sheltered bay of the island's east coast. It holds the island's port, and its airport lies on the plain to the north.[1][2]

History

Lisavan village

The Lisavan village on the bay gave the town its name. By about the 14th century it was the seat of the rulers of the whole island, whose meeting ground, the Litakima, drew the elders of every village each spring.

Port Wrenfield

In the 1860s the English settlers of the cotton estates built Port Wrenfield on the wharf north of the village, with its street of stores and its customs house, and offices, churches and houses rose on the slope behind the wharf. From 1884 the protectorate's signal station on Signal Hill announced the ships.

Agreement Quarter

The Lumeport Agreement of 1974 brought the regular shipping service, and with it the warehouses, brokers and trades of the Agreement Quarter.
