Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania

Urvania's public rail infrastructure manager

Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania
SeatFerrano, Urvania

The Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania is the public body that owns and maintains Urvania's railway tracks, allocates the train paths and dispatches every train on them. It is seated on Via della Stazione in Ferrano.

Contents
  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. See also

History

Urvania set up the agency in 2004, when it separated its rail infrastructure from the train operators of the state railway, as it later separated its electricity grid from supply; the railway's freight arm became Ferrano Rail Cargo, a company of its own. The agency reports to Urvania's Ministry of Transport.

Operations

The agency's network covers the ore line from the mine siding at Fucinalta down to Valdara Industries' works at Ferrano and the line from Ferrano to the border station on the way to Marrowdale. It also maintains the lines' bridges, among them the ore line's river bridge, 78 m high. Its traffic control works around the clock. Train operators such as Ferrano Rail Cargo run their trains on the paths it gives them, and it reports a train's position and timetable on its network to the operator, from the intermodal hub at Ferrano to the border station. Its network has about 420 km of line and 26 stations; the ore line is about 166 km long and the line from Ferrano to the border about 67 km.
