Signal Hill

Young volcanic cone above Selanu, Lisavu

Signal Hill
KindHill
CountryLisavu
Heightabout 780 m
NamesSeife (Lisavan) · Signal Hill (English)

Signal Hill (Lisavan Seife, lookout) is the young volcanic cone of about 780 m on the south-east of Lisavu, west of Selanu, from which the sea to the east and south lies open to view.[1][2]

History

From its top the watchmen of the island's rulers looked out for canoes. From 1884, under the British protectorate, a signal station on the summit announced the ships coming in to Port Wrenfield, which gave the hill its English name.
