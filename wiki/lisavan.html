Lisavan

Language of Lisavu

Lisavan
Own namekopele
Spoken inLisavu

Lisavan is the language of Lisavu and, beside English, one of the island's two official languages. Its words are syllables of a consonant and a vowel, without clusters, and end in a vowel.

Place names

Most of the island's villages have Lisavan names only, among them Felulaka, Natahoma and Setahu. The villages of the 19th-century cotton estates have English names with Lisavan ones beside them: Lintwell (Kefalasa) and Bollworth (Pakesa). The capital keeps its Lisavan name, Selanu, beside the English Port Wrenfield.
