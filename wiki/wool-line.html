Wool line

Railway from Marrowdale to the harbour of Larkmouth

Wool line
KindRailway
CountryBayvarra

The Wool line is the railway from Marrowdale down to the harbour of Larkmouth, in Bayvarra. It was built for the wool of the plateau and the dry east.[1][2]

Contents
  1. History
  2. See also
  3. References

History

The line is one of the railways that Bayvarra's colonies laid in the late 19th century. Wool stores and sale rooms stood along its last stretch into Larkmouth from the 1880s.
