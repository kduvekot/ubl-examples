Fontebrina is a small town in Urvania, with about 4,370 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

It lies at the foot of the hills where springs rise along a line of harder rock, beside a confluence.

History

It was founded by Urvanians before 1600.

Economy

It lives from wheat and sheep, the springs bottled since the Italian period.
