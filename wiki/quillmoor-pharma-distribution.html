Quillmoor Pharma Distribution

Quillmoor Pharma Distribution is a pharmaceutical wholesaler beside the airport of Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 1991. It supplies pharmacies and hospitals in the three countries of the Lumeport Trade Accord and employs about 64 people.

History

A group of Lumeport pharmacists founded Quillmoor in 1991 to buy medicines together, and built it beside the airport, so that medicines that come in by air and go on by air never wait far from the runway.

Operations

It buys medicines, vaccines and radiopharmaceuticals from makers abroad, among them makers in India and the Netherlands, and keeps them in cold rooms at 2 to 8 °C and in a freezer room. It holds the wholesale distribution authorisation of Bayvarra's medicines regulator, which the Accord's mutual recognition makes valid in Urvania and Lisavu, and a licence to hold and transport radioactive material. Its warehouse works every day and through the night, because radiopharmaceuticals arrive and leave on night and early flights. On the island it supplies Selanu General Hospital by air, with vaccines, insulin and technetium generators for its nuclear medicine unit.

Fleet

Its own closed van, with a locked and lined cargo compartment and a driver trained for radioactive material, takes radioactive packages to the airport's cargo terminal.
