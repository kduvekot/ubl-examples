Urvanian

Language of Urvania

Urvanian
Own namebinbir
Spoken inUrvania

Urvanian is the language of Urvania's original people and, beside English, one of the country's two official languages. It has a consonant-rich sound, with words ending in -ek, -an or -ir.

History

Under Italian and then British rule the place and street names stayed Italian, while the people kept their own Urvanian names. On 17 June 1951 the British administration made Urvanian an official language beside English, in the courts, the schools and the administration, after years in which the Urvanians had asked for it; independence in 1962 kept the bilingual state. Urvania keeps 17 June as Language Day.

Place names

Urvania's places have Urvanian names beside the Italian ones that the British kept and that now count as English: Ferrano is Ruldraz, Portaggio Trenzir, Fierabruna Hihek and Forte Euridice Girkul. Streets and towns are named in both languages, and an address gives both.
