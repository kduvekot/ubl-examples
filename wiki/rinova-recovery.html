Rinova Recovery

Urvanian recycler of polyethylene production waste

Rinova Recovery is a plastics recycler in Ferrano, Urvania, founded in 2003. It recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories and turns it into granulate. It employs about 47 people.[1][2]

History

In 2003 the Environment Agency of Urvania stopped the factories' plastic waste from going to landfill. Ferrano's factories, Valdara Industries among them, set up Rinova that year as their joint recycler, and they hold its shares.

Operations

Rinova collects baled polyethylene film and offcuts from factories, Valdara Industries among them, whose waste Trasvia Haulage brings across Ferrano by truck. Each load is weighed full and empty on Rinova's weighbridge, and the waste is charged by the net tonne. The plant holds a permit for the recovery of non-hazardous plastic waste from the Environment Agency of Urvania and reports each load it receives to the agency. Its granulate is certified for its recycled content by Certovira Certification, an accredited certification body in Ferrano, which audits the plant every year. Besides Valdara, the packaging lines of Ferrano's food and drink makers supply its waste.

Facilities and trade

The plant stands on the river at Ferrano with a quay of its own. From it the granulate goes in jumbo bags of one tonne on the barges of Fiumella Barges, about 250 km down the river and the estuary, to Rivermouth Packaging on Bayvarra's coast, which makes stretch film and garment bags from it. Rinova is registered with the Codana Identification Agency and numbers its goods under its system.
