Lumeport–Selanu air cargo route

Air cargo route between Lumeport and Selanu

Lumeport–Selanu air cargo route
KindAir route
Distanceabout 1,080 km
Timetable2 h 45 east, 3 h 00 west

The Lumeport–Selanu air cargo route links the airports of Lumeport and Selanu, about 1,080 km apart. Kelaro Air Cargo, the island's airline, flies it with a freighter.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Operation
  2. See also
  3. References

Operation

The flight takes about 2½ hours from gate to gate in still air; the timetable allows 2 h 45 eastbound and 3 h 00 westbound, for the west-north-westerly winds of winter and for delays.
