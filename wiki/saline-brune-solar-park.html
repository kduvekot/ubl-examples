Saline Brune solar park

Solar power station on the south coast of Urvania

Saline Brune solar park
KindSolar park
CountryUrvania
Capacityabout 150 MW
In service2018
Ownerthe state generating company of Urvania

The Saline Brune solar park is a solar power station on the south coast of Urvania, on the dry salt-marsh edge behind Saline Brune, the sunniest stretch of the country's coast. It belongs to the state generating company.[1][2]

Operation

The park stands on flat, open land too poor for crops, beside the town's salt pans. With the rooftops of homes and farms it gives about a tenth of Urvania's electricity. Its output is greatest in the dry summers, from November to March, when the lake of the Nevola dam can hold back its water for the evening.
