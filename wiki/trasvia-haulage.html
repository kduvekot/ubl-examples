Trasvia Haulage

Urvanian road haulier for cross-border and local loads

Trasvia Haulage is a road haulier in Ferrano, Urvania, founded in 1996, when cross-border trucking opened under the Lumeport Trade Accord. It carries full truck loads across the land border with Bayvarra and within Urvania, and employs about 27 people.

History

Trasvia was founded in January 1996, the month the Lumeport Trade Accord entered into force, by owner-drivers who had driven the border road for the state transport pool; they put their trucks together in one company when goods began to cross the border free of duty.

Operations

Trasvia trucks benches and spare frames from Ferrano to Lumeway Distribution in Marrowdale and samples to Lumeport airport, and on the way back brings bench slats and stretch film to Valdara Industries. It holds an international road haulage licence and is registered with the Environment Agency of Urvania as a carrier of non-hazardous waste, which lets it take Valdara's baled waste film across Ferrano to Rinova Recovery. Its planning desk works every day from 06:00 to 22:00. It charges by the kilometre driven loaded, tolls included.

Fleet

Trasvia runs 18 tractor units and 24 trailers. Its tractor unit 07 pulls a 13.6 m curtain-side trailer for 33 pallets. Tractor and trailer weigh about 15.3 t empty, which leaves about 24.7 t of payload within the Accord's limit of 40 t.
