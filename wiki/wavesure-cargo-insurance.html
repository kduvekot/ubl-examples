Wavesure Cargo Insurance is a marine insurer in Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 1927. It insures cargo on its way overseas and employs about 120 people.

Contents
  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. See also

History

Wavesure began in 1927 as a mutual club of Lumeport's shipowners and meat and wool exporters, after the frozen meat trade from Larkmouth had made the cargoes going overseas valuable. It became a company in 1972. Besides cargo it insures exporters against invoices that go unpaid, and it owns Harbourline Factoring together with the Bank of Lumeport.

Timeline

  • 1927: founded as a mutual club of Lumeport's shipowners and meat and wool exporters
  • 1972: becomes a company
  • 1974: its founders among the shipowners who form Tidewell Ocean Lines
  • 1996: founds Harbourline Factoring with the Bank of Lumeport

Operations

Its offices are on Beacon Avenue in Dockside, Lumeport's quarter of deep-water quays and warehouses. Wavesure insures goods shipped overseas, among them the park benches that Lumeway Distribution sells to Velumi Trading on the island cost, insurance and freight: Bayforth Forwarding places the cover for Lumeway shipment by shipment, on the goods' value plus a tenth, as is usual in the trade. Its claims department handles losses and damage to the goods it covers. It also insures the apples and pears that Harrowmead Fruit Growers ship to the island in reefers, and the cotton bales that Lumeport Textile Mill buys free on board at Selanu.
