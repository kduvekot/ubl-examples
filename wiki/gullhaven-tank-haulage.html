Gullhaven Tank Haulage

Gullhaven Tank Haulage is a road tank haulier at Gullhaven, the fishing village in Bayvarra's Northern Territory, beside the gas plant of Kelpstone Gas. It was founded in 2012 and employs about 22 people.

History

The haulier began as the fishing village's own carrier, trucking the catch to Lumeport. When Kelpstone Gas began selling propane in 2012, a year after the gas plant opened, it bought road tankers and took its present name.

Operations

The company holds the federal permit to carry dangerous goods by road in tanks. Its tankers carry the propane Kelpstone Gas recovers at its plant to customers in the south, among them the Coastal State Water Company, which burns it in the peak boilers of Lumeport's district heating in winter. A tanker is weighed on the plant's weighbridge empty and full, drives the day to Lumeport, about 515 km, pumps the propane into the customer's storage and returns empty the next day. Its planning desk works around the clock.

Fleet

The haulier runs twelve road tankers; besides the water company's heating plant it delivers propane to farms and homes across the dry east and the north, for heating and for grain dryers. Its tractor units pull pressure tank semi-trailers approved for liquefied flammable gas. One such tank holds 49 m³ and up to 20,500 kg; with about 20 t of propane the loaded vehicle weighs about 37 t, within the Accord's limit of 40 t.
