Selvana

River of Urvania

Selvana

The Selvana and its basin; dots: towns
CountryUrvania
Length91 km
Basin1,849 km²
Sourceabout 1,020 m
MouthRunmek
NamesSelvana (Italian) · Huvir (Urvanian)

The Selvana, a river of Urvania 91 km long, drains a basin of 1,849 km². From its source at about 1,020 m it joins the Runmek. Its Urvanian name, Huvir, means forest.[1][2]
