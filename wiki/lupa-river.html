Lupa (river)

The Lupa and its basin; dots: towns
CountryUrvania
Length94 km
Basin1,511 km²
Sourceabout 860 m
MouthBruna
NamesLupa (Italian) · Rezhir (Urvanian)

The Lupa is a river of Urvania, 94 km long, with a basin of 1,511 km². It rises at about 860 m and joins the Bruna. Its Urvanian name, Rezhir, means wolf.[1][2]
