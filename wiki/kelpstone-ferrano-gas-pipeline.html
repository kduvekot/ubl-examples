Kelpstone–Ferrano gas pipeline

Gas pipeline from Bayvarra's north coast to Urvania

Kelpstone–Ferrano gas pipeline
KindPipeline
Lengthabout 207 km
CountriesBayvarra, Urvania
Opened2011

The Kelpstone–Ferrano gas pipeline carries natural gas from the gas plant of Kelpstone Gas near Gullhaven, on Bayvarra's north coast, across the border to the power station near Serravona in Urvania. It has carried gas since 2011.[1][2]

  1. Route
  2. See also
  3. References

Route

The gas comes ashore from an offshore field by a subsea line of about 83 km, from the production platform to the plant of Kelpstone Gas near Gullhaven. From the plant the pipeline runs about 207 km to Montebrio Power; a branch of about 97 km leads to the city gate of Ferrano Grid. A metering station on Bayvarra's side of the border measures the gas that crosses into Urvania.
