Lumeport Textile Mill

Cotton spinning, weaving and clothing mill in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Lumeport Textile Mill spins and weaves the cotton of Lisavu into fabric and makes clothing in Lumeport, Bayvarra. It was founded in 1976 and employs about 190 people.

History

The mill was built in 1976 to spin the island's cotton, which the Lumeport Agreement of 1974 let into Bayvarra free of duty. The Mill Quarter of Lumeport grew up around it.

Operations

The mill buys its lint from Selanu Cotton Ginnery on the island. Before each harvest it tells the ginnery how many bales it expects to buy; it then orders the bales free on board at Selanu, has Bayforth Forwarding book the sea freight, and clears them itself on arrival in Lumeport. It makes shirts under its own brand, Lumeport, and sells them to Brightmarket Stores, delivered to the store. Its garment bags come from Rivermouth Packaging. The mill numbers its products in the GS1 system, under its own company prefix.
