Brackenmere Furniture

Furniture maker in Marrowdale, Bayvarra

Brackenmere Furniture makes wooden furniture in its workshop on Joiners Row in Marrowdale, Bayvarra. Founded in 2000, it supplies the department store Brightmarket Stores and furnishes public offices, installation included; it employs about 45 people.

History

The company takes its name from the carriage works at Brackenmere, one of Bayvarra's railway and coast towns, whose furniture shop also made benches and desks for stations and offices. When the works closed in 1999, the company took over the furniture's name and drawings and set up a new workshop in Marrowdale in 2000, beside the sawmill's timber.

Operations

Brackenmere works the timber of Marrowdale Sawmill, which it collects at the sawmill's yard with its own flatbed truck and loader crane. For Brightmarket it makes the Elderwick Marrow dining bench and chair in oak and in ash, to the store's specification and under the store's label, and delivers them in its van. Under its own name it sells an office desk, the park bench PB-2 and the furnishing of public offices with installation, as for the Lumeport Public Works Department. It tenders for public contracts, among them the city's park benches, where it competes with Valdara Industries. Its workshop has about 3,000 m² of floor; besides the Marrow line of home furniture it makes office desks and chairs and library shelving.

Design

With Saltmere Engineering it forms a design team whose references are the reading garden of Lumeport's central library and the promenade benches on Larkmouth's seafront. Led by Brackenmere's own designer, the team won the first prize in the city's design contest for the bench of the harbour promenade in Lumeport.
