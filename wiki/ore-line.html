Ore line

Railway in Urvania from the mine to the works at Ferrano

Ore line
KindRailway
Lengthabout 166 km
CountryUrvania

The Ore line is a railway in Urvania that carries iron ore from the mine on the lower flank of the massif down to the works at Ferrano. It is about 166 km long.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Route
  2. Operation
  3. See also
  4. References

Route

The line starts at the mine's siding at Fucinalta, where the wagons are loaded and weighed at the mine's portal, and follows the valleys down to Ferrano, keeping to gradients of at most 2 %. Its river bridge crosses a deep river valley about 100 m above the water.

Operation

The tracks belong to the Rail Infrastructure Agency of Urvania, which allocates the train paths and dispatches every train; Ferrano Rail Cargo runs the ore trains.
