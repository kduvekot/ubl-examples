Rivermouth Packaging

Bayvarran maker of packaging from recycled plastic

Rivermouth Packaging

Rivermouth Packaging is a maker of packaging from recycled plastic on Estuary Road in Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 2005. It makes stretch film and garment bags and employs about 23 people.[1][2]

History

Rivermouth began as the packaging department of the Lumeport Textile Mill. In 2005 it became a company of its own, so that it could sell to other makers too, Valdara Industries first, and set up at the river quay on Estuary Road, where the barges bring the recycled granulate of Rinova Recovery.

Operations

Rivermouth buys recycled polyethylene granulate from Rinova Recovery in Ferrano, which Fiumella Barges carries about 250 km down the river and the estuary to Rivermouth's river quay. From it Rivermouth makes stretch film for wrapping pallets, which Trasvia Haulage trucks to Valdara Industries to wrap its bench pallets, and garment bags for the shirts of the Lumeport Textile Mill, which Bayforth Forwarding delivers. It makes about 1,800 t of film and bags a year; its customers also include Harrowmead Fruit Growers, for the liners of its fruit cartons, and Brightmarket Stores, for its shop bags.

Identification

Although it is Bayvarran, Rivermouth registered with Urvania's Codana Identification Agency in February 2006, because its main customer, Valdara, works in that system.
