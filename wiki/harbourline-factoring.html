Harbourline Factoring

Factoring company in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Harbourline Factoring is a factoring company in the Old Town of Lumeport, Bayvarra, founded in 1996 to finance cross-border receivables. It employs about 21 people.[1][2]

History

Harbourline was founded in 1996, the year the Lumeport Trade Accord entered into force, by the Bank of Lumeport and Wavesure Cargo Insurance together. Exporters selling across the new border wanted their invoices paid before their buyers paid them; the bank brought the money and Wavesure the cover against debts that go unpaid, and the two still own the company.

Operations

Harbourline buys the receivables of Lumeway Distribution and collects them: Lumeway's customers, among them Brightmarket Stores in Lumeport and Velumi Trading on Lisavu, pay the factor instead of the distributor, less any credit Lumeway has granted them. It keeps its account at the Bank of Lumeport and works from Exchange Street. Besides Lumeway it factors the receivables of about 30 small and mid-size wholesalers and makers of Bayvarra that sell to Urvania and the island.
