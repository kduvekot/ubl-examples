River Barle

The Barle and its basin; dots: towns
CountryBayvarra
Length105 km
Basin1,662 km²
Sourceabout 570 m
MouthTallow

The Barle, a river of Bayvarra 105 km long, drains a basin of 1,662 km². From its source at about 570 m it joins the Tallow.[1][2]
