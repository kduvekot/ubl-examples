Federal Department of Agriculture of Bayvarra

Bayvarra's federal department of agriculture and plant health

Federal Department of Agriculture of Bayvarra
SeatLumeport, Bayvarra
Reports toits own federal minister

The Federal Department of Agriculture of Bayvarra is Bayvarra's federal government department for agriculture, on Civic Square on Government Hill in Lumeport. Its Plant Health Service is the country's plant protection organisation.

Contents
  1. History
  2. Operations
  3. See also

History

The department was set up in 1936, after the drought years in the dry east. The federation took over plant health and the certificates for exports; advice to farms stays with the states. The department is headed by its own federal minister.

Operations

The Plant Health Service inspects plants and plant products for export and issues their phytosanitary certificates. For fresh fruit bound for Lisavu it inspects the fruit at the packhouse before loading, on the exporter's application, and certifies it free of codling moth and fire blight, the two pests the island, which has neither, keeps out; the apples and pears of Harrowmead Fruit Growers for Hafefo Fresh Produce are certified so.
