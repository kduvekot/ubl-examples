River of Urvania

Nevola

The Nevola and its basin; dots: towns

NamesNevola (Italian) · Kutek (Urvanian)

The Nevola rises at about 1,920 m in Urvania and joins the Runmek after 184 km; its basin covers 6,323 km². Its Urvanian name, Kutek, means snow.[1][2]

Hydro scheme

In the massif a dam holds the river in the Lago della Nevola, and a power station of about 300 MW below it, built from 1956 to 1961, gives Urvania about half its electricity in a normal year.
