Valdara Industries

Urvanian maker of park benches and street furniture

Valdara Industries is a maker of park benches and street furniture in Ferrano, Urvania, founded in 1962, the year of Urvania's independence. It casts its frames in its own foundry and employs about 3,083 people.

History

Valdara was founded in 1962 as one of the manufacturers of Urvania's protected economy. When the Lumeport Trade Accord entered into force in 1996, it made Lumeway Distribution its distributor for Bayvarra and Lisavu, which is why its benches reach the island through Lumeport.

Products

Its best-known product is the park bench Serra 180: frames of ductile iron, cast in its foundry from the mine's ore and about 35 % steel and iron scrap, with slats from the sawmill in Marrowdale, packed in recycled packaging from Bayvarra.

Trade

Valdara sells to its distributor abroad and tenders in public procurement; it buys ore from Serravona Mining and timber from Marrowdale Sawmill.
