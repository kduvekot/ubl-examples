Brightmarket Stores

Department store in Lumeport, Bayvarra

Brightmarket Stores, trading as Brightmarket, is a department store company in Lumeport, Bayvarra, with its flagship store on Market Street in the Old Town. Founded in 1979, it sells clothing, home and garden goods to consumers in its stores and its web shop, and employs about 2,900 people.

History

Brightmarket grew from a draper's shop in the Old Town of Lumeport and became a company of department stores in 1979; besides the flagship on Market Street it has seven stores in the towns of Bayvarra. Around 1981 Brightmarket and other retailers wanted bar codes at the till, and so began the article numbering association that is now GS1 Bayvarra. Brightmarket still numbers its own-label goods and its places from its GS1 company prefix.

Operations

The store buys from Lumeway Distribution, among other things the park bench Serra 180 of Valdara Industries, and shares its forecasts, promotions and stock levels with it. It sells the shirts of the Lumeport Textile Mill under the mill's own brand, Lumeport. In 2026 it renovated the home and garden department of its Lumeport store, which reopened on Monday 11 May with two weeks at 10 % off. The store's van delivers furniture to customers' homes. Besides clothing, home and garden goods it sells toys, kitchenware and stationery. The flagship on Market Street has about 12,000 m² of floor, and the web shop opened in 2008.

Elderwick

Elderwick is Brightmarket's own label for home and garden. It began in 1989 with bed and bath linen, towels, cushions and curtains; garden tools and plant pots followed in 1996, and the Marrow line of 2026 is its first furniture. Its Marrow dining bench and chair, in oak or in ash, are made to the store's specification by Brackenmere Furniture in Marrowdale; the maker's name is on no label. The store sells the bench at 549 lume and the chair at 199, gives a care kit with each bench, and offers a set of a bench and two chairs.
