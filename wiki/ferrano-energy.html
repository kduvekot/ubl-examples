Ferrano Energy

Urvania's electricity and gas supplier

Ferrano Energy

Ferrano Energy supplies electricity and gas to the homes and businesses of Urvania, from its head office on Corso della Repubblica in Ferrano. Founded in 1949, it buys the energy, pays the grid for its networks and bills its customers; it employs about 640 people.[1][2]

History

The British administration set up Ferrano Energy in 1949, after the war, to bring electricity to the valley; its first great work was the Nevola hydro scheme in the massif, built from 1956 to 1961. At independence in 1962 it passed to the new state and stayed a state company through the years of the protected economy. When Urvania opened its economy in the early 1990s, the state sold a minority of the shares on the Ferrano Stock Exchange and kept the majority. The Trade Accord's energy protocol of 2007 asked for networks to be run apart from supply, and Urvania chose the full split: Ferrano Energy's networks became Ferrano Grid that year, and its power stations went to a state generating company. Until 2011 Ferrano Energy sold only electricity; when Kelpstone Gas began producing and the pipeline brought gas to Urvania, it took up gas as a second business.

Operations

Under the Lumeport Trade Accord's chapter on public services, the supply stays national, and Ferrano Energy is Urvania's supplier. It buys electricity from the state generating company, which runs the Nevola hydro scheme, the run-of-river plants and the Saline Brune solar park, from Montebrio Power, from the roofs of homes and farms, and from Bayvarra on the Accord's wholesale market, and gas from Kelpstone Gas, which it imports itself across the border. Ferrano Grid carries the energy and reads the meters, and Ferrano Energy collects the grid's network charges with its own bill: of its tariff of 27.3 ferra a kilowatt hour, 21.1 is for the energy and 6.2 for the network. A business is billed every month on its meter reading; households pay instalments on account each month and are settled once a year, after the annual reading at the end of March. Its customers include Valdara Industries. It supplies about 520,000 electricity customers across Urvania, about 470,000 households and 50,000 businesses, and about 90,000 gas customers in the Province of Ferrano, where Ferrano Grid has gas mains.
