Faggiorna is a small town in Urvania, with about 3,410 people.

Contents
  1. Geography
  2. History
  3. Economy
  4. See also

Geography

High on the wet western hills, on a shelf below the beech woods where two streams meet.

History

It was founded by Urvanians before 1600.

Economy

It lives from dairy cattle and hay meadows, beech wood and charcoal.
