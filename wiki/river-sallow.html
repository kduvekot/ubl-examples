River Sallow

River of Bayvarra

River Sallow

The Sallow and its basin; dots: towns
CountryBayvarra
Length198 km
Basin6,294 km²
Sourceabout 460 m
Mouththe sea, on the north-east coast
TributariesReedwater, Linnet

The Sallow, a river of Bayvarra 198 km long, drains a basin of 6,294 km². From its source at about 460 m it reaches the sea on the north-east coast.[1][2]
