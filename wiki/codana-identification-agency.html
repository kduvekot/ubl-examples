Codana Identification Agency

Urvania's public numbering agency for companies and products

Codana Identification Agency
SeatFerrano, Urvania
Founded bythe state
Reports tothe Ministry of Industry

The Codana Identification Agency is Urvania's public numbering body, seated in Ferrano, Urvania. It was founded in 1999, when Urvanian makers began to sell across the Accord's borders. It gives companies a company number under which they number their own products, packages and places.

History

Codana was set up by the state in 1999, when Urvanian makers began to sell across the Accord's borders and their buyers asked for products and packages numbered in a way every partner could read. Its earliest members include Valdara Industries, registered in November 1999, and Montebrio Power, registered in December 1999. It reports to Urvania's Ministry of Industry.

Timeline

  • 1999: set up by the state, as Urvanian makers begin to sell across the Accord's borders
  • 8 November 1999: registers Valdara Industries, among its earliest members
  • 6 December 1999: registers Montebrio Power
  • 13 February 2006: registers Rivermouth Packaging of Bayvarra, whose main customer works in its system
  • 8 October 2012: registers Kelpstone Gas of Bayvarra, whose customers are Urvanian

Operations

Codana is an issuing agency under ISO/IEC 15459, with the issuing agency code KXU, and keeps the organisation register behind the ISO/IEC 6523 code 0990. It gives each company a five-digit number, in order of registration; the company prefix is KXU followed by that number. Codana hands out no item numbers: each company numbers its own items, lots, pallets and places under its prefix, and Codana's rule book keeps every identifier to 20 characters or fewer. Codana registers any company that asks, foreign ones too: Rivermouth Packaging and Kelpstone Gas of Bayvarra are members because their customers in Urvania use the system.

Members

Its members include Valdara Industries, Montebrio Power, Serravona Mining, Castrano Logistics, Rinova Recovery, Rivermouth Packaging, Ferrano Grid and Kelpstone Gas. It is Urvania's counterpart to GS1 Bayvarra, the association that licenses GS1 company prefixes in Bayvarra and on Lisavu. The agency has a staff of about 25 and is funded by its members' yearly fees.
