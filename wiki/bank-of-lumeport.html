Bank of Lumeport is a bank of Bayvarra, with its head office at Exchange House on Exchange Street in the Old Town of Lumeport. Founded in 1903, it holds the accounts of companies and households, makes their payments and issues guarantees, and employs about 2,313 people.

History

The bank was founded in 1903, when Bayvarra was still a British colony: the country's settler economy is older than Urvania's state.

Operations

Besides its main branch it has a harbour branch in Lumeport and branches in Marrowdale and in Gullhaven on the north coast. Its customers include Lumeway Distribution, Brightmarket Stores, Brackenmere Furniture, Marrowdale Sawmill, Lumeport Textile Mill, Kelpstone Gas, the Coastal State Water Company and the Lumeport Public Works Department. It issues tender guarantees: for Valdara Industries it gave a bank guarantee on the counter-guarantee of Valdara's own bank, Crevalto Bank, and for Brackenmere it certified government bonds pledged to the buyer. Valdara also keeps a lume account with it for its contract with the Lumeport Public Works Department. Payments run over Bayvarra's clearing or, to the other two countries, over the Trade Accord's payment link. Its customers pay with the Lumecard, the debit card that Bayvarra's banks run together, and with Tressa, the credit card used across the Accord.
