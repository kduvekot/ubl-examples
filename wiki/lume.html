Lume

Currency of Bayvarra

Lume
CodeXBL
CountryBayvarra
Minor unit1/100

The lume is the currency of Bayvarra, divided into a hundred parts.[1][2]

Contents
  1. Exchange
  2. See also
  3. References

Exchange

The lume is the anchor of the Lumeport Trade Accord's fixed parity, in force since 1996: one lume is worth a hundred ferra or four tenths of a coral.
