How UBL helps

An intermodal terminal is where rail and road meet. UBL status messages and transport reports let it tell carriers and forwarders when a container arrived, was transferred or left, using the same equipment and consignment identifiers on both sides of the transfer.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Sends

  • Transport Progress Status to Ferrano Rail Cargo: The hub, as network manager, reports the train's position and timetable. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty.

  • Transportation Status to Ferrano Rail Cargo: The intermodal terminal reports to the rail carrier that the containers are loaded on the train. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/MainCarriageShipmentStage/TerminalOperatorParty.

Receives

  • Transport Progress Status Request from Ferrano Rail Cargo: The rail carrier asks the hub, which manages the tracks and slots, where the container train stands. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.
