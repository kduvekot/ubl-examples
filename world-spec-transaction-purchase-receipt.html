Purchase receipt

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:purchase-receipt:1.0

One document type carries the transaction, the Purchase Receipt, which UBL 2.5 defines as follows: “A receipt for a purchase made with cash or cash equivalents.”

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.
