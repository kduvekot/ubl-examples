In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

A customs declarant files declarations for importers and can act as their tax representative. UBL gives it the commercial, transport and customs data in one vocabulary, so it can assemble the declaration from documents it already receives.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys 104 benches, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock, since it buys by the pallet and two pallets add little to the freight of a container already booked. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, are CIF prices: they hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor pays to its freight forwarder together with the forwarder's truck to the port, and the invoice bills the goods alone. CIF stays as common practice and as the flow's theme, although the ICC's guidance note to CIF says that where goods are handed over to a carrier at a container terminal, the appropriate rule is CIP rather than CIF (Incoterms 2020; D30, O105). The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Import Customs Declaration to Island Customs Service: Under CIF the buyer clears the import; its licensed customs broker files the declaration on its behalf. See the example document.

    In it as NotifierParty, on behalf of Velumi Trading (ImporterParty).

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as ImporterParty; Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as ConsigneeParty; Bayforth Forwarding as FreightForwarderParty.

Medicines and a technetium generator for the island's hospital, by air (cold chain and radioactive material)

Selanu General Hospital orders the vaccines for the island's winter influenza campaign, insulin, a frozen varicella vaccine and its weekly technetium-99m generator from a pharmaceutical wholesaler in Lumeport, carriage paid to Selanu airport. The forwarder books the morning flight of Monday 15 June: the vaccines and the insulin fly at 2 to 8 °C in the airline's active temperature-controlled container, the frozen vaccine in an insulated box on dry ice, and the generator, a Type A package of radioactive material with a Yellow label, under a shipper's declaration of its own, in the aft hold. The wholesaler's own van brings the generator to the cargo terminal at 05:30. On the island the customs broker declares the import for the hospital, free of duty and exempt from the import consumption tax, and the hospital pharmacy's van fetches the goods.

Sends

  • Import Customs Declaration to Island Customs Service: The customs broker, as the hospital's direct representative, declares the import at Selanu airport in the hospital's name: no duty on medicines, and the exemption from the import consumption tax for medicines supplied to a public hospital (O201, O203). See the example document.

    In it as NotifierParty, on behalf of Selanu General Hospital (ImporterParty).

    Also mentions: Selanu General Hospital as ImporterParty; Quillmoor Pharma Distribution as ConsignorParty; Selanu General Hospital as ConsigneeParty.
