In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The island's seaport discharges the ship. It receives the same UBL transport report the loading port sent, so it can plan berths and inform customs before the vessel arrives.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Transportation Status to Bayforth Forwarding: The port of discharge reports the container discharged and available. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/MainCarriageShipmentStage/TerminalOperatorParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Consignment/CarrierParty; Velumi Trading as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Common Transportation Report from Tidewell Ocean Lines to Maritime Administration of Lisavu: The ship flies the island's flag; before entering port the carrier reports to the maritime administration, which also issued the ship's security certificate. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/ShipmentStage/TerminalOperatorParty (A terminal operator associated with this shipment stage).

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Is named in documents between others

  • Weight Statement from Selanu Cotton Ginnery to Tidewell Ocean Lines: Before loading, the shipper declares the verified gross mass of the container, weighed on the port's weighbridge. See the example document.

    In it as WeighingParty (The Party who executes the weight measure (e.g. weighing station)).
