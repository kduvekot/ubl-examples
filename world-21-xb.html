How UBL helps

A government information service publishes procurement notices and keeps the business registry. UBL notices reach it in one structured format, and business information documents let it share registry data that tenderers can reuse as evidence.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Sends

  • Business Information to Lumeport Public Works Department: The authority checks the tenderer's registration with the business registry.

    In it as SenderParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as BusinessParty.

Receives

  • Prior Information Notice from Lumeport Public Works Department: The authority announces the planned purchase early, through the publication service.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

  • Contract Notice from Lumeport Public Works Department: The call for competition is published; it names the review body for appeals and mediation.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty; Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/MediationParty.

  • Contract Award Notice from Lumeport Public Works Department: The award is published; the review body is named for appeals.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as TenderResult/WinningParty; Procurement Review Board of Bayvarra as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.
