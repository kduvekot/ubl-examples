#21 Government Information Service

Country B (XB) · Government

Publishes procurement notices and keeps the business registry.

How UBL helps

A government information service publishes procurement notices and keeps the business registry. UBL notices reach it in one structured format, and business information documents let it share registry data that tenderers can reuse as evidence.

Documents it exchanges

  • PriorInformationNotice
  • BusinessInformation
