Invoice rules of Urvania

National specification in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:XU:invoice:1.0

Urvania's rules for the invoices of its value added tax: the seller's VAT number, the tax category and rate per line, and the bilingual notes of an Urvanian sender. An invoice to a public body of Bayvarra claims that country's rules for public buyers instead; its tax is still Urvania's.

Owner: Government of Urvania (tax administration). It applies to Invoice, CreditNote from a seller in Urvania, on top of the Trade Accord's transaction, and is chained after it.

Document types: Invoice, CreditNote.

Examples that claim it

  • Invoice, Ferrano Grid to Ferrano Energy (Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania))
  • Invoice, Montebrio Power to Ferrano Energy (Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania))
  • Invoice, Tostara Wood Modification to Marrowdale Sawmill (Modification: bench slats modified under contract (Bayvarra → Urvania))
  • Invoice, Ferrano Rail Cargo to Valdara Industries (Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania))
  • Invoice, Serravona Mining to Valdara Industries (Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania))
  • Invoice, Fiumella Barges to Rinova Recovery (Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra))
  • Invoice, Rinova Recovery to Rivermouth Packaging (Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra))
  • Invoice, Ferrano Rail Cargo to Valdara Industries (Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra))
  • Invoice, Valdara Industries to Lumeway Distribution (Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra))
  • Invoice, Trasvia Haulage to Lumeway Distribution (Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra))
