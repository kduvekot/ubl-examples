Invoice rules of Urvania

National specification in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:XU:invoice:1.0

Urvania's rules for the invoices of its value added tax, modelled on the EU VAT directive: the seller's VAT number, the tax category and rate per line, the 2 % cash discount for payment within ten days, statutory interest on late payment, and the bilingual notes of an Urvanian sender.

Owner: Government of Urvania (tax administration). It applies to Invoice, CreditNote from a seller in Urvania, on top of the Trade Accord's transaction, and is chained after it.

Document types: Invoice, CreditNote.
