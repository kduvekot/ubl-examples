How UBL helps

The ocean carrier issues the bill of lading, the document that represents the goods at sea. With UBL that document, the vessel reports and the status updates share the same consignment and container references as the forwarder's booking.

In this world it sends 8 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 6 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys 104 benches, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock, since it buys by the pallet and two pallets add little to the freight of a container already booked. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, are CIF prices: they hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor pays to its freight forwarder together with the forwarder's truck to the port, and the invoice bills the goods alone. CIF stays as common practice and as the flow's theme, although the ICC's guidance note to CIF says that where goods are handed over to a carrier at a container terminal, the appropriate rule is CIP rather than CIF (Incoterms 2020; D30, O105). The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs every bale for the container's verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

  • Bill of Lading to Bayforth Forwarding: The bill of lading goes to the forwarder that booked the freight for the mill; the ginnery is the shipper. See the example document.

    In it as CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Cotton Ginnery as ConsignorParty; Lumeport Textile Mill as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Common Transportation Report to Port of Lumeport: Before entering Lumeport the carrier reports its second feeder to the port. The ship came under Bayvarra's flag in January, so it sails with an interim security certificate, and it carries a sanitation control exemption certificate, as the inspection at Lumeport in January found no control measures needed. See the example document.

Apples and pears in a reefer container by sea (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

In the southern autumn the island's fresh-produce wholesaler buys a 40-foot reefer of apples and pears from the fruit growers' cooperative at Harrowmead, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu (CIP): the cooperative books the reefer through its forwarder, which brings it pre-cooled to the packhouse and trucks it back to Lumeport on a generator; the plant health service of Bayvarra's Department of Agriculture certifies the fruit free of the pests the island names. The reefer sails at 0.5 °C and 90 % humidity, its fresh-air vent set for fruit that breathes, on Tidewell's weekly service; at Selanu it waits on the port's power until the wholesaler's carrier takes it to the cold store, where it is unloaded and its temperature log read. Food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import: the island taxes imported food to encourage its own farms.

Sends

  • Bill of Lading to Bayforth Forwarding: The bill of lading of the reefer, issued to the forwarder that booked it, with the reefer's settings as the carrier is to keep them. See the example document.

    In it as CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Harrowmead Fruit Growers as ConsignorParty; Hafefo Fresh Produce as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transportation Status to Hafefo Fresh Produce: The carrier reports the reefer discharged at Selanu and on the port's power, with the temperatures its monitoring recorded at sea. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

Is named in documents between others

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).
