#11 Ocean Carrier

Country B (XB) · Logistics

Sails the container ship to the island and issues the negotiable bill of lading.

How UBL helps

The ocean carrier issues the bill of lading, the document that represents the goods at sea. With UBL that document, the vessel reports and the status updates share the same consignment and container references as the forwarder's booking.

Documents it exchanges

  • BillOfLading
  • Waybill
  • CommonTransportationReport

UBL roles

The party elements in which this party appears:

  • CarrierParty
  • ReporterParty
