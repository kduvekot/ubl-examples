In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states, and the federal territory in the north, levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer. Sales to businesses that resell, exports and sales to public bodies are exempt, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The ocean carrier issues the bill of lading, the document that represents the goods at sea. With UBL that document, the vessel reports and the status updates share the same consignment and container references as the forwarder's booking.

In this world it sends 6 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 5 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Transport Service Description to Lumeway Distribution: The ocean carrier describes its weekly service to Selanu. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

  • Bill of Lading to Bayforth Forwarding: The negotiable bill of lading, issued to the forwarder that gave the instructions; it names the insurer of the cargo and the buyer's 3PL as party to notify on arrival. See the example document.

    In it as CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Nalevi Warehousing as Shipment/Consignment/NotifyParty; Wavesure Cargo Insurance as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Manifest to Island Customs Service: Before arrival the carrier lists the cargo on board for the island's customs. See the example document.

    In it as SendingLogisticsOperatorParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as ConsigneeParty.

  • Common Transportation Report to Maritime Administration of Lisavu: Before entering Selanu the carrier reports to the island's maritime administration, the port state authority there. The ship flies Bayvarra's flag, so its security certificate was issued by Bayvarra's maritime administration, the flag state. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, ReporterParty.

    Also mentions: Port of Selanu as Shipment/ShipmentStage/TerminalOperatorParty.

Receives

  • Transport Service Description Request from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor asks the ocean carrier for its sailings to the island. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

  • Forwarding Instructions from Bayforth Forwarding: The forwarder, now acting as shipper, books the container with the ocean carrier. See the example document.

    In it as CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transportation Status from Port of Lumeport to Bayforth Forwarding: The port of loading reports the container loaded on board. See the example document.

    In it as Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

  • Transportation Status from Port of Selanu to Bayforth Forwarding: The port of discharge reports the container discharged and available. See the example document.

    In it as Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice from Bayforth Forwarding to Lumeway Distribution: The forwarder invoices what it arranged for the distributor: sea freight, export clearance and the cargo insurance premium (CIF obliges the seller to insure). See the example document.

    In it as Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution to Nalevi Warehousing: The distributor announces the shipment to the delivery party named in the order, the buyer's 3PL. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as BuyerCustomerParty.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

  • Bill of Lading to Bayforth Forwarding: The bill of lading goes to the forwarder that booked the freight for the mill; the ginnery is the shipper. See the example document.

    In it as CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Cotton Ginnery as ConsignorParty; Lumeport Textile Mill as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Common Transportation Report to Port of Lumeport: Before entering Lumeport the carrier reports its second feeder to the port. The ship came under Bayvarra's flag in January, so it sails with an interim security certificate, and it carries a sanitation control exemption certificate, as the inspection at Lumeport in January found no control measures needed. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, ReporterParty.

Receives

  • Weight Statement from Selanu Cotton Ginnery: Before loading, the shipper declares the verified gross mass of the container, weighed on the port's weighbridge. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Port of Selanu as WeighingParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Bayforth Forwarding to Lumeport Textile Mill: The forwarder invoices the sea freight it booked for the mill. See the example document.

    In it as Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).
