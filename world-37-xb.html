In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer. Sales to businesses that resell, exports and sales to public bodies are exempt, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

A supplier to a retailer shares more than orders: sales per store, stock policies and agreed exception thresholds. UBL's retail collaboration documents carry them, and a Work Report documents installation work for the customer to approve.

In this world it sends 9 and receives 7 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

The sawmill in Upland State saws timber to size. The manufacturer in Urvania asks for a quotation for bench slats to its own dimensions and orders them delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill arranges the truck across the border. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Sends

  • Order to Marrowdale Sawmill: Timber for furniture, collected with the furniture maker's own van at the sawmill. Delivery terms EXW sawmill.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

Receives

  • Invoice from Marrowdale Sawmill: Timber sold for manufacture: exempt from the state sales tax, and the invoice says so.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Furniture for the department store and for public offices (Bayvarra)

The furniture maker supplies the department store with a garden furniture line made for it. They agree on the item data and the stock policy per store, the store reports what it sells, and exceptions against agreed thresholds are flagged automatically. Slow sellers are taken back. For the public works department the furniture maker also installs, and documents the work for approval.

Sends

  • Quotation to Brightmarket Stores: The furniture maker quotes the line.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Item Information Request to Brightmarket Stores: To plan production, the furniture maker asks the store for sales and stock data of the line.

    In it as SenderParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Exception Notification to Brightmarket Stores: Sales of the bench set run far below forecast: past the agreed threshold, so the furniture maker notifies the store.

    In it as SenderParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Invoice to Brightmarket Stores: Furniture sold for resale: exempt from the state sales tax, which the store charges its consumers.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Instruction For Returns to Brightmarket Stores: The bench set does not sell; the furniture maker asks for the remaining stock back to free shelf space.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty, ManufacturerParty.

  • Credit Note to Brightmarket Stores: The returned bench sets are credited.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Work Report to Lumeport Public Works Department: The installers report the work done; the department approves it.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeport Public Works Department: Furniture and installation; a sale to a public body is exempt from sales tax, and the invoice says so.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Request For Quotation from Brightmarket Stores: The store wants its own garden furniture line and asks for a quotation.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Trade Item Location Profile from Brightmarket Stores: The store sets the replenishment policy per item and store.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Exception Criteria from Brightmarket Stores: Both agree when a deviation is worth a message: thresholds for forecast variance and sales.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Product Activity from Brightmarket Stores: Weekly sales and stock movements of the line.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

  • Order from Brightmarket Stores: The store orders the line. Delivery terms DAP store: the furniture maker delivers with its own van.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Order from Lumeport Public Works Department: Furniture for the public works offices, installed. Delivery terms DAP the offices.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.
