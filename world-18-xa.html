How UBL helps

An intermodal terminal is where rail and road meet. UBL status messages and transport reports let it tell carriers and forwarders when a container arrived, was transferred or left, using the same equipment and consignment identifiers on both sides of the transfer.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Containers by rail under transit (Country A → Country B)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Sends

  • Transportation Status to Ferrano Rail Cargo: The intermodal terminal reports to the rail carrier that the containers are loaded on the train.
