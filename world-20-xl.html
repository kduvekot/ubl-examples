Selanu Chamber of Commerce

#20 Chamber of Commerce (XL) · Lisavu (XL) · Government

Guarantees ATA carnets on the import side.

Contact Carnet office, carnet@chamber-selanu.example
In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The chamber of commerce on the island guarantees goods-item passports for goods that enter temporarily, so customs can accept them without duties.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Sends

  • Proof of Reexportation Request to Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: The island's chamber has no re-export stamp for the samples and asks for proof that they left. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

  • Proof of Reexportation Reminder to Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: No answer after a month: a reminder. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

Receives

  • Proof of Reexportation from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: Urvania's chamber proves the samples were re-exported: they came home through Urvania's customs. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Goods Item Passport from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce to Valdara Industries: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingGuarantorParty (The Party who provides a guarantee for the Goods while being temporarily imported. This Party is normally a chamber of commerce).

    Also mentions: Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty; Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

  • Goods Certificate to Selanu Cotton Ginnery: The island's chamber of commerce certifies the bales for export, at the ginnery's application. See the example document.

    In it as IssuerParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Textile Mill as ImporterParty.
