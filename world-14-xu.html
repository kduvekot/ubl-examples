How UBL helps

A rail carrier plans capacity on a fixed timetable. UBL transport documents let its customers request and confirm transport plans and receive status updates per train and wagon, with the same consignment references the forwarder and the receiving terminal use.

In this world it sends 8 and receives 4 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

The manufacturer buys ore free carrier (FCA) at the mine: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so the manufacturer, not the mine, books the ore train with the rail carrier.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan to Valdara Industries: The rail carrier confirms the train, the wagons and the schedule; this is the agreed transport plan. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

  • Waybill to Valdara Industries: The rail consignment note goes to the party that contracted the carriage, the manufacturer; the mine is named as consignor because the ore is loaded there. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as ConsignorParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: Under FCA the buyer pays the carriage: the rail carrier invoices the ore freight to the manufacturer, per tonne. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer, as transport user, asks the rail carrier for wagons and a slot to move the ore from the mine to its siding. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Serravona Mining as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order from Valdara Industries to Serravona Mining: Monthly ore call-off. Delivery terms FCA mine: the buyer books the train, so the order names the rail carrier that will collect. See the example document.

    In it as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

  • Despatch Advice from Serravona Mining to Valdara Industries: The mine announces what was loaded into which wagons, so the factory can plan the unloading. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan to Valdara Industries: The rail carrier confirms the train and the slot at the intermodal hub. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transport Progress Status Request to Ferrano Intermodal Hub: The rail carrier asks the hub, which manages the tracks and slots, where the container train stands. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty.

  • Waybill to Valdara Industries: The rail consignment note goes to the transport user, now the seller. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Goods Item Itinerary to Valdara Industries: The carrier tells the transport user the route and timing of the containers. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportationSegment/TransportServiceProviderParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: Under DAP the seller pays the carriage: the rail carrier invoices the container slots to the manufacturer. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Valdara Industries: Under DAP the manufacturer is the transport user; it books a container train to the distributor's siding. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transport Progress Status from Ferrano Intermodal Hub: The hub, as network manager, reports the train's position and timetable. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

  • Transportation Status from Ferrano Intermodal Hub: The intermodal terminal reports to the rail carrier that the containers are loaded on the train. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transit Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Urvania: The goods travel under customs transit from Urvania to the destination in Bayvarra; the manufacturer, as holder of the procedure, lodges the declaration at the office of departure. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsigneeParty.
