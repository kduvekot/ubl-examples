Element coverage

The endorsed elements of UBL 2.5 that the documents of the world use: every document type, aggregate and basic element the UBL 2.5 model allows, without what it deprecates. An element is added only through a real business situation in the world, never as a filler, so some stay out on purpose. Every missing element is listed with where it fits.

Counted: only what UBL 2.5 endorses and does not deprecate. Left out:

  • aggregates no endorsed document reaches (PerformanceDataLine): 6
  • aggregates no endorsed document reaches (StockAvailabilityReportLine): 7
  • deprecated document types and aggregates: 9
  • deprecated properties: 81
