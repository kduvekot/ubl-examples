#5 Manufacturer

Country A (XA) · Commerce

Turns raw material into finished goods; buys from #4, sells to #6 abroad and tenders in public procurement.

How UBL helps

The manufacturer sits in the middle of the chain: it buys, produces, sells, exports and tenders. UBL gives it one vocabulary for all of that, from the order it places with the mine to the certificate of origin and the tender it submits to a public buyer, so the same item, party and price data are reused instead of re-entered for every partner.

In this world it sends 14 and receives 15 documents, and is named in 10 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Country A)

The manufacturer buys ore free carrier (FCA) at the mine: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so the manufacturer, not the mine, books the ore train with the rail carrier.

Sends

  • Order to #4 Raw Material Supplier: Monthly ore call-off. Delivery terms FCA mine: the buyer books the train, so the order names the rail carrier that will collect.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

    Also mentions: #14 Rail Carrier as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to #14 Rail Carrier: The manufacturer, as transport user, asks the rail carrier for wagons and a slot to move the ore from the mine to its siding.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: #4 Raw Material Supplier as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

  • Remittance Advice to #4 Raw Material Supplier: The manufacturer tells the mine which invoices the transfer pays. Both companies bank at the same domestic bank.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #23 Seller's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; #23 Seller's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan from #14 Rail Carrier: The rail carrier confirms the train, the wagons and the schedule; this is the agreed transport plan.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: #4 Raw Material Supplier as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

  • Despatch Advice from #4 Raw Material Supplier: The mine announces what was loaded into which wagons, so the factory can plan the unloading.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #14 Rail Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Waybill from #14 Rail Carrier: The rail consignment note goes to the party that contracted the carriage, the manufacturer; the mine is named as consignor because the ore is loaded there.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: #4 Raw Material Supplier as ConsignorParty.

  • Invoice from #4 Raw Material Supplier: The mine invoices the ore; transport is not on this invoice because under FCA the buyer pays it. Payment goes to the mine's account at the seller's bank.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: #23 Seller's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Country A → Country B)

The distributor buys free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Order Response to #6 Distributor: The manufacturer accepts the order and confirms the collection date at its warehouse.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration to #19 Customs (XA): Goods leave Country A: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; #13 Road Carrier as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Certificate of Origin to #20 Chamber of Commerce (XA): The goods qualify for preferential duty in Country B; the manufacturer applies to its chamber of commerce for a certificate of origin.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ImporterParty.

  • Invoice to #6 Distributor: The manufacturer invoices the goods only (no freight under FCA) and asks for payment to its account at the seller's bank.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: #23 Seller's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from #6 Distributor: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: #8 Seller's Warehouse (3PL) as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty; #13 Road Carrier as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Remittance Advice from #6 Distributor: The distributor announces the cross-border payment and which invoices it settles.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: #23 Seller's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from #6 Distributor to #13 Road Carrier: The distributor, as transport user, books the truck: collection at the seller's 3PL, delivery at its own distribution centre, across the border.

    In it as Consignment/ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Despatch Advice from #8 Seller's Warehouse (3PL) to #6 Distributor: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty (The seller).

    Also mentions: #13 Road Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Certificate of Origin from #20 Chamber of Commerce (XA) to #6 Distributor: The chamber endorses the certificate and makes it available to the importer, who needs it for the import declaration.

    In it as ExporterParty (The Party who makes the export declaration, or on whose behalf the export declaration is made, and who is the owner of the goods or has similar right of disposal over them at the time when the declaration is accepted).

  • Waybill from #13 Road Carrier to #6 Distributor: The road consignment note goes to the party that booked the truck, the distributor; it names the manufacturer as consignor.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Import Customs Declaration from #6 Distributor to #19 Customs (XB): The distributor, as importer, declares the goods in Country B and claims the preferential duty rate with the certificate of origin.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Receipt Advice from #6 Distributor to #8 Seller's Warehouse (3PL): The distributor confirms what arrived to the party that despatched it, the 3PL, naming the manufacturer as seller.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty (The seller).

Containers by rail under transit (Country A → Country B)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to #14 Rail Carrier: Under DAP the manufacturer is the transport user; it books a container train to the distributor's siding.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transit Customs Declaration to #19 Customs (XA): The goods travel under customs transit from Country A to the destination in Country B; the manufacturer, as holder of the procedure, lodges the declaration at the office of departure.

    In it as TransitExporterParty, ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsigneeParty; #14 Rail Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to #6 Distributor: Under DAP the price includes the transport, so the invoice covers goods and freight.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: #23 Seller's Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from #6 Distributor: Delivery terms DAP distributor's siding: no carrier in the order, because the seller chooses and pays the transport.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan from #14 Rail Carrier: The rail carrier confirms the train and the slot at the intermodal hub.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportUserParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill from #14 Rail Carrier: The rail consignment note goes to the transport user, now the seller.

    In it as ReceiverParty, ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Bulk by river barge (Country A → Country B)

Bulk lots go down the river. The distributor buys carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay in Country B: the manufacturer books the barge and pays the freight; the risk passes when the barge is loaded.

Sends

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to #15 Inland Waterway Carrier: The manufacturer, as transport user under CPT, asks the inland waterway carrier for barge capacity.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Receives

  • Order from #6 Distributor: Delivery terms CPT river quay: the seller books the barge, the buyer collects at the quay.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan from #15 Inland Waterway Carrier: The barge operator confirms the vessel and the sailing.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportUserParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill from #15 Inland Waterway Carrier: The inland waterway consignment note, issued to the shipper.

    In it as ReceiverParty, ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond.

Sends

  • Tenderer Qualification to #2 Contracting Authority: The manufacturer declares its qualifications and those of its consortium partner.

    In it as TendererPartyQualification/MainQualifyingParty.

    Also mentions: #27 Consortium Partner as TendererPartyQualification/AdditionalQualifyingParty.

  • Tender to #2 Contracting Authority: The tender, with the consortium partner as subcontractor.

    In it as TendererParty.

    Also mentions: #27 Consortium Partner as SubcontractorParty.

Receives

  • Call For Tenders from #2 Contracting Authority: The manufacturer retrieves the procurement documents.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #28 Review Body as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Business Information from #21 Government Information Service to #2 Contracting Authority: The authority checks the tenderer's registration with the business registry.

    In it as BusinessParty (The Party who owns this Business Registration Information).

  • Guarantee Certificate from #25 Guarantor to #2 Contracting Authority: The guarantor notifies the authority of the bid bond deposited for the manufacturer's tender.

    In it as InterestedParty (The Party who deposits the bid bond guarantee).

  • Contract Award Notice from #2 Contracting Authority to #21 Government Information Service: The award is published; the review body is named for appeals.

    In it as TenderResult/WinningParty (A party that is identified as the awarded by a tender result).

    Also mentions: #28 Review Body as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Receives

  • Goods Item Passport from #20 Chamber of Commerce (XA): The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel.

    In it as HolderParty.

    Also mentions: #20 Chamber of Commerce (XC) as ImportingGuarantorParty; #19 Customs (XA) as ExportingCustomsParty; #19 Customs (XC) as ImportingCustomsParty.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Country A)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Country A one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Sends

  • Waste Notification to #19 Customs (XA): The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority.

    In it as SenderParty, NotifierParty, WasteProducerParty.

    Also mentions: #7 Waste Processor as RecoveryFacilityParty; #13 Road Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Invoice from #7 Waste Processor: The waste processor invoices collection and recovery.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Movement from #7 Waste Processor to #19 Customs (XA): The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority.

    In it as NotifierParty, WasteProducerParty (The Party that acts as the notifier in relation to the Waste Movements covered by this document) (The Party producing the waste material).

    Also mentions: #13 Road Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.
