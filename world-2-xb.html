#2 Contracting Authority

Country B (XB) · Public procurement

A public body that buys under procurement law: publishes notices, receives and evaluates tenders.

How UBL helps

A contracting authority has to buy in a transparent and comparable way. UBL tendering documents take it from the prior information notice to the contract award, so tenders arrive in a structured form that can be checked and evaluated consistently.

In this world it sends 4 and receives 4 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond.

Sends

  • Prior Information Notice to #21 Government Information Service: The authority announces the planned purchase early, through the publication service.

    In it as ContractingParty.

  • Contract Notice to #21 Government Information Service: The call for competition is published; it names the review body for appeals and mediation.

    In it as ContractingParty.

    Also mentions: #28 Review Body as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty; #28 Review Body as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/MediationParty.

  • Call For Tenders to #5 Manufacturer: The manufacturer retrieves the procurement documents.

    In it as ContractingParty.

    Also mentions: #28 Review Body as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.

  • Contract Award Notice to #21 Government Information Service: The award is published; the review body is named for appeals.

    In it as ContractingParty.

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as TenderResult/WinningParty; #28 Review Body as TenderingTerms/AppealTerms/AppealReceiverParty.

Receives

  • Business Information from #21 Government Information Service: The authority checks the tenderer's registration with the business registry.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as BusinessParty.

  • Tenderer Qualification from #5 Manufacturer: The manufacturer declares its qualifications and those of its consortium partner.

    In it as ContractingParty.

    Also mentions: #27 Consortium Partner as TendererPartyQualification/AdditionalQualifyingParty.

  • Guarantee Certificate from #25 Guarantor: The guarantor notifies the authority of the bid bond deposited for the manufacturer's tender.

    In it as BeneficiaryParty.

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as InterestedParty.

  • Tender from #5 Manufacturer: The tender, with the consortium partner as subcontractor.

    In it as ContractingParty.

    Also mentions: #27 Consortium Partner as SubcontractorParty.
