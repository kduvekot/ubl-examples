How UBL helps

A contracting authority has to buy in a transparent and comparable way. UBL tendering documents take it from the prior information notice to the contract award, so tenders arrive in a structured form that can be checked and evaluated consistently.

In this world it sends 19 and receives 19 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Furniture for the department store and for public offices (Bayvarra)

The furniture maker supplies the department store with a garden furniture line made for it. They agree on the item data and the stock policy per store, the store reports what it sells, and exceptions against agreed thresholds are flagged automatically. Slow sellers are taken back. For the public works department the furniture maker also installs, and documents the work for approval.
