In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states, and the federal territory in the north, levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer. Sales to businesses that resell, exports and sales to public bodies are exempt, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

A factor buys the distributor's invoices and collects the payment. UBL invoices can name the factor as payee, with its account, so the buyer pays the right party without a separate letter.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Lumeway Distribution to Brightmarket Stores: The distributor has sold its receivables to the factor, so the invoice names the factor as payee. See the example document.

    In it as PayeeParty (The Party who receives the Payment).

  • Credit Note from Lumeway Distribution to Brightmarket Stores: The shop reported a damaged carton in its receipt advice; the distributor credits it. The factor stays the payee, because it owns the receivable being reduced. See the example document.

    In it as PayeeParty (The Party who receives the Payment).

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Statement to Velumi Trading: The factor, who now owns the receivables, sends the statement of account on behalf of the distributor. See the example document.

    In it as PayeeParty, on behalf of Lumeway Distribution (AccountingSupplierParty).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as AccountingSupplierParty.

Receives

  • Remittance Advice from Velumi Trading: The buyer pays the factor from its account at the buyer's bank and says which invoices it settles. See the example document.

    In it as PayeeParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as AccountingSupplierParty; Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Lumeway Distribution to Velumi Trading: Goods, sea freight and insurance (CIF) in one invoice; the receivable is sold to the factor, who is the payee. See the example document.

    In it as PayeeParty (The Party who receives the Payment).

    Also mentions: Nalevi Warehousing as Delivery/DeliveryParty.
