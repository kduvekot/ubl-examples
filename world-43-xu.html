How UBL helps

A producer sells energy in bulk, by the month, to a few buyers. UBL's Invoice carries the quantities in megawatt hours and the price per unit, and the Utility Statement of the grid operator shows what was fed in.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Sends

  • Invoice to Ferrano Energy: The power station invoices the electricity it fed in for the retail company in March. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration to Customs Administration of Urvania: The power station declares the gas it received in March into Urvania. See the example document.

    In it as ImporterParty, ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Kelpstone Gas as ConsignorParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Kelpstone Gas: The power station pays the invoice and the surcharge, in ferra. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

Receives

  • Invoice from Kelpstone Gas: The gas producer invoices March's gas to the power station, in ferra as the supply contract says: an export, so no Bayvarran sales tax. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Reminder from Kelpstone Gas: The power station has not paid by the due date. The gas producer reminds it in lume, its own currency: the reminder shows the ferra invoice at the exchange rate and adds Bayvarra's surcharge for the month late. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Export Customs Declaration from Kelpstone Gas to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The gas producer declares March's gas leaving Bayvarra by pipeline, in one monthly declaration. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).
