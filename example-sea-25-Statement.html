Statement: Harbourline Factoring to Velumi Trading

Example 25 of the flow "Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)" · Statement in the UBL 2.5 specification

The factor, who now owns the receivables, sends the statement of account on behalf of the distributor.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
