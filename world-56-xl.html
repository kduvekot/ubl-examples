Fresh Produce Wholesaler · Lisavu (XL) · Commerce

Imports fruit and vegetables the island does not grow or not in the season, keeps them in its own cold store by the port and supplies the island's shops, hotels and the hospital's kitchen.

How UBL helps

A fresh-produce importer must know what is on its way and in what state it arrives. UBL brings it the supplier's despatch advice with the lots, the carrier's reefer data and its own receipt advice with the temperatures read at unloading, so a claim on a lot that arrived too warm rests on the documents.

In this world it sends 4 documents and receives 4 documents, and is named in 4 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Apples and pears in a reefer container by sea (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

In the southern autumn the island's fresh-produce wholesaler buys a 40-foot reefer of apples and pears from the fruit growers' cooperative at Harrowmead, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu (CIP): the cooperative books the reefer through its forwarder, which brings it pre-cooled to the packhouse and trucks it back to Lumeport on a generator; the plant health service of Bayvarra's Department of Agriculture certifies the fruit free of the pests the island names. The reefer sails at 0.5 °C and 90 % humidity, its fresh-air vent set for fruit that breathes, on Tidewell's weekly service; at Selanu it waits on the port's power until the wholesaler's carrier takes it to the cold store, where it is unloaded and its temperature log read. Food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import: the island taxes imported food to encourage its own farms.

Sends

  • Order to Harrowmead Fruit Growers: The wholesaler orders one reefer of apples and pears for the island's shops, hotels and hospital kitchen in the autumn season, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu: the cooperative books and insures the reefer. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration to Island Customs Service: The wholesaler lodges its import declaration before the ship arrives, so that the fruit is released when the reefer is discharged; the import consumption tax applies to food as to every import. See the example document.

    In it as ImporterParty, ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Harrowmead Fruit Growers as ConsignorParty.

  • Receipt Advice to Harrowmead Fruit Growers: The wholesaler confirms what it unloaded at its cold store, with the temperatures read from the reefer's log and measured in the fruit. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Harrowmead Fruit Growers: The wholesaler pays the cooperative's invoice in lume on its due date, from its account at the island's bank, and tells the cooperative which invoice it pays. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order Response from Harrowmead Fruit Growers: The cooperative accepts the order on a condition: its stored pears are running short, so it offers three pallets of pears instead of four and a fifth pallet of Granny Smith in their place, the offer open until Friday noon, its cut-off for booking the reefer on the next Thursday's ship. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Harrowmead Fruit Growers: The cooperative tells the wholesaler what is in the reefer: the pallets, their varieties, grower lots and packing dates. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice from Harrowmead Fruit Growers: The fruit at its CIP prices, which hold the reefer's carriage and the insurance, invoiced on shipment. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Transportation Status from Tidewell Ocean Lines: The carrier reports the reefer discharged at Selanu and on the port's power, with the temperatures its monitoring recorded at sea. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.
