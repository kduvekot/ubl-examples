Fernhollow Studio

#48 Design Studio · Bayvarra (XB) · Public procurement · proposed addition to the 26+C set

A small industrial design studio in Lumeport, founded in 2019 by two designers. It asked to take part in the design contest for the harbour promenade bench and was excluded at the selection: the certificate of the social security fund showed contributions due and unpaid, above the minor amounts the procurement act tolerates (O102).

How UBL helps

A candidate that is excluded is told why. UBL's Tenderer Qualification Response carries the decision on each candidate, with the reason for an exclusion, so the candidate knows where it stands and can appeal.

A design contest for the promenade bench (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority relaunches a design contest for the bench of Lumeport's harbour promenade, after cancelling the first one because no design met the brief. Several documents are revised and say which version they supersede: the prior information notice after the council raised the prize fund, the contest notice and its documents in a corrigendum, the design team's declarations, the registry's extract once new accounts are filed, and the results notice, which corrects a prize amount. No goods move.
