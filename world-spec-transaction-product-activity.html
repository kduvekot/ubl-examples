Product activity

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:product-activity:1.0

One document type carries the transaction, the Product Activity, which UBL 2.5 defines as follows: “A document reporting the movement of goods at specified retail locations for inventory tracking purposes.”

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.
