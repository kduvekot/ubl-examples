Retail community

Community specification in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:community:retail:1.0

The distributor and the department store exchange their documents the GS1 way: GTINs, GLNs and SSCCs, a catalogue the store orders from, and the replenishment documents.

Members: Lumeway Distribution, Brightmarket Stores. It applies to the documents the members exchange among themselves, chained after the transaction and any national specification.

Document types: every document type the members exchange.
