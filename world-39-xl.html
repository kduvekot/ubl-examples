How UBL helps

The ginnery buys from many farms and sells overseas. UBL covers both sides: self-billing towards the farms, and towards the mill an export with weight statement, goods certificate, customs declaration and invoice.

In this world it sends 6 and receives 6 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

  • Self Billed Invoice to Lisavu Cotton Farm: Under the self-billing agreement the ginnery writes the farm's invoice from the weight and grade it measured. No tax on the island, stated as such.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Self Billed Credit Note to Lisavu Cotton Farm: Classing after ginning puts part of the delivery a grade lower; the ginnery credits the difference on the farm's behalf.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Lisavu Cotton Farm: The ginnery pays the farm the net amount.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Export Customs Declaration to Island Customs Service: The island's export: the ginnery declares the bales to customs.

    In it as ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Textile Mill as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

  • Weight Statement to Tidewell Ocean Lines: Before loading, the shipper declares the verified gross mass of the container, weighed on the port's weighbridge.

    In it as SenderParty, ShipperParty.

    Also mentions: Port of Selanu as WeighingParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeport Textile Mill: The bales, free on board. No tax applies on the island, and the invoice says so.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Forecast from Lumeport Textile Mill: The mill tells the ginnery how many bales it expects to buy this season.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Delivery Note from Lisavu Cotton Farm: The farm delivers a trailer of seed cotton at the gin and confirms what it delivered.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Forecast Revision from Lumeport Textile Mill: Demand for clothing is up; the mill revises its forecast upwards.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Order from Lumeport Textile Mill: Bales of grade cotton. Delivery terms FOB the island port: the mill arranges the sea freight.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Goods Certificate from Selanu Chamber of Commerce: The island's chamber of commerce certifies the bales for export, at the ginnery's application.

    In it as ApplicantParty, ExporterParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Textile Mill as ImporterParty.

  • Remittance Advice from Lumeport Textile Mill: The mill pays the island's ginnery: money flows to the island for once.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Forwarding Instructions from Lumeport Textile Mill to Bayforth Forwarding: Under FOB the buyer books the main carriage: the mill instructs its forwarder.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Bill of Lading from Tidewell Ocean Lines to Bayforth Forwarding: The bill of lading goes to the forwarder that booked the freight for the mill; the ginnery is the shipper.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

    Also mentions: Lumeport Textile Mill as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeport Textile Mill to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The mill declares the bales in Bayvarra.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).
