How UBL helps

Telecom bills list calls, data and subscriptions per line. The UBL Utility Statement has a telecommunications structure of its own, so a business customer can allocate the costs of each line without retyping a paper bill.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays for electricity, water and the telephone. A business with a meter read every month is billed every month on its reading. A household pays on account: a twelfth of the year's estimate every month by direct debit, the meter read once a year, and an annual statement and a settlement bill for the actual year. In Urvania the electricity bill carries the grid operator's network charges beside the retail company's energy; in Lumeport the water company also runs the district heating, and its bill carries the city's sewage levy; the island's telephone company bills its business customers every month. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with the meter readings.

Batteries for the island's telecom mast, by air (dangerous goods)

The island's telecom company orders back-up batteries, smoke detectors and a coating stripper for its mast from an engineering firm in Bayvarra, carriage paid to the mast site. All three are dangerous goods: the forwarder trucks them to Lumeport airport and the island's airline flies them on its freighter, the batteries and the stripper, which may fly on cargo aircraft only, where the crew can reach them and apart from each other, the smoke detectors in a closed container.

Sends
