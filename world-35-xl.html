How UBL helps

Telecom bills list calls, data and subscriptions per line. The UBL Utility Statement has a telecommunications structure of its own, so a business customer can allocate the costs of each line without retyping a paper bill.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Sends

  • Utility Statement to Velumi Trading: Calls, data and subscriptions per telephone line of the buyer, in the telecommunications supply structure. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty.

  • Invoice to Velumi Trading: The telecom bill. The island has no sales tax or VAT, and the invoice says that no tax applies rather than leaving it out. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Remittance Advice from Velumi Trading: The buyer pays the telecom bill from its account at the island's bank. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Selanu as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Batteries for the island's telecom mast, by air (dangerous goods)

The island's telecom company orders back-up batteries, smoke detectors and a coating stripper for its mast from an engineering firm in Bayvarra. All three are dangerous goods; the batteries fly in a temperature-controlled container.

Sends

  • Order to Saltmere Engineering: The telecom company orders back-up power and safety equipment for its mast. Carriage paid to its site: the seller arranges the dangerous goods transport. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

Receives

  • Despatch Advice from Saltmere Engineering: The engineering firm announces the goods to the telecom company. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Kelaro Air Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice from Saltmere Engineering: Goods and carriage to the site; an export, so no sales tax. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Forwarding Instructions from Saltmere Engineering to Bayforth Forwarding: The engineering firm instructs the forwarder, with the shipper's declaration of dangerous goods. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Waybill from Kelaro Air Cargo to Bayforth Forwarding: The air waybill for cargo aircraft only, with the dangerous goods on it. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: Saltmere Engineering as ConsignorParty.
