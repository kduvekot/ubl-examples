Transportation Status: Ferrano Intermodal Hub to Ferrano Rail Cargo

Document 5 of 14 in the flow Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra) · Transportation Status in the UBL 2.5 specification

From Ferrano Intermodal Hub to Ferrano Rail Cargo, under the Trade Accord's transaction Transportation status.

← Transit Customs Declaration · all documents of the flow · Waybill →

The intermodal terminal reports to the rail carrier that the containers are loaded on the train.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
