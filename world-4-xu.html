How UBL helps

A mine sells a few products in large volumes to a small number of customers. With UBL, its orders, despatch advices and invoices carry the same quantities, lot numbers and weights from the first document to the last, so the buyer can match them without retyping weighbridge tickets.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Ore from the mine to the factory (domestic, Urvania)

Under a supply agreement for 2026, which sets the price basis, one train a month and a moisture clause, Valdara calls off one train of iron ore a month from Serravona Mining, free carrier (FCA) at Fucinalta mine siding: the buyer arranges and pays the main transport, so Valdara, not the mine, books Ferrano Rail Cargo's ore train under their framework transport agreement, within the standing plan for the year. On Tuesday 10 March, the first day of the loading window, the mine loads 30 wagons, which are weighed at the siding: 1,788.4 t of lump ore with 62 % iron. The train runs about 160 km on the line past Ferrano into the factory's siding and arrives the same afternoon, which earns the carrier the 2 % bonus of the plan, so that the factory can start the blend a day early. The mine deducts 1.5 % of the value for the 7.5 % moisture measured at loading, as the agreement's moisture clause says (decision D45).

Sends

  • Despatch Advice to Valdara Industries: The mine announces what was loaded into which wagons, so the factory can plan the unloading. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Ferrano Rail Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: The mine invoices the ore; transport is not on this invoice because under FCA the buyer pays it. Payment goes to the mine's account at the seller's bank. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order from Valdara Industries: Monthly ore call-off. Delivery terms FCA mine: the buyer books the train, so the order names the rail carrier that will collect. See the example document.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty.

    Also mentions: Ferrano Rail Cargo as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Remittance Advice from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer tells the mine which invoices the transfer pays. Both companies bank at the same domestic bank. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Valdara Industries to Ferrano Rail Cargo: The manufacturer, as transport user, asks the rail carrier for wagons and a slot to move the ore from the mine to its siding. See the example document.

    In it as Consignment/ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Transport Execution Plan from Ferrano Rail Cargo to Valdara Industries: The rail carrier confirms the train, the wagons and the schedule; this is the agreed transport plan. See the example document.

    In it as Consignment/ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

  • Waybill from Ferrano Rail Cargo to Valdara Industries: The rail consignment note goes to the party that contracted the carriage, the manufacturer; the mine is named as consignor because the ore is loaded there. See the example document.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).
