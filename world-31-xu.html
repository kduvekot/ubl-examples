Codana Identification Agency

Identifier Issuing Agency · Urvania (XU) · Government

Urvania's identification agency: it gives each company that numbers its products and packages under ISO/IEC 15459 a company number, under the agency's issuing agency code; the company then numbers its own items and packages under that number.

How UBL helps

One issuing agency code and one company number make an item or package number unique, so a catalogue line, an order line and an invoice line point to the same thing, whoever reads them.

In the world

See Identifier Issuing Agency on the map · Urvania: Mainland, in the west: a rugged coast without a deep-water port, and the river down to Lumeport. Raw materials, manufacturing and power.

Who sends and who receives each document follows the UBL 2.5 model of the documents and their party elements.
