How UBL helps

Everyone in the world refers to the same products and properties. An identifier issuing agency gives those items and properties stable identifiers, which UBL documents carry in their identification elements, so a catalogue line, an order line and an invoice line point to exactly the same thing.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment.

Is named in documents between others

  • Catalogue from Lumeway Distribution to Brightmarket Stores: The distributor's catalogue of items and prices; items carry the identifiers of the identifier issuing agency. See the example document.

    In it as CatalogueLine/Item/StandardItemIdentification/IssuerParty (The Party who issues this Item Identification).

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Is named in documents between others

  • Catalogue from Lumeway Distribution to Velumi Trading: The export catalogue for the island customer. See the example document.

    In it as CatalogueLine/Item/StandardItemIdentification/IssuerParty (The Party who issues this Item Identification).
