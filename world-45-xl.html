Department of Agriculture of Lisavu

Department of Agriculture (XL) · Lisavu (XL) · Government

The island's Department of Agriculture: its Plant Protection Service is Lisavu's plant protection organisation under the IPPC and issues the phytosanitary certificates of plants and plant products leaving the island; it also keeps the farm register.

How UBL helps

A plant protection organisation inspects a consignment before export and certifies it free of the pests the importing country names. UBL's goods certificate carries the inspection, its findings and the issuing authority, so the importing country's inspectors and customs can check it before the goods arrive.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's cotton is picked from March to May. Before the harvest the ginnery advances the farm a part of the value of its expected delivery, and the textile mill in Lumeport tells the ginnery how much it expects to buy this season. The farm delivers seed cotton to the gin; the ginnery weighs it and writes the farm's invoice itself (self-billing), and credits the farm for the part that classing puts a grade lower. The mill orders bales free on board (FOB) the island port and books the sea freight through its forwarder. The island's plant protection service certifies the bales free of pests, the ginnery declares the export and states the container's verified gross mass, and the ship carries the container to Lumeport, where the mill clears it. The mill pays the ginnery: money flows to the island for once.
