Bill of Lading: Tidewell Ocean Lines to Bayforth Forwarding

Document 13 of 18 in the flow Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra) · Bill of Lading in the UBL 2.5 specification

From Tidewell Ocean Lines to Bayforth Forwarding, under the Trade Accord's transaction Bill of lading.

← Weight Statement · all documents of the flow · Invoice →

The bill of lading goes to the forwarder that booked the freight for the mill; the ginnery is the shipper.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.

Values and their reasons

The document itself

    The earliest version of UBL 2 that defines every element of the document, as UBLVersionID asks: computed from the published models of UBL 2.0 to 2.5; Party/AdditionalPartyIdentification first appears in UBL 2.5.

  • ProfileID = urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:transport:1.0
    The Trade Accord's process profile the document belongs to: transport, a generic, versioned profile, not the flow.
  • ProfileExecutionID = BYF-TX-26-2410
    The collaboration this document belongs to: the sea carriage of the lint Bayforth books with Tidewell Ocean Lines. Bayforth Forwarding started it and gave it this identifier, which every document of it repeats.

    The bill of lading number.

    A universally unique identifier of the document, derived from its place in the world (UUID version 5), so it stays the same on every build.
