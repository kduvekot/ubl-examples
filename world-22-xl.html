In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The buyer's bank sends the payment abroad. The payment reference and payee account come straight from the UBL invoice, so the payment arrives with the information the seller's bank needs to match it.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 6 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Is named in documents between others

  • Remittance Advice from Velumi Trading to Harbourline Factoring: The buyer pays the factor from its account at the buyer's bank and says which invoices it settles.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as AccountingSupplierParty.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Is named in documents between others

  • Invoice from Selanu Telecom to Velumi Trading: The telecom bill. The island has no sales tax or VAT, and the invoice says that no tax applies rather than leaving it out.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

  • Remittance Advice from Velumi Trading to Selanu Telecom: The buyer pays the telecom bill from its account at the island's bank.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution, PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)) (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Is named in documents between others

  • Remittance Advice from Selanu Cotton Ginnery to Lisavu Cotton Farm: The ginnery pays the farm the net amount.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution, PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)) (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

  • Invoice from Selanu Cotton Ginnery to Lumeport Textile Mill: The bales, free on board. No tax applies on the island, and the invoice says so.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

  • Remittance Advice from Lumeport Textile Mill to Selanu Cotton Ginnery: The mill pays the island's ginnery: money flows to the island for once.

    In it as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution (The financial institution that this branch belongs to (if applicable)).

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.
