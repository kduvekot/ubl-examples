The map sheet

The printed map of the example world: an A3 landscape sheet at 1 : 2,500,000, drawn from the same terrain, places and routes as the interactive map. It shows the landscape, the towns and villages, the roads, railways and pipelines, and the border between Urvania and Bayvarra. Lisavu, about 1,100 km east of Lumeport, stands in a panel of its own at the same scale.

Open the sheet at full size (4,961 × 3,508 pixels, 300 dpi, about 6 MB).

Work in progress. Not an OASIS publication. UBL is an OASIS Standard and ISO/IEC 19845.
