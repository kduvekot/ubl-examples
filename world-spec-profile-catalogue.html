Catalogue

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:catalogue:1.0

A buyer asks for a catalogue or for information about an item, and the seller provides and maintains its catalogue.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: CatalogueRequest, Catalogue, CataloguePricingUpdate, CatalogueItemSpecificationUpdate, CatalogueDeletion, ItemInformationRequest.
