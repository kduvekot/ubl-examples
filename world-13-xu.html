How UBL helps

Road carriers handle many short jobs with frequent changes. With UBL they receive transport instructions, report status at each stop and invoice the freight in one structured format, so every customer, large or small, can process them automatically.

In this world it sends 5 and receives 4 documents, and is named in 13 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Waybill to Lumeway Distribution: The road consignment note goes to the party that booked the truck, the distributor; it names the manufacturer as consignor. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

  • Transportation Status to Lumeway Distribution: The carrier reports that the truck has cleared the border and gives the expected arrival time. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: The carrier invoices the transport to the party that booked it; under FCA that is the buyer, not the seller. A transport service is invoiced with the ordinary Invoice; the Freight Invoice is deprecated in UBL 2.5. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor, as transport user, books the truck: collection at the seller's 3PL, delivery at its own distribution centre, across the border. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order from Lumeway Distribution to Valdara Industries: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them. See the example document.

    In it as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Castrano Logistics as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Castrano Logistics to Lumeway Distribution: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Urvania: Goods leave Urvania: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment.

Sends

  • Waybill to Lumeway Distribution: The outgoing consignment note goes to the distributor, who contracted the carriage; the retailer is only the consignee. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Brightmarket Stores as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: Under DDP the seller pays the transport, so the carrier invoices the distributor. A transport service is invoiced with the ordinary Invoice; the Freight Invoice is deprecated in UBL 2.5. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Lumeway Distribution: Because it sold DDP, the distributor books the outgoing truck from its distribution centre to the shop. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Brightmarket Stores as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transportation Status Request from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor, as transport user, asks the carrier for the truck's arrival time. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution to Brightmarket Stores: The distributor announces the delivery and the truck that brings it. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

An urgent part of the order by air (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waybill from Kelaro Air Cargo to Bayforth Forwarding: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty (The Party who subtitutes the carrier of this Consignment).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Urvania one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

The sawmill in Upland State saws timber to size. The manufacturer in Urvania asks for a quotation for bench slats to its own dimensions and orders them delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill arranges the truck across the border. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Marrowdale Sawmill: Under DAP the sawmill is the transport user; it books the road carrier across the border. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Export Customs Declaration from Marrowdale Sawmill to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The slats leave Bayvarra; the sawmill declares the export at the land border. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Marrowdale Sawmill to Valdara Industries: The sawmill announces the slats per lot, so the factory can trace the wood of every bench. See the example document.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).
