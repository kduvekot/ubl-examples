How UBL helps

Road carriers handle many short jobs with frequent changes. With UBL they receive transport instructions, report status at each stop and invoice the freight in one structured format, so every customer, large or small, can process them automatically.

In this world it sends 5 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 9 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Country A → Country B)

The distributor buys free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Waybill to Lumeway Distribution: The road consignment note goes to the party that booked the truck, the distributor; it names the manufacturer as consignor.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

  • Transportation Status to Lumeway Distribution: The carrier reports that the truck has cleared the border and gives the expected arrival time.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: The carrier invoices the transport to the party that booked it; under FCA that is the buyer, not the seller. A transport service is invoiced with the ordinary Invoice; the Freight Invoice is deprecated in UBL 2.5.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor, as transport user, books the truck: collection at the seller's 3PL, delivery at its own distribution centre, across the border.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order from Lumeway Distribution to Valdara Industries: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them.

    In it as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Castrano Logistics as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Castrano Logistics to Lumeway Distribution: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Country A: Goods leave Country A: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Country B)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment.

Sends

  • Waybill to Lumeway Distribution: The outgoing consignment note goes to the distributor, who contracted the carriage; the retailer is only the consignee.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Brightmarket Stores as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: Under DDP the seller pays the transport, so the carrier invoices the distributor. A transport service is invoiced with the ordinary Invoice; the Freight Invoice is deprecated in UBL 2.5.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty, Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Lumeway Distribution: Because it sold DDP, the distributor books the outgoing truck from its distribution centre to the shop.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: Brightmarket Stores as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution to Brightmarket Stores: The distributor announces the delivery and the truck that brings it.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

An urgent part of the order by air (Country B → island Country C)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waybill from Kelaro Air Cargo to Bayforth Forwarding: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty (The Party who subtitutes the carrier of this Consignment).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Is named in documents between others

  • Despatch Advice from Castrano Logistics to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer's 3PL despatches the call-off and names the carrier.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Country A: The call-off leaves Country A; the manufacturer declares the export.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Lumeport Public Works Department as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Country A)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Country A one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Country A: The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as RecoveryFacilityParty.

  • Waste Movement from Rinova Recovery to Customs Administration of Country A: The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as NotifierParty; Valdara Industries as WasteProducerParty.
