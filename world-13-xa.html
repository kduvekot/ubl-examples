#13 Road Carrier

Country A (XA) · Logistics

Trucks the goods across the land border to the distributor; last-mile delivery.

How UBL helps

Road carriers handle many short jobs with frequent changes. With UBL they receive transport instructions, report status at each stop and invoice the freight in one structured format, so every customer, large or small, can process them automatically.

In this world it sends 5 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 7 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Country A → Country B)

The distributor buys free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Waybill to #6 Distributor: The road consignment note goes to the party that booked the truck, the distributor; it names the manufacturer as consignor.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as ConsignorParty.

  • Transportation Status to #6 Distributor: The carrier reports that the truck has cleared the border and gives the expected arrival time.

    In it as SenderParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Freight Invoice to #6 Distributor: The carrier invoices the transport to the party that booked it; under FCA that is the buyer, not the seller.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from #6 Distributor: The distributor, as transport user, books the truck: collection at the seller's 3PL, delivery at its own distribution centre, across the border.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order from #6 Distributor to #5 Manufacturer: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them.

    In it as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: #8 Seller's Warehouse (3PL) as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty.

  • Despatch Advice from #8 Seller's Warehouse (3PL) to #6 Distributor: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Export Customs Declaration from #5 Manufacturer to #19 Customs (XA): Goods leave Country A: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Country B)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment.

Sends

  • Waybill to #6 Distributor: The outgoing consignment note goes to the distributor, who contracted the carriage; the retailer is only the consignee.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: #26 Retailer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Freight Invoice to #6 Distributor: Under DDP the seller pays the transport, so the carrier invoices the distributor.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from #6 Distributor: Because it sold DDP, the distributor books the outgoing truck from its distribution centre to the shop.

    In it as ReceiverParty, TransportServiceProviderParty.

    Also mentions: #26 Retailer as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Despatch Advice from #6 Distributor to #26 Retailer: The distributor announces the delivery and the truck that brings it.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

An urgent part of the order by air (Country B → island Country C)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waybill from #12 Air Carrier to #10 Freight Forwarder: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty (The Party who subtitutes the carrier of this Consignment).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Country A)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Country A one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Notification from #5 Manufacturer to #19 Customs (XA): The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: #7 Waste Processor as RecoveryFacilityParty.

  • Waste Movement from #7 Waste Processor to #19 Customs (XA): The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: #5 Manufacturer as NotifierParty; #5 Manufacturer as WasteProducerParty.
