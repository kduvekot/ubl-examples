#13 Road Carrier

Country A (XA) · Logistics

Trucks the goods across the land border to the distributor; last-mile delivery.

How UBL helps

Road carriers handle many short jobs with frequent changes. With UBL they receive transport instructions, report status at each stop and invoice the freight in one structured format, so every customer, large or small, can process them automatically.

Documents it exchanges

  • Waybill
  • TransportationStatus
  • FreightInvoice
