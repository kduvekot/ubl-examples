Import customs on the island.

In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

Import customs on the island receives a declaration that reuses the data of the export side and the transport documents, which speeds up clearance at the port.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Receives

  • Manifest from Tidewell Ocean Lines: Before arrival the carrier lists the cargo on board for the island's customs. See the example document.

    In it as AuthorityParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as ConsigneeParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration from Tevaro Customs Brokers: Under CIF the buyer clears the import; its licensed customs broker files the declaration on its behalf. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as ImporterParty; Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as ConsigneeParty; Bayforth Forwarding as FreightForwarderParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Is named in documents between others

  • Goods Item Passport from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce to Valdara Industries: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingCustomsParty (The Party who is competent of Customs in the importing country).

    Also mentions: Selanu Chamber of Commerce as ImportingGuarantorParty; Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty.

  • Proof of Reexportation Request from Selanu Chamber of Commerce to Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: The island's chamber has no re-export stamp for the samples and asks for proof that they left. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingCustomsParty (The Party who originally requests the Proof of Reexportation).

  • Proof of Reexportation Reminder from Selanu Chamber of Commerce to Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: No answer after a month: a reminder. See the example document.

    In it as ImportingCustomsParty (The Party who originally requests the Proof of Reexportation).

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Selanu Cotton Ginnery: The island's export: the ginnery declares the bales to customs. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Textile Mill as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.
