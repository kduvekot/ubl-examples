Island Customs Service

Customs (XL) · Lisavu (XL) · Government

The island's customs, at the port and the airport of Selanu: clears imports and the island's exports, its cotton among them, and receives the ship's manifest before the ship arrives.

In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

Customs on the island receives declarations that reuse the data of the other side and of the transport documents, which speeds up clearance at the port and the airport.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The distributor wins a new customer on the island. It introduces itself, both join the Trade Accord's digital agreement, and the buyer orders 104 benches and spare frames, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 benches for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor buys through its freight forwarder; the invoice bills the goods alone. The forwarder clears the export and books the container; the carrier reports its ship and cargo to the island's authorities before it arrives; the buyer's customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them. The distributor has sold the receivable to a factor, which sends the statement of account. A frame arrives crushed and a pallet's illegible mark makes customs inspect the container, so the distributor credits the frame and the cost of the inspection, and the buyer pays the factor. The buyer sells two of the benches in its shop.

Receives

  • Manifest from Tidewell Ocean Lines: Before arrival the carrier lists the cargo on board for the island's customs. See the example document.

    In it as AuthorityParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as ConsigneeParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration from Tevaro Customs Brokers: Under CIF the buyer clears the import; its licensed customs broker files the declaration on its behalf. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as ImporterParty; Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as ConsigneeParty; Bayforth Forwarding as FreightForwarderParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows two sample benches at the Selanu Chamber of Commerce's trade fair in early June and brings them back. An ATA carnet issued by Urvania's chamber of commerce lets them into the island without its import consumption tax; customs stamp its counterfoils as the samples leave Urvania, cross Bayvarra on its transit vouchers, and enter and leave the island by air. The island's re-exportation voucher goes astray, so after the time limit the island's chamber asks Urvania's chamber for proof of re-exportation, reminds it a month later, and receives the proof; the claim for the island's tax falls away.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's cotton is picked from March to May. Before the harvest the ginnery advances the farm a part of the value of its expected delivery, and the textile mill in Lumeport tells the ginnery how much it expects to buy this season. The farm delivers seed cotton to the gin; the ginnery weighs it and writes the farm's invoice itself (self-billing), and credits the farm for the part that classing puts a grade lower. The mill orders bales free on board (FOB) the island port and books the sea freight through its forwarder. The island's plant protection service certifies the bales free of pests, the ginnery declares the export and states the container's verified gross mass, and the ship carries the container to Lumeport, where the mill clears it. The mill pays the ginnery: money flows to the island for once.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Selanu Cotton Ginnery: The island's export: the ginnery declares the bales to customs. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Textile Mill as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Apples and pears in a reefer container by sea (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

In the southern autumn the island's fresh-produce wholesaler buys a 40-foot reefer of apples and pears from the fruit growers' cooperative at Harrowmead, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu (CIP): the cooperative books the reefer through its forwarder, which brings it pre-cooled to the packhouse and trucks it back to Lumeport on a generator; the plant health service of Bayvarra's Department of Agriculture certifies the fruit free of the pests the island names. The reefer sails at 0.5 °C and 90 % humidity, its fresh-air vent set for fruit that breathes, on Tidewell's weekly service; at Selanu it waits on the port's power until the wholesaler's carrier takes it to the cold store, where it is unloaded and its temperature log read. Food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import: the island taxes imported food to encourage its own farms.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Hafefo Fresh Produce: The wholesaler lodges its import declaration before the ship arrives, so that the fruit is released when the reefer is discharged; the import consumption tax applies to food as to every import. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Harrowmead Fruit Growers as ConsignorParty.

Medicines and a technetium generator for the island's hospital, by air (cold chain and radioactive material)

Selanu General Hospital orders the vaccines for the island's winter influenza campaign, insulin, a frozen varicella vaccine and its weekly technetium-99m generator from a pharmaceutical wholesaler in Lumeport, carriage paid to Selanu airport. The forwarder books the morning flight of Monday 15 June: the vaccines and the insulin fly at 2 to 8 °C in the airline's active temperature-controlled container, the frozen vaccine in an insulated box on dry ice, and the generator, a Type A package of radioactive material with a Yellow label, under a shipper's declaration of its own, in the aft hold. The wholesaler's own van brings the generator to the cargo terminal at 05:30. On the island the customs broker declares the import for the hospital, free of duty and exempt from the import consumption tax, and the hospital pharmacy's van fetches the goods.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Tevaro Customs Brokers: The customs broker, as the hospital's direct representative, declares the import at Selanu airport in the hospital's name: no duty on medicines, and the exemption from the import consumption tax for medicines supplied to a public hospital. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu General Hospital as ImporterParty; Quillmoor Pharma Distribution as ConsignorParty; Selanu General Hospital as ConsigneeParty.
