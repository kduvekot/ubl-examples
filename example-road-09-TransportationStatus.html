Transportation Status: Trasvia Haulage to Lumeway Distribution

Example 9 of the flow "Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)" · Transportation Status in the UBL 2.5 specification

The carrier reports that the truck has cleared the border and gives the expected arrival time.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
