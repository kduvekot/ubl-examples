Lumeport port community

Community specification in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:community:port-lumeport:1.0

The port, its customs, the shipping lines and the forwarders of Lumeport exchange the documents of sea transport and of customs through the port in one way: the port's code UN/LOCODE XBLMP, ISO 6346 container numbers, and the declarations the port's customs office needs before the ship arrives.

Members: Port of Lumeport, Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra, Tidewell Ocean Lines, Bayforth Forwarding. It applies to the documents of these types in the flows sea, cotton, chained after the transaction and any national specification.

Document types: ForwardingInstructions, PackingList, BillOfLading, Waybill, Manifest, CommonTransportationReport, TransportationStatus, TransportationStatusRequest, TransportServiceDescription, TransportServiceDescriptionRequest, WeightStatement, ExportCustomsDeclaration, ImportCustomsDeclaration.
