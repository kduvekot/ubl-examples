#20 Chamber of Commerce (XA)

Country A (XA) · Government

Certifies the origin of the exported goods and issues ATA carnets.

How UBL helps

A chamber of commerce certifies that goods come from where the exporter says. UBL certificate-of-origin and goods-item-passport documents carry the application, the endorsements and the goods in a form customs on the other side can read and verify.

Documents it exchanges

  • CertificateOfOrigin
  • GoodsItemPassport

UBL roles

The party elements in which this party appears:

  • IssuerParty
  • EndorserParty
  • ExportingGuarantorParty
