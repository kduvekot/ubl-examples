#10 Freight Forwarder

Country B (XB) · Logistics

Books the transport, consolidates shipments and prepares the export documents; also arranges cargo insurance.

How UBL helps

A freight forwarder organises transport for others. It uses UBL forwarding instructions, packing lists and manifests to combine shipments, book carriers and arrange insurance, keeping one consignment reference across all of them.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 8 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Country B → island Country C)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Export Customs Declaration to #19 Customs (XB): The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the distributor at the seaport.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/ShipmentStage/CustomsAgentParty, on behalf of #6 Distributor (ExporterParty).

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ExporterParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

  • Forwarding Instructions to #11 Ocean Carrier: The forwarder, now acting as shipper, books the container with the ocean carrier.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Receives

  • Forwarding Instructions from #6 Distributor: The distributor instructs its forwarder: book the sea freight to the island and insure the cargo (CIF obliges the seller to insure).

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; #29 Insurer as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Packing List from #6 Distributor: How the goods are packed in the container, for the forwarder's booking and the export declaration.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transportation Status from #16 Seaport (XB): The port of loading reports the container loaded on board.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #11 Ocean Carrier as Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Bill of Lading from #11 Ocean Carrier: The negotiable bill of lading, issued to the forwarder that gave the instructions; it names the insurer of the cargo and the buyer's 3PL as party to notify on arrival.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; #9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) as Shipment/Consignment/NotifyParty; #29 Insurer as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Transportation Status from #16 Seaport (XC): The port of discharge reports the container discharged and available.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #11 Ocean Carrier as Consignment/CarrierParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Import Customs Declaration from #3 Customs Declarant / Broker to #19 Customs (XC): Under CIF the buyer clears the import; its licensed customs broker files the declaration on its behalf.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty (The Party who combines individual smaller consignments into a single larger shipment (a so-called consolidated consignment or shipment) which is sent to a counterpart who mirrors the consolidator's activity by dividing the consolidated consignment into its original components).

    Also mentions: #1 Commercial Buyer as ImporterParty; #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as ConsigneeParty.

An urgent part of the order by air (Country B → island Country C)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Receives

  • Forwarding Instructions from #6 Distributor: The distributor asks its forwarder to book air freight for the urgent part.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill from #12 Air Carrier: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport.

    In it as ReceiverParty, FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; #13 Road Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty.

  • Transportation Status from #17 Airport (XC): The island's air cargo terminal reports the cargo arrived and ready for customs clearance.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: #12 Air Carrier as Consignment/CarrierParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.
