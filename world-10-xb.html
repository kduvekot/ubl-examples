How UBL helps

A freight forwarder organises transport for others. It uses UBL forwarding instructions, packing lists and manifests to combine shipments, book carriers and arrange insurance, keeping one consignment reference across all of them.

In this world it sends 5 and receives 10 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Export Customs Declaration to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the distributor at the seaport. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/ShipmentStage/CustomsAgentParty, on behalf of Lumeway Distribution (ExporterParty).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ExporterParty; Velumi Trading as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

  • Forwarding Instructions to Tidewell Ocean Lines: The forwarder, now acting as shipper, books the container with the ocean carrier. See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: The forwarder invoices what it arranged for the distributor: sea freight, export clearance and the cargo insurance premium (CIF obliges the seller to insure). See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Forwarding Instructions from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor instructs its forwarder: book the sea freight to the island and insure the cargo (CIF obliges the seller to insure). See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Wavesure Cargo Insurance as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Packing List from Lumeway Distribution: How the goods are packed in the container, for the forwarder's booking and the export declaration. See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Transportation Status from Port of Lumeport: The port of loading reports the container loaded on board. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Bill of Lading from Tidewell Ocean Lines: The negotiable bill of lading, issued to the forwarder that gave the instructions; it names the insurer of the cargo and the buyer's 3PL as party to notify on arrival. See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Nalevi Warehousing as Shipment/Consignment/NotifyParty; Wavesure Cargo Insurance as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Transportation Status from Port of Selanu: The port of discharge reports the container discharged and available. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Consignment/CarrierParty; Velumi Trading as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

An urgent part of the order by air (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Sends

  • Invoice to Lumeway Distribution: The forwarder invoices the air freight, including the road feeder leg, to the distributor, who sold carriage paid to the island airport. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Kelaro Air Cargo as Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Forwarding Instructions from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor asks its forwarder to book air freight for the urgent part. See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Waybill from Kelaro Air Cargo: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty.

  • Transportation Status from Selanu Airport Cargo: The island's air cargo terminal reports the cargo arrived and ready for customs clearance. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Kelaro Air Cargo as Consignment/CarrierParty; Velumi Trading as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

  • Invoice to Lumeport Textile Mill: The forwarder invoices the sea freight it booked for the mill. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Tidewell Ocean Lines as Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Receives

  • Forwarding Instructions from Lumeport Textile Mill: Under FOB the buyer books the main carriage: the mill instructs its forwarder. See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Cotton Ginnery as ConsignorParty.

  • Bill of Lading from Tidewell Ocean Lines: The bill of lading goes to the forwarder that booked the freight for the mill; the ginnery is the shipper. See the example document.

    In it as FreightForwarderParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Cotton Ginnery as ConsignorParty; Lumeport Textile Mill as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.
