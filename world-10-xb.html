How UBL helps

A freight forwarder organises transport for others. It uses UBL forwarding instructions, packing lists and manifests to combine shipments, book carriers and arrange insurance, keeping one consignment reference across all of them.

In this world it sends 9 and receives 14 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment. In 2026 the store renovates the home and garden department of its Lumeport store and reopens it on Monday 11 May, with two weeks at 10 % off; to set up the range it asks the distributor for a catalogue, which the distributor deletes when its winter catalogue takes over on 20 June.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys 104 benches, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock, since it buys by the pallet and two pallets add little to the freight of a container already booked. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, are CIF prices: they hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor pays to its freight forwarder together with the forwarder's truck to the port, and the invoice bills the goods alone. CIF stays as common practice and as the flow's theme, although the ICC's guidance note to CIF says that where goods are handed over to a carrier at a container terminal, the appropriate rule is CIP rather than CIF (Incoterms 2020; D30, O105). The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Urgent spare frames by air (Urvania → Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

On 11 May the buyer asks for eight more side frames by air, spares for the Port of Selanu's harbour benches, on top of the twelve on the ship: it changes its order with a line carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The distributor has none left: Valdara casts them on 12 May and packs them on 13 May, and Trasvia trucks them under the Trade Accord's common transit to the distributor's dispatch area in Marrowdale, where the forwarder takes them straight on to Lumeport airport, a cross-dock. They are booked on the afternoon flight KXL209, but the truck from Ferrano is late and the cargo reaches the terminal after the cut-off; the island's airline flies it on the next morning's KXL207 under a direct air waybill. The distributor cancels its despatch advice and replaces it, and the 3PL collects the skid at Selanu airport on 14 May. The distributor pays the air freight and recharges it to the buyer at cost, as a surcharge on its invoice for the frames.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs every bale for the container's verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Batteries for the island's telecom mast, by air (dangerous goods)

The island's telecom company orders back-up batteries, smoke detectors and a coating stripper for its mast from an engineering firm in Bayvarra, carriage paid to the mast site. All three are dangerous goods: the forwarder trucks them to Lumeport airport and the island's airline flies them on its freighter, the batteries and the stripper, which may fly on cargo aircraft only, where the crew can reach them and apart from each other, the smoke detectors in a closed container.
