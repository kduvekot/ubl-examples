How UBL helps

A consortium partner tenders together with a main tenderer. UBL tenders and qualification documents name each partner and its qualifications separately, so the authority can see who contributes what.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Is named in documents between others

  • Tenderer Qualification from Valdara Industries to Lumeport Public Works Department: The manufacturer declares its qualifications and those of its consortium partner. See the example document.

    In it as TendererPartyQualification/AdditionalQualifyingParty (The qualifications of a tenderer party other than the main tenderer party when bidding as a consortium).

  • Tender from Valdara Industries to Lumeport Public Works Department: The tender, with the consortium partner as subcontractor. See the example document.

    In it as SubcontractorParty (The Subcontractor or other Tenderer who participates in the same Tender).

Batteries for the island's telecom mast, by air (dangerous goods)

The island's telecom company orders back-up batteries, smoke detectors and a coating stripper for its mast from an engineering firm in Bayvarra. All three are dangerous goods; the batteries fly in a temperature-controlled container.

Sends

  • Forwarding Instructions to Bayforth Forwarding: The engineering firm instructs the forwarder, with the shipper's declaration of dangerous goods. See the example document.

    In it as ConsignorParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Telecom as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Despatch Advice to Selanu Telecom: The engineering firm announces the goods to the telecom company. See the example document.

    In it as DespatchSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

    Also mentions: Kelaro Air Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Selanu Telecom: Goods and carriage to the site; an export, so no sales tax. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

Receives

  • Order from Selanu Telecom: The telecom company orders back-up power and safety equipment for its mast. Carriage paid to its site: the seller arranges the dangerous goods transport. See the example document.

    In it as SellerSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waybill from Kelaro Air Cargo to Bayforth Forwarding: The air waybill for cargo aircraft only, with the dangerous goods on it. See the example document.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).

    Also mentions: Selanu Telecom as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.
