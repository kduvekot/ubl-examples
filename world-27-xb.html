Saltmere Engineering

Consortium Partner · Bayvarra (XB) · Public procurement

An engineering firm founded in 1994 in Saltmere, the east-coast village it is named after, when Bayvarra's mobile networks were built; it later moved to Marrowdale, on the road and rail to Urvania. It designs telecom mast sites and obtains their permits (a registered architect, Edda Corwin, is on its staff), and installs back-up power and safety equipment at masts across Bayvarra, dangerous goods included, with crews and anchoring gear for ground foundations. It installs and anchors the park benches as Valdara's subcontractor in the public tender, and in the design contest it teams with Brackenmere Furniture for the structure and the anchoring.

How UBL helps

A subcontractor or a member of a group of tenderers is named with its own role. UBL tenders and qualification documents name each member and its qualifications separately, so the authority can see who contributes what and check each one in the register.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 10 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches for the city's parks under procurement law, in two lots: the benches and their installation. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders alone, with an engineering firm as its subcontractor for the installation, backed by a bank guarantee; a furniture maker tenders wooden benches, backed by pledged government bonds. After an electronic auction and the award the authority orders the first release under the framework contract; the manufacturer delivers and invoices the benches, the subcontractor installs them, the authority accepts the release, and the installation is invoiced once accepted.

A design contest for the promenade bench (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority relaunches a design contest for the bench of Lumeport's harbour promenade, after cancelling the first one because no design met the brief. The council raises the prize fund before the contest notice; a corrigendum corrects the site plan, extends the deadline for requests and lets an industrial designer lead a design. The furniture maker and the engineering firm enter as a team, and the furniture maker's own designer takes over the lead; every member of the team is checked in the register, and a small design studio is excluded at the selection. The jury awards the first prize to the team and the second to the manufacturer; the results notice is corrected for a prize amount. Several documents are revised and say which version they supersede. No goods move.

Batteries for the island's telecom mast, by air (dangerous goods)

The island's telecom company orders back-up batteries, smoke detectors and a coating stripper for its mast from an engineering firm in Bayvarra, carriage paid to the mast site. All three are dangerous goods: the forwarder trucks them to Lumeport airport and the island's airline flies them on its freighter, the batteries and the stripper, which may fly on cargo aircraft only, where the crew can reach them and apart from each other, the smoke detectors in a closed container.
