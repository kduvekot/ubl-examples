Nalevi Warehousing

#9 Buyer's Warehouse (3PL) · Country C (XC) · Commerce

Receives the goods for the buyer and confirms what arrived.

In Country C Currency: Coral (XCW). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The buyer's warehouse receives the goods. Its UBL receipt advice confirms what arrived, per line and per package, which lets the buyer approve the invoice for exactly what was delivered.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 2 documents, and is named in 4 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Country B → island Country C)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Sends

  • Receipt Advice to Lumeway Distribution: The buyer's 3PL confirms what arrived, for the buyer.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as BuyerCustomerParty.

Receives

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor announces the shipment to the delivery party named in the order, the buyer's 3PL.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as BuyerCustomerParty; Tidewell Ocean Lines as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order from Velumi Trading to Lumeway Distribution: Delivery terms CIF port of the island: the order names the buyer's 3PL as delivery party; freight and insurance are the seller's business.

    In it as Delivery/DeliveryParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Bill of Lading from Tidewell Ocean Lines to Bayforth Forwarding: The negotiable bill of lading, issued to the forwarder that gave the instructions; it names the insurer of the cargo and the buyer's 3PL as party to notify on arrival.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/NotifyParty (The Party who is notified upon arrival of Goods and when special occurrences (usually pre-defined) take place during a transportation service).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Wavesure Cargo Insurance as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty.

  • Invoice from Lumeway Distribution to Velumi Trading: Goods, sea freight and insurance (CIF) in one invoice; the receivable is sold to the factor, who is the payee.

    In it as Delivery/DeliveryParty (The Party who receives the goods).

    Also mentions: Harbourline Factoring as PayeeParty.

An urgent part of the order by air (Country B → island Country C)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Receives

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor announces the air part to the buyer's 3PL, with the airline as carrier.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as BuyerCustomerParty; Kelaro Air Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Order Change from Velumi Trading to Lumeway Distribution: Part of the quantity is needed urgently: delivery by air, CPT island airport, at an extra charge.

    In it as Delivery/DeliveryParty (The Party who receives the goods).
