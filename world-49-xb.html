Quillmoor Pharma Distribution

Pharmaceutical Wholesaler · Bayvarra (XB) · Commerce

A pharmaceutical wholesaler founded in 1991 beside Lumeport airport, so that medicines that come in by air and go on by air never wait far from the runway. It buys medicines, vaccines and radiopharmaceuticals from makers abroad, keeps them in its cold rooms at 2 to 8 °C and in a freezer room, and supplies pharmacies and hospitals in the three countries of the Accord. It holds the wholesale distribution authorisation of Bayvarra's medicines regulator, which the Accord's mutual recognition makes valid in Urvania and Lisavu, and a licence to hold and transport radioactive material. Its warehouse works every day and through the night, because radiopharmaceuticals arrive and leave by the night and early flights; its own van, with a driver trained for class 7, takes radioactive packages to the airport.

How UBL helps

A wholesaler sells many lines to many customers and must show, for medicines, the lot and its expiry date on every delivery. UBL's despatch advice carries the lot of each line and its expiry, and the transport documents the temperatures the goods need, so the hospital can check the cold chain and the lots when it receives them.

In this world it sends 7 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Medicines and a technetium generator for the island's hospital, by air (cold chain and radioactive material)

Selanu General Hospital orders the vaccines for the island's winter influenza campaign, insulin, a frozen varicella vaccine and its weekly technetium-99m generator from a pharmaceutical wholesaler in Lumeport, carriage paid to Selanu airport. The forwarder books the morning flight of Monday 15 June: the vaccines and the insulin fly at 2 to 8 °C in the airline's active temperature-controlled container, the frozen vaccine in an insulated box on dry ice, and the generator, a Type A package of radioactive material with a Yellow label, under a shipper's declaration of its own, in the aft hold. The wholesaler's own van brings the generator to the cargo terminal at 05:30. On the island the customs broker declares the import for the hospital, free of duty and exempt from the import consumption tax, and the hospital pharmacy's van fetches the goods.
