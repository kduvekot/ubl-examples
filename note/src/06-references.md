# References

- **UBL 2.5:** *Universal Business Language Version 2.5*, OASIS Standard,
  <https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/>.
- **OASIS TC Process:**
  <https://www.oasis-open.org/policies-guidelines/tc-process/>.
- **Global Test Data:** safe example identifiers per country,
  <https://sipico.github.io/global-test-data/>.
- **ISO 3166-1:** *Codes for the representation of names of countries and
  their subdivisions — Part 1: Country code*, including the user-assigned
  codes AA, QM–QZ, XA–XZ and ZZ, <https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html>.
- **ISO 4217:** *Codes for the representation of currencies*; current list
  published by the maintenance agency,
  <https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/data-standards.html>.
- **UN/EDIFACT 5153:** *Duty or tax or fee type name code*,
  <https://unece.org/fileadmin/DAM/trade/untdid/d16b/tred/tred5153.htm>.
- **RFC 2606:** *Reserved Top Level DNS Names* (the `.example` domain),
  <https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2606>.
- **ISO 13616:** *Financial services — International bank account number
  (IBAN)*, whose structure and check digits the world's account numbers
  follow.
