How UBL helps

A tank haulier carries one load at a time. UBL's consignment note carries the dangerous goods as they are loaded in the tank, with the orange plates, the placard, the tunnel restriction and the tank as transport equipment, so that consignor, carrier and consignee read the same data.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Propane by road tanker to Lumeport's district heating (dangerous goods, domestic, Bayvarra)

Kelpstone Gas separates propane from its field's gas at the Gullhaven plant and sells it by road tanker. In winter the Coastal State Water Company, which runs Lumeport's district heating, burns propane in its peak boilers and orders a tanker load when its storage runs low. Kelpstone has it carried by Gullhaven Tank Haulage, licensed to carry dangerous goods in tanks: about 20 t of propane, UN 1965, class 2.1, in a tank semi-trailer that weighs about 17 t with its tractor, so within the Accord's 40 t; the tank shows the class 2.1 placard and orange plates with 23 over 1965. The tanker is weighed on the plant's weighbridge empty and full, drives the day to Lumeport, pumps the propane into the water company's storage and returns empty the next day. The propane is burned, not resold, so Bayvarra's sales tax applies to the sale (D48, O204).
