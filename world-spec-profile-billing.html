Billing

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:billing:1.0

The supplier bills, the customer responds, the supplier reminds and states the account, and the customer says what it paid.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: Invoice, CreditNote, DebitNote, SelfBilledInvoice, SelfBilledCreditNote, ApplicationResponse, InvoiceStatusRequest, InvoiceStatusResponse, Reminder, Statement, RemittanceAdvice.
