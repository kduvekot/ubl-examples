Utility billing

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:utility-billing:1.0

A utility or an energy market party states the consumption at a point of supply, bills it, reminds and is paid.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: UtilityStatement, Invoice, CreditNote, Reminder, RemittanceAdvice.
