Specifications

Every document of the world says which specifications it follows. CustomizationID names the transaction specification at up to three levels, chained with #compliant#, as EN 16931, Peppol and XRechnung do: the Trade Accord's transaction, a national specification built on it, and a community's specification. ProfileID names the Accord's process profile the document belongs to. These are the specifications of the world; the world claims no compliance with real ones such as EN 16931.
