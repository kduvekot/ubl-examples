How UBL helps

The distributor publishes the catalogue its customers order from, buys from manufacturers and sells at home and overseas. UBL links all of it: catalogue items appear unchanged in orders, orders are referenced by despatch advices and invoices, and receipt advices confirm what actually arrived.

In this world it sends 33 and receives 24 documents, and is named in 15 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Order to Valdara Industries: Delivery terms FCA seller's warehouse: the order names the warehouse where the goods are collected and the road carrier the distributor has booked to collect them. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

    Also mentions: Castrano Logistics as Delivery/Despatch/DespatchParty; Trasvia Haulage as Delivery/Despatch/CarrierParty.

  • Transport Execution Plan Request to Trasvia Haulage: The distributor, as transport user, books the truck: collection at the seller's 3PL, delivery at its own distribution centre, across the border. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty, TransportUserParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as Consignment/ConsignorParty.

  • Import Customs Declaration to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The distributor, as importer, declares the goods in Bayvarra and claims the preferential duty rate with the certificate of origin. See the example document.

    In it as ImporterParty, ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

  • Receipt Advice to Castrano Logistics: The distributor confirms what arrived to the party that despatched it, the 3PL, naming the manufacturer as seller. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty.

  • Application Response to Valdara Industries: The distributor's system confirms the invoice was received and accepted. See the example document.

    In it as SenderParty.

  • Remittance Advice to Valdara Industries: The distributor announces the cross-border payment and which invoices it settles. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Crevalto Bank as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Order Response from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer accepts the order and confirms the collection date at its warehouse. See the example document.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Castrano Logistics: The 3PL picks and loads the goods for the manufacturer and announces the despatch; it knows the goods and the truck but not the prices. See the example document.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as SellerSupplierParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Certificate of Origin from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce: The chamber endorses the certificate and makes it available to the importer, who needs it for the import declaration. See the example document.

    In it as ImporterParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ExporterParty.

  • Waybill from Trasvia Haulage: The road consignment note goes to the party that booked the truck, the distributor; it names the manufacturer as consignor. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

  • Transportation Status from Trasvia Haulage: The carrier reports that the truck has cleared the border and gives the expected arrival time. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

Receives
