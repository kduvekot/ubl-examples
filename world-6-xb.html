#6 Distributor

Country B (XB) · Commerce

Buys finished goods from #5, stocks them in its distribution centre, publishes the catalogue and sells to #26 and overseas to #1.

How UBL helps

The distributor publishes the catalogue its customers order from, buys from manufacturers and sells at home and overseas. UBL links all of it: catalogue items appear unchanged in orders, orders are referenced by despatch advices and invoices, and receipt advices confirm what actually arrived.
