Guarantee Certificate: Bank of Lumeport to Lumeport Public Works Department

Document 10 of 39 in the flow A public tender (Bayvarra) · Guarantee Certificate in the UBL 2.5 specification

From Bank of Lumeport to Lumeport Public Works Department, under the Trade Accord's transaction Guarantee certificate.

← Procurement Status · all documents of the flow · Unsubscribe From Procedure Request →

The furniture maker's bank certifies the government bonds immobilised in favour of the authority as the guarantee of the furniture maker's tender.

The XML file is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
