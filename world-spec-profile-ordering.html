Ordering

Process profile of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:ordering:1.0

A buyer orders, the seller responds, the buyer changes or cancels the order and asks for its status.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: Order, OrderResponse, OrderChange, OrderCancellation, DocumentStatusRequest, DocumentStatus.
