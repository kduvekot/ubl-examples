In Urvania Currency: Ferra (XUF). Taxes: Value added tax in the style of the EU VAT: charged at every stage, deducted by businesses, not charged on exports.

How UBL helps

Export customs needs to know what leaves the country, for whom and how. UBL customs declarations reuse the parties, goods and transport details already present in the commercial and transport documents, so the declarant does not have to collect them again.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 8 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: Goods leave Urvania: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Receives

  • Transit Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: The goods travel under customs transit from Urvania to the destination in Bayvarra; the manufacturer, as holder of the procedure, lodges the declaration at the office of departure. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsigneeParty; Ferrano Rail Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The waste processor recovers plastic from production waste and sells it as granulate to the packaging maker on the coast of Bayvarra, carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the barge is loaded. The packaging maker turns it into packaging for the benches and the clothing.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Rinova Recovery: The granulate leaves Urvania; the waste processor declares the export. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Rivermouth Packaging as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Fiumella Barges as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: The call-off leaves Urvania; the manufacturer declares the export. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Public Works Department as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Is named in documents between others

  • Goods Item Passport from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce to Valdara Industries: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel. See the example document.

    In it as ExportingCustomsParty (The Party who is competent of Customs in the exporting country).

    Also mentions: Selanu Chamber of Commerce as ImportingGuarantorParty; Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

  • Proof of Reexportation from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce to Selanu Chamber of Commerce: Urvania's chamber proves the samples were re-exported: they came home through Urvania's customs. See the example document.

    In it as ExportingCustomsParty (The Party who is competent of Customs in the exporting country).

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Urvania)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Urvania one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Receives

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty, CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as RecoveryFacilityParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Waste Movement from Rinova Recovery: The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority. See the example document.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as NotifierParty; Valdara Industries as WasteProducerParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

The sawmill in Upland State saws timber to size. The manufacturer in Urvania asks for a quotation for bench slats to its own dimensions and orders them delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill arranges the truck across the border. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer declares the import into Urvania, where import VAT is due. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Marrowdale Sawmill as ConsignorParty.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Montebrio Power: The power station declares the gas it received in March into Urvania. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Kelpstone Gas as ConsignorParty.
