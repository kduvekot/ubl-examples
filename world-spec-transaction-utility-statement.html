Utility statement

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:utility-statement:1.0

One document type carries the transaction, the Utility Statement, which UBL 2.5 defines as follows: “A supplement to an Invoice or Credit Note, containing information on the consumption of services provided by utility suppliers to private and public customers, including electricity, gas, water, and telephone services.”

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.

Document types: UtilityStatement.
