Digital agreement

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:digital-agreement:1.0

One document type carries the transaction, the Digital Agreement, which UBL 2.5 defines as follows: “A document used to support business parties agreeing on a set of digital processes, terms and conditions to ensure interoperability.”

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.
