#12 Air Carrier

Country C (XC) · Logistics

The island's airline; flies urgent cargo and uses road feeder services on the mainland.

How UBL helps

The air carrier flies urgent shipments and uses road feeder services on the mainland. UBL waybills can name the road carrier as substitute carrier, so the whole route, by truck and by plane, stays under one transport document.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

An urgent part of the order by air (Country B → island Country C)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Sends

  • Waybill to #10 Freight Forwarder: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: #6 Distributor as ConsignorParty; #1 Commercial Buyer as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; #13 Road Carrier as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transportation Status from #17 Airport (XB): The air cargo terminal reports the cargo accepted and built up for the flight.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transportation Status from #17 Airport (XC) to #10 Freight Forwarder: The island's air cargo terminal reports the cargo arrived and ready for customs clearance.

    In it as Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: #1 Commercial Buyer as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.
