Self-billing

Transaction of the Trade Accord in the UBL example world · all specifications

Identifier: urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:self-billing:1.0

The customer bills itself on the supplier's behalf, under a self-billing agreement, and corrects that bill.

Owner: Lumeport Trade Accord Council. Version 1.0.
