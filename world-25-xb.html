#25 Guarantor

Country B (XB) · Finance

Issues guarantees, such as the bid bond for a tender.

How UBL helps

A guarantor stands surety, for example for a bid bond in a tender. UBL's guarantee certificate names the guarantee, the tenderer and the contracting authority, so the authority can check it is valid.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond.

Sends

  • Guarantee Certificate to #2 Contracting Authority: The guarantor notifies the authority of the bid bond deposited for the manufacturer's tender.
