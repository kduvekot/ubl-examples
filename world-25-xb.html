How UBL helps

A guarantor stands surety, for example for a bid bond in a tender. UBL's guarantee certificate names the guarantee, the tenderer and the contracting authority, so the authority can check it is valid.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

Rinova Recovery in Ferrano recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories, Valdara's among them (the waste flow), and sells 120 tonnes of granulate in 120 jumbo bags of one tonne to Rivermouth Packaging on Bayvarra's coast, carriage paid to (CPT) Rivermouth's river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the bags are on board. Before the order Rinova sends its product information for the quarter, with the recycled-content certificate of an accredited certification body (plastics/12). The bags are loaded on Monday 20 April and sail down the river by day; the freight of exported goods is exempt from Urvania's value added tax. Rivermouth turns the granulate into stretch film and garment bags (the packaging flow) (decision D41).

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches for the city's parks under procurement law, in two lots: the benches and their installation. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders alone, with an engineering firm as its subcontractor for the installation, backed by a bank guarantee; a furniture maker tenders wooden benches, backed by pledged government bonds. After an electronic auction and the award the authority orders the first release under the framework contract; the manufacturer delivers and invoices the benches, the subcontractor installs them, the authority accepts the release, and the installation is invoiced once accepted.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

Marrowdale Sawmill in Upland State saws Valdara's bench slats to Valdara's drawing, 1800 × 95 × 38 mm, kiln dried, and sells them by the bundle of 50. Valdara asks for a quotation, which the sawmill gives under Valdara's purchasing terms, for oak with ash as a priced alternative, and orders 96 bundles delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill books the truck across the border: 4,800 slats on 24 heat-treated pallets, about 22.2 t, a quarter's supply for 960 benches. This delivery is oak; from May the sawmill saws ash, as its felling in the managed forest moves to an ash stand. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, against the furniture maker's exemption certificate, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why (decision D43).

Furniture for the department store and for public offices (Bayvarra)

The furniture maker makes the department store's own-label dining furniture, the Elderwick Marrow bench and chair, each in oak and in ash, and supplies it piece by piece: each wood is an item of its own, because the shopper chooses it. The line is for the reopening of the store's home and garden department on Monday 11 May: the stock is delivered in March and held for it, and the first sale is on the day of the reopening. They agree on the item data and the stock policy per piece, the store reports what it sells, and exceptions against agreed thresholds are flagged automatically. The new line is taken on sale-or-return terms for a trial of its first four weeks of sales, and what is above the target at the end of the trial goes back in the store's van, at the maker's cost: the maker credits it in full and the store invoices the transport. The store asked for seat pads as an option; the maker quoted one, and the store sells pads from its own textile range instead. For the public works department the furniture maker also installs, and documents the work for approval.

Apples and pears in a reefer container by sea (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

In the southern autumn the island's fresh-produce wholesaler buys a 40-foot reefer of apples and pears from the fruit growers' cooperative at Harrowmead, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu (CIP): the cooperative books the reefer through its forwarder, which brings it pre-cooled to the packhouse and trucks it back to Lumeport on a generator; the plant health service of Bayvarra's Department of Agriculture certifies the fruit free of the pests the island names. The reefer sails at 0.5 °C and 90 % humidity, its fresh-air vent set for fruit that breathes, on Tidewell's weekly service; at Selanu it waits on the port's power until the wholesaler's carrier takes it to the cold store, where it is unloaded and its temperature log read. Food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import: the island taxes imported food to encourage its own farms.
