How UBL helps

A chamber of commerce certifies that goods come from where the exporter says. UBL certificate-of-origin and goods-item-passport documents carry the application, the endorsements and the goods in a form customs on the other side can read and verify.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Sends

  • Certificate of Origin to Lumeway Distribution: The chamber endorses the certificate and makes it available to the importer, who needs it for the import declaration.

    In it as EndorserParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ExporterParty.

Receives

  • Certificate of Origin from Valdara Industries: The goods qualify for preferential duty in Bayvarra; the manufacturer applies to its chamber of commerce for a certificate of origin.

    In it as EndorserParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ImporterParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Sends

  • Goods Item Passport to Valdara Industries: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel.

    In it as IssuerParty, ExportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Chamber of Commerce as ImportingGuarantorParty; Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty; Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.
