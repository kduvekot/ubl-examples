How UBL helps

A chamber of commerce certifies that goods come from where the exporter says. UBL certificate-of-origin and goods-item-passport documents carry the application, the endorsements and the goods in a form customs on the other side can read and verify.

In this world it sends 3 and receives 3 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor in Bayvarra buys 96 park benches and 12 spare side frames from the manufacturer in Urvania, free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's 3PL warehouse, and books the road carrier itself. The manufacturer confirms the order with the new casting of the side frame, at the same price; Urvania's chamber of commerce certifies the goods' preferential origin; the manufacturer clears them for export, the distributor for import. At goods-in a bench seems to be short and a carton of frames over; the next morning the recount finds the bench's carton on the pallet of spare frames, and the distributor withdraws its first receipt advice and sends a corrected one: everything ordered arrived. The carrier invoices the transport to the distributor, the manufacturer invoices the goods, and the distributor pays across the border.

Sends

  • Certificate of Origin to Lumeway Distribution: The chamber, the body Urvania authorises under the Trade Accord to issue proofs of preferential origin, checks the application, issues the certificate under its own number and is its only endorser: a certificate between Accord members needs no legalisation. It makes the certificate available to the importer, who needs it for the import declaration. See the example document.

    In it as EndorserParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ExporterParty.

Receives

  • Certificate of Origin from Valdara Industries: The goods qualify for preferential duty in Bayvarra; the manufacturer applies to its chamber of commerce for a certificate of origin. The application is Valdara's own document, with its own number; the chamber's e-certificate system gives it a job number when it is lodged. See the example document.

    In it as EndorserParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ImporterParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows two sample benches at the Selanu Chamber of Commerce's trade fair in early June and brings them back. An ATA carnet issued by Urvania's chamber of commerce lets them into the island without its import consumption tax; customs stamp its counterfoils as the samples leave Urvania, cross Bayvarra on its transit vouchers, and enter and leave the island by air. The island's re-exportation voucher goes astray, so after the time limit the island's chamber asks Urvania's chamber for proof of re-exportation, reminds it a month later, and receives the proof; the claim for the island's tax falls away.

Sends

  • Goods Item Passport to Valdara Industries: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel. See the example document.

    In it as IssuerParty, ExportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Chamber of Commerce as ImportingGuarantorParty; Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty; Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

  • Proof of Reexportation to Selanu Chamber of Commerce: Urvania's chamber proves the samples were re-exported, with the stamped re-exportation counterfoil and Urvania's record of their re-importation; the claim of the island's tax falls away. See the example document.

    In it as ExportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Customs Administration of Urvania as ExportingCustomsParty.

Receives

  • Proof of Reexportation Request from Selanu Chamber of Commerce: The re-exportation limit of 30 June has passed and the island's customs have no discharged re-exportation voucher: it went astray. The island's chamber asks Urvania's chamber for proof that the samples left. See the example document.

    In it as ExportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

  • Proof of Reexportation Reminder from Selanu Chamber of Commerce: No answer after a month: a reminder. See the example document.

    In it as ExportingGuarantorParty.

    Also mentions: Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.
