How UBL helps

Water is billed on metered consumption. With UBL the Utility Statement carries the meter readings and the Invoice the amount; the tax exemption for water is stated explicitly, and a Reminder follows the same structure when a household pays late.

In this world it sends 5 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.
