How UBL helps

Water is billed on metered consumption. With UBL the Utility Statement carries the meter readings and the Invoice the amount; the tax exemption for water is stated explicitly, and a Reminder follows the same structure when a household pays late.

In this world it sends 7 documents and receives 3 documents, and is named in 1 document between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays for electricity, water and the telephone. A business with a meter read every month is billed every month on its reading. A household pays on account: a twelfth of the year's estimate every month by direct debit, the meter read once a year, and an annual statement and a settlement bill for the actual year. In Urvania the electricity bill carries the grid operator's network charges beside the retail company's energy; in Lumeport the water company also runs the district heating, and its bill carries the city's sewage levy; the island's telephone company bills its business customers every month. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with the meter readings.

Propane by road tanker to Lumeport's district heating (dangerous goods, domestic, Bayvarra)

Kelpstone Gas separates propane from its field's gas at the Gullhaven plant and sells it by road tanker. In winter the Coastal State Water Company, which runs Lumeport's district heating, burns propane in its peak boilers and orders a tanker load when its storage runs low. Kelpstone has it carried by Gullhaven Tank Haulage, licensed to carry dangerous goods in tanks: about 20 t of propane, UN 1965, class 2.1, in a tank semi-trailer that weighs about 17 t with its tractor, so within the Accord's 40 t; the tank shows the class 2.1 placard and orange plates with 23 over 1965. The tanker is weighed on the plant's weighbridge empty and full, drives the day to Lumeport, pumps the propane into the water company's storage and returns empty the next day. The propane is burned, not resold, so Bayvarra's sales tax applies to the sale.
