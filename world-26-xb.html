How UBL helps

A retailer buys from the distributor and sells to consumers. Besides orders and invoices, UBL lets it share forecasts, promotions and sales data with its supplier, and issue a structured purchase receipt to the consumer.

In this world it sends 14 and receives 14 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)

The retailer buys delivered duty paid (DDP) at its shop: the distributor arranges the transport and bears all costs, so the distributor, not the retailer, books the truck and receives the transport documents. The retailer shares forecasts, promotions and its stock levels so the distributor can plan replenishment.

Sends

  • Forecast to Lumeway Distribution: The retailer forecasts its sales for the coming weeks.

    In it as SenderParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Retail Event to Lumeway Distribution: A promotion next month will lift demand; the distributor needs to know before it orders from the manufacturer.

    In it as SenderParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Inventory Report to Lumeway Distribution: The retailer reports its stock per item, so the distributor can replenish.

    In it as RetailerCustomerParty, InventoryReportingParty.

  • Order to Lumeway Distribution: Delivery terms DDP retailer's shop: the order names only where to deliver; transport is the distributor's business.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Receipt Advice to Lumeway Distribution: The shop confirms the delivery and reports a damaged carton.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Purchase Receipt to Robin Ashdale: A consumer buys in the shop and gets a receipt.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

Receives

  • Catalogue from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor's catalogue of items and prices; items carry the identifiers of the identifier issuing agency.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Codana Identification Agency as CatalogueLine/Item/StandardItemIdentification/IssuerParty.

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor announces the delivery and the truck that brings it.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor has sold its receivables to the factor, so the invoice names the factor as payee.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Harbourline Factoring as PayeeParty.

  • Credit Note from Lumeway Distribution: The shop reported a damaged carton in its receipt advice; the distributor credits it. The factor stays the payee, because it owns the receivable being reduced.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Harbourline Factoring as PayeeParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Transport Execution Plan Request from Lumeway Distribution to Trasvia Haulage: Because it sold DDP, the distributor books the outgoing truck from its distribution centre to the shop.

    In it as Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

  • Waybill from Trasvia Haulage to Lumeway Distribution: The outgoing consignment note goes to the distributor, who contracted the carriage; the retailer is only the consignee.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty (The Party who receives the goods).

Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)

Every business and household pays a utility. UBL bills a utility with an Invoice and supplements it with a Utility Statement: the consumption behind the bill, per connection, with meter readings (UBL 2.5, section 2.3.7.2, Utility Billing). The three utilities differ on purpose: electricity and water use the energy and water supply structure, telecom its own telecommunications structure, and the three countries tax them differently.

Sends

  • Remittance Advice to Coastal State Water Company: The shop pays its water bill and says which invoice the transfer settles.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayerFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution; Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Receives

  • Utility Statement from Coastal State Water Company: The shop's water consumption, read from its meter.

    In it as ReceiverParty, SubscriberParty.

  • Invoice from Coastal State Water Company: The water bill for the shop. Water is exempt from the state sales tax, and the invoice says so with the exemption reason: leaving the tax out would say nothing (IND6).

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

Furniture for the department store and for public offices (Bayvarra)

The furniture maker supplies the department store with a garden furniture line made for it. They agree on the item data and the stock policy per store, the store reports what it sells, and exceptions against agreed thresholds are flagged automatically. Slow sellers are taken back. For the public works department the furniture maker also installs, and documents the work for approval.

Sends

  • Request For Quotation to Brackenmere Furniture: The store wants its own garden furniture line and asks for a quotation.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Trade Item Location Profile to Brackenmere Furniture: The store sets the replenishment policy per item and store.

    In it as SenderParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Exception Criteria to Brackenmere Furniture: Both agree when a deviation is worth a message: thresholds for forecast variance and sales.

    In it as SenderParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Product Activity to Brackenmere Furniture: Weekly sales and stock movements of the line.

    In it as SenderParty.

  • Order to Brackenmere Furniture: The store orders the line. Delivery terms DAP store: the furniture maker delivers with its own van.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

Receives

  • Quotation from Brackenmere Furniture: The furniture maker quotes the line.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Item Information Request from Brackenmere Furniture: To plan production, the furniture maker asks the store for sales and stock data of the line.

    In it as ReceiverParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Exception Notification from Brackenmere Furniture: Sales of the bench set run far below forecast: past the agreed threshold, so the furniture maker notifies the store.

    In it as ReceiverParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Invoice from Brackenmere Furniture: Furniture sold for resale: exempt from the state sales tax, which the store charges its consumers.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.

  • Instruction For Returns from Brackenmere Furniture: The bench set does not sell; the furniture maker asks for the remaining stock back to free shelf space.

    In it as RetailerCustomerParty.

  • Credit Note from Brackenmere Furniture: The returned bench sets are credited.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

Packaging for the benches, clothing for the store

The packaging maker supplies the manufacturer in Urvania and the textile mill at home; the textile mill sells clothing to the department store, delivered duty paid (DDP) at the store.

Sends

  • Order to Lumeport Textile Mill: Clothing for the store. Delivery terms DDP store: the mill delivers.

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.

  • Purchase Receipt to Robin Ashdale: The consumer buys a shirt; the receipt shows the state sales tax, the only point where Bayvarra's tax is charged.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

Receives

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeport Textile Mill: The mill announces the delivery with the road carrier it booked.

    In it as DeliveryCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice from Lumeport Textile Mill: Clothing for resale: exempt from sales tax, which the store charges its customers.

    In it as AccountingCustomerParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

    Also mentions: Bank of Lumeport as PaymentMeans/PayeeFinancialAccount/FinancialInstitutionBranch/FinancialInstitution.
