Kelpstone Gas

#42 Gas Producer · Bayvarra (XB) · Commerce · proposed addition to the 26+C set

Produces natural gas from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast; treats it at its plant on the shore and sends it by pipeline to the power station and the grid of Urvania.

How UBL helps

Gas is sold by energy, measured at the border, and crosses it by pipeline every day. UBL carries the monthly sale as an Invoice in energy units, the export and import by fixed installation (transport mode 7) in the customs declarations, and a Reminder when the buyer pays late.

In this world it sends 4 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Sends

  • Export Customs Declaration to Customs and Border Service of Bayvarra: The gas producer declares March's gas leaving Bayvarra by pipeline, in one monthly declaration. See the example document.

    In it as ExporterParty, CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Montebrio Power as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice to Montebrio Power: The gas producer invoices March's gas to the power station, in ferra as the supply contract says: an export, so no Bayvarran sales tax. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Invoice to Ferrano Energy: The gas producer invoices the gas it fed into the grid for the retail company's customers. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty, SellerSupplierParty.

  • Reminder to Montebrio Power: The power station has not paid by the due date. The gas producer reminds it in lume, its own currency: the reminder shows the ferra invoice at the exchange rate and adds Bayvarra's surcharge for the month late. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

Receives

  • Remittance Advice from Montebrio Power: The power station pays the invoice and the surcharge, in ferra. See the example document.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Import Customs Declaration from Montebrio Power to Customs Administration of Urvania: The power station declares the gas it received in March into Urvania. See the example document.

    In it as ConsignorParty (The Party who is reponsible for sending the goods).
