In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer. Sales to businesses that resell, exports and sales to public bodies are exempt, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

An insurer covers the cargo while it travels. UBL transport documents can carry the insurance policy with the insurer, the policy holder and the insured value, so every party in the transport chain knows the goods are covered.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Is named in documents between others

  • Forwarding Instructions from Lumeway Distribution to Bayforth Forwarding: The distributor instructs its forwarder: book the sea freight to the island and insure the cargo (CIF obliges the seller to insure).

    In it as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty (The Party providing the insurance under this Insurance Policy).

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Bill of Lading from Tidewell Ocean Lines to Bayforth Forwarding: The negotiable bill of lading, issued to the forwarder that gave the instructions; it names the insurer of the cargo and the buyer's 3PL as party to notify on arrival.

    In it as Shipment/InsurancePolicy/InsurerParty (The Party providing the insurance under this Insurance Policy).

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Nalevi Warehousing as Shipment/Consignment/NotifyParty.
