In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states, and the federal territory in the north, levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the final use. A sale for resale or manufacture (goods sold on as they are, or that become part of the product sold, the packaging that goes to the customer included), exports, sales to public bodies and services to a business are exempt, and the invoice says so; what a business uses up or keeps (fuel and energy it burns, consumables, tools, machines, vehicles, buildings) is taxed as a retail sale, at the rate of the state where it is delivered (O204).

How UBL helps

An insurer covers the cargo while it travels. UBL transport documents can carry the insurance policy with the insurer, the policy holder and the insured value, so every party in the transport chain knows the goods are covered.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 2 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys 104 benches, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock, since it buys by the pallet and two pallets add little to the freight of a container already booked. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, are CIF prices: they hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor pays to its freight forwarder together with the forwarder's truck to the port, and the invoice bills the goods alone. CIF stays as common practice and as the flow's theme, although the ICC's guidance note to CIF says that where goods are handed over to a carrier at a container terminal, the appropriate rule is CIP rather than CIF (Incoterms 2020; D30, O105). The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.
