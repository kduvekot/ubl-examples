In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states, and the federal territory in the north, levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the sale to the final consumer. Sales to businesses that resell, exports and sales to public bodies are exempt, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

Import customs checks goods arriving over the land border and through the seaport. UBL import and transit declarations carry the same consignment and goods data as the export declaration on the other side, which makes it possible to compare them.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 7 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor, as importer, declares the goods in Bayvarra and claims the preferential duty rate with the certificate of origin. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The waste processor recovers plastic from production waste and sells it as granulate to the packaging maker on the coast of Bayvarra, carriage paid to (CPT) the river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the barge is loaded. The packaging maker turns it into packaging for the benches and the clothing.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Rivermouth Packaging: The packaging maker declares the import in Bayvarra. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as ConsignorParty.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Bayforth Forwarding: The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the distributor at the seaport. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ExporterParty; Velumi Trading as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches and street furniture for the city's parks under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeport Public Works Department: Under DAP the buyer clears the import, so the contracting authority declares the goods in Bayvarra. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

The sawmill in Upland State saws timber to size. The manufacturer in Urvania asks for a quotation for bench slats to its own dimensions and orders them delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill arranges the truck across the border. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Marrowdale Sawmill: The slats leave Bayvarra; the sawmill declares the export at the land border. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeport Textile Mill: The mill declares the bales in Bayvarra. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Cotton Ginnery as ConsignorParty.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Kelpstone Gas: The gas producer declares March's gas leaving Bayvarra by pipeline, in one monthly declaration. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Montebrio Power as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.
