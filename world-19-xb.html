#19 Customs (XB)

Country B (XB) · Government

Import customs at the land border crossing and at the seaport.

How UBL helps

Import customs checks goods arriving over the land border and through the seaport. UBL import and transit declarations carry the same consignment and goods data as the export declaration on the other side, which makes it possible to compare them.

Documents it exchanges

  • ImportCustomsDeclaration
  • TransitCustomsDeclaration
