In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states, and the federal territory in the north, levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the final use. A sale for resale or manufacture (goods sold on as they are, or that become part of the product sold, the packaging that goes to the customer included), exports, sales to public bodies and services to a business are exempt, and the invoice says so; what a business uses up or keeps (fuel and energy it burns, consumables, tools, machines, vehicles, buildings) is taxed as a retail sale, at the rate of the state where it is delivered (O204).

How UBL helps

Import customs checks goods arriving over the land border and through the seaport. UBL import and transit declarations carry the same consignment and goods data as the export declaration on the other side, which makes it possible to compare them.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 11 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor buys park benches free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads Valdara sells Lumeway 192 benches delivered at place (DAP) Lumeway's rail siding in Marrowdale. The seller arranges and pays the transport: it books Ferrano Rail Cargo under their framework contract for container slots. The carrier's tractor brings the two packed 40-foot containers from Castrano's warehouse to the intermodal hub at Ferrano, a pre-carriage by road of the same contract, and the hub lifts them onto the container train FRC 4417; the consignment note follows the Accord's uniform rules for international carriage by rail, modelled on the CIM. Urvania's rail infrastructure manager, which runs the tracks and the train paths since the state separated them from the train operators, reports the train's progress to the carrier. The goods travel under the Accord's common transit, which Valdara lodges the evening before departure, to Bayvarra customs' inland office at Marrowdale, where Lumeway declares the import on 15 April and claims the Accord preference. Lumeway empties the first container the next day; the second waits until 22 April, because the siding's fork-lift is in repair, and runs two days past the five days' free time. Ferrano Rail Cargo charges the demurrage to Valdara in a debit note to its freight invoice, and Valdara passes it on to Lumeway, which bears a delay in unloading under DAP, in a debit note to its goods invoice: the world's debit notes (decision D37).

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeway Distribution: At Bayvarra customs' inland office in Marrowdale, the office of destination of the Accord's common transit, the distributor, as importer under DAP, declares the benches for import and claims the Accord preference with the statement on origin on Valdara's invoice (D37, D18). See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

Rinova Recovery in Ferrano recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories, Valdara's among them (the waste flow), and sells 120 tonnes of granulate in 120 jumbo bags of one tonne to Rivermouth Packaging on Bayvarra's coast, carriage paid to (CPT) Rivermouth's river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the bags are on board. Before the order Rinova sends its product information for the quarter, with the recycled-content certificate of an accredited certification body (plastics/12). The bags are loaded on Monday 20 April and sail down the river by day; the freight of exported goods is exempt from Urvania's value added tax. Rivermouth turns the granulate into stretch film and garment bags (the packaging flow) (decision D41).

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Rivermouth Packaging: The packaging maker declares the import in Bayvarra. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as ConsignorParty.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The commercial buyer on the island buys 104 benches, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock, since it buys by the pallet and two pallets add little to the freight of a container already booked. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, are CIF prices: they hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor pays to its freight forwarder together with the forwarder's truck to the port, and the invoice bills the goods alone. CIF stays as common practice and as the flow's theme, although the ICC's guidance note to CIF says that where goods are handed over to a carrier at a container terminal, the appropriate rule is CIP rather than CIF (Incoterms 2020; D30, O105). The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Bayforth Forwarding: The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the distributor at the seaport. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ExporterParty; Velumi Trading as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches for the city's parks under procurement law, in two lots: the benches and their installation. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders alone, with an engineering firm as its subcontractor for the installation, backed by a bank guarantee; a furniture maker tenders wooden benches, backed by pledged government bonds. After an electronic auction and the award the authority orders the first release under the framework contract; the manufacturer delivers and invoices the benches, the subcontractor installs them, the authority accepts the release, and the installation is invoiced once accepted.

Receives

Wood: slats for the benches and timber for furniture (Bayvarra → Urvania, and at home)

Marrowdale Sawmill in Upland State saws Valdara's bench slats to Valdara's drawing, 1800 × 95 × 38 mm, kiln dried, and sells them by the bundle of 50. Valdara asks for a quotation, which the sawmill gives under Valdara's purchasing terms, for oak with ash as a priced alternative, and orders 96 bundles delivered at its factory (DAP), so the sawmill books the truck across the border: 4,800 slats on 24 heat-treated pallets, about 22.2 t, a quarter's supply for 960 benches. This delivery is oak; from May the sawmill saws ash, as its felling in the managed forest moves to an ash stand. The furniture maker buys timber at home; selling for manufacture, against the furniture maker's exemption certificate, the sawmill charges no sales tax and says why (decision D43).

Receives

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled, as the EU's is: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, by pipeline across the border. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, the customs declarations of a pipeline, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Apples and pears in a reefer container by sea (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

In the southern autumn the island's fresh-produce wholesaler buys a 40-foot reefer of apples and pears from the fruit growers' cooperative at Harrowmead, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu (CIP): the cooperative books the reefer through its forwarder, which brings it pre-cooled to the packhouse and trucks it back to Lumeport on a generator; the plant health service of Bayvarra's Department of Agriculture certifies the fruit free of the pests the island names. The reefer sails at 0.5 °C and 90 % humidity, its fresh-air vent set for fruit that breathes, on Tidewell's weekly service; at Selanu it waits on the port's power until the wholesaler's carrier takes it to the cold store, where it is unloaded and its temperature log read. Food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import: the island taxes imported food to encourage its own farms.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Bayforth Forwarding: The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the cooperative at the seaport. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Harrowmead Fruit Growers as ExporterParty; Hafefo Fresh Produce as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Medicines and a technetium generator for the island's hospital, by air (cold chain and radioactive material)

Selanu General Hospital orders the vaccines for the island's winter influenza campaign, insulin, a frozen varicella vaccine and its weekly technetium-99m generator from a pharmaceutical wholesaler in Lumeport, carriage paid to Selanu airport. The forwarder books the morning flight of Monday 15 June: the vaccines and the insulin fly at 2 to 8 °C in the airline's active temperature-controlled container, the frozen vaccine in an insulated box on dry ice, and the generator, a Type A package of radioactive material with a Yellow label, under a shipper's declaration of its own, in the aft hold. The wholesaler's own van brings the generator to the cargo terminal at 05:30. On the island the customs broker declares the import for the hospital, free of duty and exempt from the import consumption tax, and the hospital pharmacy's van fetches the goods.
