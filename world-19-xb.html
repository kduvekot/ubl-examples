In Bayvarra Currency: Lume (XBL). Taxes: A federal country: its states, and the federal territory in the north, levy a retail sales tax, charged once, on the final use. A sale for resale or manufacture (goods sold on as they are, or that become part of the product sold, the packaging that goes to the customer included), exports, sales to public bodies and services to a business are exempt, and the invoice says so; what a business uses up or keeps (fuel and energy it burns, consumables, tools, machines, vehicles, buildings) is taxed as a retail sale, at the rate of the state where it is delivered.

How UBL helps

Customs checks goods arriving and leaving over the land border, through the seaport and through the airport. UBL import, export and transit declarations carry the same consignment and goods data as the declaration on the other side of the border, which makes it possible to compare them.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 11 documents, and is named in 0 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)

The distributor in Bayvarra buys 96 park benches and 12 spare side frames from the manufacturer in Urvania, free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's 3PL warehouse, and books the road carrier itself. The manufacturer confirms the order with the new casting of the side frame, at the same price; Urvania's chamber of commerce certifies the goods' preferential origin; the manufacturer clears them for export, the distributor for import. At goods-in a bench seems to be short and a carton of frames over; the next morning the recount finds the bench's carton on the pallet of spare frames, and the distributor withdraws its first receipt advice and sends a corrected one: everything ordered arrived. The carrier invoices the transport to the distributor, the manufacturer invoices the goods, and the distributor pays across the border.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeway Distribution: The distributor, as importer, declares the goods in Bayvarra and claims the preferential duty rate with the certificate of origin. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as ConsignorParty.

Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)

For full container loads Valdara sells Lumeway 192 benches delivered at place (DAP) Lumeway's rail siding in Marrowdale. The seller arranges and pays the transport: it books Ferrano Rail Cargo under their framework contract for container slots. Castrano Logistics packs the two 40-foot containers at its warehouse, each with one of the two production lots of 9 and 10 April, and announces the despatch to Lumeway; the despatch advice and the consignment note name the lot of each container. The carrier's tractor brings the two packed containers from Castrano's warehouse to the intermodal hub at Ferrano, a pre-carriage by road of the same contract, and the hub lifts them onto the container train FRC 4417; the consignment note follows the Accord's uniform rules for international carriage by rail, modelled on the CIM. Urvania's rail infrastructure manager, which runs the tracks and the train paths since the state separated them from the train operators, reports the train's progress to the carrier. The goods travel under the Accord's common transit, which Valdara lodges the evening before departure, to Bayvarra customs' inland office at Marrowdale, where Lumeway declares the import on 15 April and claims the Accord preference. Lumeway empties the first container the next day; the second waits until 22 April, because the siding's fork-lift is in repair, and runs two days past the five days' free time. Ferrano Rail Cargo charges the demurrage to Valdara in a debit note to its freight invoice, and Valdara passes it on to Lumeway, which bears a delay in unloading under DAP, in a debit note to its goods invoice: the world's debit notes.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeway Distribution: At Bayvarra customs' inland office in Marrowdale, the office of destination of the Accord's common transit, the distributor, as importer under DAP, declares the benches for import and claims the Accord preference with the statement on origin on Valdara's invoice. See the example document.

Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)

Rinova Recovery in Ferrano recovers polyethylene from the production waste of several factories, Valdara's among them (the waste flow), and sells 120 tonnes of granulate in 120 jumbo bags of one tonne to Rivermouth Packaging on Bayvarra's coast, carriage paid to (CPT) Rivermouth's river quay: the seller books the barge and pays the freight, the buyer takes the risk once the bags are on board. Before the order Rinova sends its product information for the quarter, with the recycled-content certificate of an accredited certification body. The bags are loaded on Monday 20 April and sail down the river by day; the freight of exported goods is exempt from Urvania's value added tax. Rivermouth turns the granulate into stretch film and garment bags (the packaging flow).

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The distributor wins a new customer on the island. It introduces itself, both join the Trade Accord's digital agreement, and the buyer orders 104 benches and spare frames, cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge: 96 benches for the Port of Selanu's harbour parks and two more pallets of four for its own stock. The prices, 520 lume a bench and 68 a frame, hold the sea freight and the cargo insurance, which the distributor buys through its freight forwarder; the invoice bills the goods alone. The forwarder clears the export and books the container; the carrier reports its ship and cargo to the island's authorities before it arrives; the buyer's customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them. The distributor has sold the receivable to a factor, which sends the statement of account. A frame arrives crushed and a pallet's illegible mark makes customs inspect the container, so the distributor credits the frame and the cost of the inspection, and the buyer pays the factor. The buyer sells two of the benches in its shop.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Bayforth Forwarding: The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the distributor at the seaport. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

A public tender (Bayvarra)

The contracting authority buys park benches for the city's parks under procurement law, in two lots: the benches and their installation. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders alone, with an engineering firm as its subcontractor for the installation, backed by a bank guarantee; a furniture maker tenders wooden benches, backed by pledged government bonds. After an electronic auction and the award the authority orders the first release under the framework contract; the manufacturer delivers and invoices the benches, the subcontractor installs them, the authority accepts the release, and the installation is invoiced once accepted.

Receives

Modification: bench slats modified under contract (Bayvarra → Urvania)

From October to March, the southern summer, Marrowdale Sawmill's thermal modification kiln is full with its own decking and cladding, so it buys kiln slots for the park-bench slats from Tostara Wood Modification at Conciaro, east of Ferrano, under a season agreement that gives each slot a job number in advance. In January it orders one job for its season stock of park-bench slats, the standing product it sells to Valdara each quarter: the modification, planing, end sealing and bundling of 96 bundles. It sends its own kiln-dried ash blanks, 4,800 of 1,830 × 101 × 51 mm, across the border in two loads of its timber truck. The blanks remain the sawmill's and are only worked in Urvania, so they do not enter free circulation: the plant holds an authorisation for inward processing and enters them under it, and Urvania's import VAT waits until the sawmill releases the finished slats to sell them in Urvania. Bayvarra records the blanks as a permanent export for processing. The plant reports what it holds for the sawmill and when the second charge is ready, stores the bundles until the sawmill calls them off, and hands them to the carrier the sawmill books, against the sawmill's pick-up release. It invoices the processing in ferra, a service to a customer established abroad and so outside Urvania's VAT, and the sawmill pays within the discount period.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Marrowdale Sawmill: The blanks leave Bayvarra for processing abroad and do not come back; the sawmill declares the export at the land border. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Tostara Wood Modification as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's cotton is picked from March to May. Before the harvest the ginnery advances the farm a part of the value of its expected delivery, and the textile mill in Lumeport tells the ginnery how much it expects to buy this season. The farm delivers seed cotton to the gin; the ginnery weighs it and writes the farm's invoice itself (self-billing), and credits the farm for the part that classing puts a grade lower. The mill orders bales free on board (FOB) the island port and books the sea freight through its forwarder. The island's plant protection service certifies the bales free of pests, the ginnery declares the export and states the container's verified gross mass, and the ship carries the container to Lumeport, where the mill clears it. The mill pays the ginnery: money flows to the island for once.

Receives

  • Import Customs Declaration from Lumeport Textile Mill: The mill declares the bales in Bayvarra. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Selanu Cotton Ginnery as ConsignorParty.

Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)

Urvania's energy market is unbundled: a power station produces electricity, a grid operator runs the networks and reads the meters, and a retail company buys the energy and bills the customers. The gas comes from an offshore field off Bayvarra's north coast, treated at the producer's plant on the shore and sent by pipeline across the border. The producer sells the month's gas to the power station and to the retail company for its gas customers; it declares the export in one declaration, with a consignment for each buyer, and each buyer declares its own import. The documents show the grid operator's metering with the correction a gas meter needs, the wholesale and network invoices, and a reminder across currencies when the power station pays late.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Kelpstone Gas: The gas producer declares March's gas leaving Bayvarra by pipeline, in one monthly declaration: one shipment, the month's flow measured at the border metering station, with a consignment for each buyer, the power station and the retail company. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

Apples and pears in a reefer container by sea (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

In the southern autumn the island's fresh-produce wholesaler buys a 40-foot reefer of apples and pears from the fruit growers' cooperative at Harrowmead, carriage and insurance paid to the Port of Selanu (CIP): the cooperative books the reefer through its forwarder, which brings it pre-cooled to the packhouse and trucks it back to Lumeport on a generator; the plant health service of Bayvarra's Department of Agriculture certifies the fruit free of the pests the island names. The reefer sails at 0.5 °C and 90 % humidity, its fresh-air vent set for fruit that breathes, on Tidewell's weekly service; at Selanu it waits on the port's power until the wholesaler's carrier takes it to the cold store, where it is unloaded and its temperature log read. Food pays the island's import consumption tax like every import: the island taxes imported food to encourage its own farms.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Bayforth Forwarding: The forwarder, as customs agent, declares the export for the cooperative at the seaport. See the example document.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Harrowmead Fruit Growers as ExporterParty; Hafefo Fresh Produce as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty.

Medicines and a technetium generator for the island's hospital, by air (cold chain and radioactive material)

Selanu General Hospital orders the vaccines for the island's winter influenza campaign, insulin, a frozen varicella vaccine and its weekly technetium-99m generator from a pharmaceutical wholesaler in Lumeport, carriage paid to Selanu airport. The forwarder books the morning flight of Monday 15 June: the vaccines and the insulin fly at 2 to 8 °C in the airline's active temperature-controlled container, the frozen vaccine in an insulated box on dry ice, and the generator, a Type A package of radioactive material with a Yellow label, under a shipper's declaration of its own, in the aft hold. The wholesaler's own van brings the generator to the cargo terminal at 05:30. On the island the customs broker declares the import for the hospital, free of duty and exempt from the import consumption tax, and the hospital pharmacy's van fetches the goods.

Receives
