In Country C Currency: Coral (XCW). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The buyer's bank sends the payment abroad. The payment reference and payee account come straight from the UBL invoice, so the payment arrives with the information the seller's bank needs to match it.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Overseas by container ship, cost insurance and freight (Country B → island Country C)

The commercial buyer on the island buys cost, insurance and freight (CIF) to the port of discharge. The distributor therefore pays the sea freight and the cargo insurance; it hands both to its freight forwarder. The buyer takes over at the port of discharge: its customs broker clears the goods and its 3PL receives them.
