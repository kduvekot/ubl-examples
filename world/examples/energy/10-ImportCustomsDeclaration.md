# Import Customs Declaration: Ferrano Energy to Customs Administration of Urvania

*Example 10 of the flow "Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)" · [Import Customs Declaration in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-IMPORT-CUSTOMS-DECLARATION-SCHEMA)*

The retail company declares its own consignment of March's gas, 2,400 MWh fed into the grid at the city gate for its gas customers, into Urvania (D44).

[The XML file](10-ImportCustomsDeclaration.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
