# Invoice: Kelpstone Gas to Ferrano Energy

*Example 7 of the flow "Gas and electricity in an unbundled market (Bayvarra's gas field → Urvania)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

The gas producer invoices the gas it fed into the grid for the retail company's customers.

[The XML file](07-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
