# Invoice: Quillmoor Pharma Distribution to Selanu General Hospital

*Example 14 of the flow "Medicines and a technetium generator for the island's hospital, by air (cold chain and radioactive material)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

The wholesaler invoices the goods, carriage paid to Selanu airport; an export, so no sales tax.

[The XML file](14-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
