# Remittance Advice: Brightmarket Stores to Coastal State Water Company

*Example 7 of the flow "Electricity, water and telecom (one utility in each country)" · [Remittance Advice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-REMITTANCE-ADVICE-SCHEMA)*

The shop pays its water bill and says which invoice the transfer settles.

[The XML file](07-RemittanceAdvice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
