# Invoice: Fiumella Barges to Rinova Recovery

*Example 7 of the flow "Recycled plastic granulate by river barge (Urvania → Bayvarra)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

Under CPT the seller pays the carriage: the barge operator invoices the river freight to the waste processor.

[The XML file](07-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
