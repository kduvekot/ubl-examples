# Transit Customs Declaration: Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Urvania

*Example 4 of the flow "Containers by rail under transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)" · [Transit Customs Declaration in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-TRANSIT-CUSTOMS-DECLARATION-SCHEMA)*

The goods travel under customs transit from Urvania to the destination in Bayvarra; the manufacturer, as holder of the procedure, lodges the declaration at the office of departure.

[The XML file](04-TransitCustomsDeclaration.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.

## Values and their reasons

### The document itself

- `UBLVersionID` = **2.5**  
  The documents of the world use UBL 2.5.
- `CustomizationID` = **urn:ubl-examples:world**  
  The example world is a customization of UBL: it uses its own code lists for countries and currencies.
- `ID` = **VI-TD-26-0329**  
  The transit declaration number.
- `IssueDate` = **2026-04-14**  
  Lodged at the office of departure before the train leaves.
