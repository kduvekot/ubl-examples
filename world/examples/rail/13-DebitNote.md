# Debit Note: Valdara Industries to Lumeway Distribution

*Example 13 of the flow "Containers by rail under the Accord's common transit (Urvania → Bayvarra)" · [Debit Note in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-DEBIT-NOTE-SCHEMA)*

Under DAP the buyer bears the costs of a delay in unloading, so Valdara passes the carrier's demurrage on to Lumeway as a debit note to its goods invoice.

[The XML file](13-DebitNote.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
