# Invoice: Kelpstone Gas to Coastal State Water Company

*Example 4 of the flow "Propane by road tanker to Lumeport's district heating (dangerous goods, domestic, Bayvarra)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

The propane delivered, by the weighbridge's net weight, with Coastal State's sales tax: the water company burns it in its boilers, so the sale is not one for resale.

[The XML file](04-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
