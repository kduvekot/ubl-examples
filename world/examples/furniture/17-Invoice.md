# Invoice: Brackenmere Furniture to Lumeport Public Works Department

*Example 17 of the flow "Furniture for the department store and for public offices (Bayvarra)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

Furniture and installation; a sale to a public body is exempt from sales tax, and the invoice says so.

[The XML file](17-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
