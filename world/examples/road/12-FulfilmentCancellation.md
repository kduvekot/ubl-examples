# Fulfilment Cancellation: Lumeway Distribution to Valdara Industries

*Example 12 of the flow "Finished goods by truck across the land border (Urvania → Bayvarra)" · [Fulfilment Cancellation in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-FULFILMENT-CANCELLATION-SCHEMA)*

The 3PL had put one bench's frame carton (parcel 2 of 3) on the pallet of spare frame cartons, which it looks like, so goods-in reported 95 benches and one carton of frames over. The next morning the distributor's recount reads the carton's label: it is the 96th bench's. The receipt advice reported a shortage and a carton over that are not there, so the distributor cancels it.

[The XML file](12-FulfilmentCancellation.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
