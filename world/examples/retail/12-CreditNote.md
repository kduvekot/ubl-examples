# Credit Note: Lumeway Distribution to Brightmarket Stores

*Example 12 of the flow "Distributor to retailer, delivered duty paid (domestic, Bayvarra)" · [Credit Note in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-CREDIT-NOTE-SCHEMA)*

The shop reported a damaged carton in its receipt advice; the distributor credits it. The factor stays the payee, because it owns the receivable being reduced.

[The XML file](12-CreditNote.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
