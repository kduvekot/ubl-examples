# Invoice: Saltmere Engineering to Selanu Telecom

*Example 5 of the flow "Batteries for the island's telecom mast, by air (dangerous goods)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

Goods and carriage to the site; an export, so no sales tax.

[The XML file](05-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.

## Values and their reasons

### The document itself

- `UBLVersionID` = **2.5**  
  The earliest version of UBL 2 that defines every element of the document, as UBLVersionID asks: computed from the published models of UBL 2.0 to 2.5; Invoice/DefaultLanguageCode first appears in UBL 2.5.
- `CustomizationID` = **urn:ubl-examples:world:trns:billing:1.0**  
  The specification the document follows, at each level it claims, chained with #compliant#: the Trade Accord's transaction "Billing". Each has a public description on the world's pages.
- `ProfileID` = **urn:ubl-examples:world:profile:billing:1.0**  
  The Trade Accord's process profile the document belongs to: billing, a generic, versioned profile, not the flow.
- `ProfileExecutionID` = **STL-TX-26-124393**  
  The collaboration this document belongs to: Selanu Telecom's order of back-up batteries, smoke detectors and coating stripper for its mast from Saltmere Engineering. Selanu Telecom started it and gave it this identifier, which every document of it repeats.
- `ID` = **26-0132**  
  The seller's invoice number.
- `UUID` = **40aff29f-0459-5985-901d-8a4a505d1f5d**  
  A universally unique identifier of the document, derived from its place in the world (UUID version 5), so it stays the same on every build.
- `IssueDate` = **2026-06-04**  
  Issued on despatch.
- `IssueTime` = **16:51:00-01:00**  
  The time the sender's system issued the document, in the sender's local time, with its offset from UTC.
- `DueDate` = **2026-07-06**  
  As the payment terms below count it: 30 days net; 1.5 % surcharge per month late. The count ends on Saturday 4 July, no business day in Bayvarra, the payee's country, so the payment is due on the next one, Monday 6 July.
- `InvoiceTypeCode` = **380** (listID `UN/EDIFACT EDED 1001`)  
  A commercial invoice, as UN/EDIFACT 1001 codes it. The code is from the world's copy of UN/EDIFACT 1001 Document name: 380 = Commercial invoice.
- `Note` = **Carriage paid to the mast site: the prices include the carriage by air and road. The exporter of the goods covered by this document declares that these goods are of Bayvarran preferential origin under the Trade Accord (criterion S).**  
  Why the invoice has no separate freight line. The exporter's statement on origin: the proof of origin the buyer's import declaration refers to, to claim the Trade Accord's preference; the seller makes it as the exporter, since it sends the goods across an Accord border to the buyer.
- `DocumentCurrencyCode` = **XBL** (listID `ISO 4217 Alpha`)

  The number of invoice lines, as a check on the transfer.

  The language of the texts that carry no language identifier: English, the working language between the countries; a seller from Urvania or the island also gives its notes in its own language. The code is from the world's copy of ISO 639 Language: en = English.
