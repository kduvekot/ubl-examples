# Invoice: Selanu Cotton Ginnery to Lumeport Textile Mill

*Example 16 of the flow "Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

The bales, free on board. No tax applies on the island, and the invoice says so.

[The XML file](16-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.
