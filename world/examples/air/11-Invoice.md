# Invoice: Lumeway Distribution to Velumi Trading

*Example 11 of the flow "Urgent spare frames by air (Urvania → Bayvarra → island Lisavu)" · [Invoice in the UBL 2.5 specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.5/UBL-2.5.html#S-INVOICE-SCHEMA)*

The distributor invoices the eight frames at its trade price, carriage paid to the island airport, and recharges the air freight at cost as a surcharge, which the buyer accepted in its order change.

[The XML file](11-Invoice.xml) is valid against the UBL 2.5 schemas and the world's code lists. Below, every value with its place in the document and the reason for it.

## Values and their reasons

- `ProfileExecutionID` = **26-0416**  
  The collaboration this document belongs to: Velumi's order from Lumeway, with its change by air, from the order to the credit note. Velumi Trading started it and gave it this identifier, which every document of it repeats.

  The seller's invoice number.
- `UUID` = **5e8ce777-529c-5b9e-99b6-c68de9c624a7**  
  A universally unique identifier of the document, derived from its place in the world (UUID version 5), so it stays the same on every build.
- `IssueDate` = **2026-05-15**  
  Issued the day after the flight, with the forwarder's invoice of the air freight it recharges.
- `IssueTime` = **13:37:00-01:00**
