# Sources of the world's external code lists that had no copy in world/codelists/sources/

Additions to `world/codelists/sources/PROVENANCE.md`. Every source was accessed on **2026-11-11**. The rows follow the same format (directory | source URL, publisher and what it is | version/date). Downloaded files sit in the directory named in the first column. Copyrighted documents (ISO, ITU, IMO, WCO, BIC) are **not** copied: only their URL, SHA-256 and the quoted text are recorded (see "Cited, not copied" below).

Verdicts: **confirmed** (code present, meaning agrees) · **differs** (code present, but the world's name or description differs from the source) · **not confirmed** (no reachable authoritative text).

### ISO20022ServiceLevel (ExternalServiceLevel1Code): 3 codes confirmed, 2 texts differ

- NURG: "NonurgentPayment", "Payment must be executed as a non-urgent transaction, which is typically identified as an ACH or low value transaction." **Differs**: the world has "NonUrgentPayment" and "…which is not to be processed by a real-time gross settlement system." (older wording).
- URGP: "UrgentPayment", "Payment must be executed as an urgent transaction cleared through a real-time gross settlement system, which is typically identified as a wire or high value transaction." **Differs slightly**: the world leaves out the second clause.
- SDVA: "SameDayValue", "Payment must be executed with same day value to the creditor." Confirmed.

### ISO20022CashAccountType (ExternalCashAccountType1Code): 5 codes confirmed, 2 definitions differ

CARD "CardAccount" "Account used for credit card payments." — **differs** (world: "Account used for card payments only.").
SLRY "Salary" "Accounts used for salary payments." — **differs** (world: "Account used for the payment of salaries.").

### HSSections: all 21 confirmed (titles shortened)

WCO HS 2022 table of contents: "SECTION I LIVE ANIMALS; ANIMAL PRODUCTS" … "SECTION XXI WORKS OF ART, COLLECTORS' PIECES AND ANTIQUES". There are exactly 21 sections, I to XXI. lists.json says the world's titles are shortened. Examples: XII "FOOTWEAR, HEADGEAR, UMBRELLAS, SUN UMBRELLAS, WALKING-STICKS, …" becomes "Footwear, headgear, umbrellas"; XVII "VEHICLES, AIRCRAFT, VESSELS AND ASSOCIATED TRANSPORT EQUIPMENT" becomes "Vehicles, aircraft, vessels and transport equipment". No title contradicts the source.

### ISO6346: all 6 codes confirmed in structure; 2 descriptions differ

BIC https://www.bic-code.org/size-type-code/: "Length 2 - 20 Feet, 4 - 40 Feet, 5 - 45 Feet, M - 48 Feet; Height 2 - 8 Feet 6 Inches, 5 - 9 Feet 6 Inches "High Cube"; Type G1 - General Purpose Container, R1 - Refrigerated Container, U1 - Open Top Container". This confirms 22G1, 22R1, 22U1, 42G1, 45G1 and 45R1 as compositions. Note: BIC's table gives 45 ft (13716 mm) as length code **5**. The second character 5 in 45G1/45R1 is the **height** 9 ft 6 in, so 45xx = 40 ft high cube, as the world says.
BIC https://www.bic-code.org/type-code-designation/ (Table 3):
- G: type designation "General purpose container without ventilation", group GP; G0 "Opening(s) at one end or both ends"; **G1 "Passive vents at upper part of cargo space"**. The world's G1 note "General purpose container without ventilation." repeats the group designation and so contradicts G1's own characteristic. Suggest: "General purpose container with passive vents at upper part of cargo space."
- R: "Thermal container"; R0 "Refrigerated", RE, "Mechanically refrigerated"; **R1 "Refrigerated and heated", RT, "Mechanically refrigerated and heated"**. The world's 22R1/45R1 notes say "Mechanically refrigerated container" (that is R0). **Differs.**
- U: "Open-top container", UT; U0 "Opening(s) at one or both ends"; U1 "Opening(s) at one or both ends, plus removable top member(s) in end frames". The world's "open top" is consistent.

### ADRTunnel: all 10 confirmed (BAM DGG-Info, ADR 2025); the world's list leaves out two ADR values

BAM ADR detail pages, field "Tunnel restriction code", quoting ADR 8.6.4:
- B: UN 3101 — "Passage forbidden through tunnels of category B, C, D and E."
- B1000C: UN 0081 — "Carriage where the total net explosive mass per transport unit: - exceeds 1000 kg: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category B, C, D and E; - does not exceed 1000 kg: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category C, D and E"
- B/D: UN 1001 — "Tank carriage: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category B, C, D and E; Other carriage: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category D and E."
- B/E: UN 2015, 1745, 1381 — "Tank carriage: … B, C, D and E; Other carriage: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category E."
- C: UN 2977 (radioactive module) — "Tunnel restriction code C: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category C, D and E."
- C/D: UN 1017 — "Tank carriage: … C, D and E; Other carriage: … D and E."
- C/E: UN 1093, UN 2810 ("highly toxic" entries) — "Tank carriage: … C, D and E; Other carriage: … E."
- D: UN 1950 — "Passage forbidden through tunnels of category D and E."
- D/E: UN 1202, 1203, 2920 — "Bulk or tank carriage: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category D and E; Other carriage: Passage forbidden through tunnels of category E."
- E: UN 3480, 3481, 3090 — "Passage forbidden through tunnels of category E."
**Not in the world's list:** C5000D (UN 0161: "exceeds 5000 kg: … C, D and E; does not exceed 5000 kg: … D and E") and "-" (UN 2911: "Tunnel restriction code -: Passage allowed through all tunnels"). Leaving them out is harmless while no world goods need them. The world's examples are consistent with BAM: 3480 → E, 2920 PG II → D/E, and 2911 carries no tunnel code (ADR "-").
The UNECE ADR 2025 text itself is blocked (403), also for WebFetch.

### BaselAnnexIV: 7 codes confirmed; R1 text differs; Annex IV is being replaced

Convention text 2025, Annex IV: "D1 Deposit into or onto land, (e.g., landfill, etc.)", "D10 Incineration on land", "R1 Use as a fuel (other than in direct incineration) or other means to generate energy", "R2 Solvent reclamation/regeneration", "R3 Recycling/reclamation of organic substances which are not used as solvents", "R4 Recycling/reclamation of metals and metal compounds", "R5 Recycling/reclamation of other inorganic materials".
