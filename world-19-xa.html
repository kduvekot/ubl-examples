In Country A Currency: Aster (XAW). Taxes: Value added tax in the style of the EU VAT: charged at every stage, deducted by businesses, not charged on exports.

How UBL helps

Export customs needs to know what leaves the country, for whom and how. UBL customs declarations reuse the parties, goods and transport details already present in the commercial and transport documents, so the declarant does not have to collect them again.

In this world it sends 0 and receives 5 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Finished goods by truck across the land border (Country A → Country B)

The distributor buys free carrier (FCA) at the manufacturer's warehouse. Because the buyer arranges the transport, the distributor books the road carrier itself and tells the seller in the order which truck company will pick the goods up and where. The seller's 3PL hands the goods over; the seller clears them for export, the distributor for import.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: Goods leave Country A: the manufacturer, as exporter, declares them to the customs office at the land border.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Containers by rail under transit (Country A → Country B)

For full container loads the manufacturer sells delivered at place (DAP) the distributor's rail siding. Now the seller arranges and pays the transport: it books the container train and moves the goods under a transit declaration, so that import formalities happen at the destination. The intermodal hub loads the containers on the train.

Receives

  • Transit Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: The goods travel under customs transit from Country A to the destination in Country B; the manufacturer, as holder of the procedure, lodges the declaration at the office of departure.

    In it as CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsigneeParty; Ferrano Rail Cargo as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

A public tender (Country B)

The contracting authority buys equipment under procurement law. The notices are published through the government information service; the manufacturer tenders together with a consortium partner, backed by a bid bond. After the award the authority places orders under the contract and the manufacturer delivers and invoices.

Receives

  • Export Customs Declaration from Valdara Industries: The call-off leaves Country A; the manufacturer declares the export.

    In it as CustomsDeclaration/CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeport Public Works Department as CustomsDeclaration/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as CustomsDeclaration/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

Samples to a trade fair on the island (temporary export)

The manufacturer shows samples at a trade fair on the island and brings them back. An ATA carnet, guaranteed by the chambers of commerce of both countries, avoids paying duties.

Is named in documents between others

  • Goods Item Passport from Ferrano Chamber of Commerce to Valdara Industries: The chamber issues the carnet to the manufacturer, who holds it while the samples travel.

    In it as ExportingCustomsParty (The Party who is competent of Customs in the exporting country).

    Also mentions: Selanu Chamber of Commerce as ImportingGuarantorParty; Island Customs Service as ImportingCustomsParty.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Country A)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Country A one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Receives

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries: The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority.

    In it as ReceiverParty, CustomsParty.

    Also mentions: Rinova Recovery as RecoveryFacilityParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Waste Movement from Rinova Recovery: The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority.

    In it as ReceiverParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as NotifierParty; Valdara Industries as WasteProducerParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.
