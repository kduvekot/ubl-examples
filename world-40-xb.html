How UBL helps

Its raw material comes from overseas on a schedule. UBL forecasts and their revisions tell the ginnery what to expect, and the bill of lading, customs documents and invoice of every shipment arrive in one structure.

In this world it sends 9 and receives 4 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Cotton: from the island's farms to the textile mill (Lisavu → Bayvarra)

The island's export. The farm delivers seed cotton to the ginnery, which weighs and grades it and pays by self-billing: it writes the invoice for the farm. The ginnery sells the bales free on board (FOB) the island port: the textile mill arranges the sea freight through its forwarder. The ginnery declares the export, weighs the container for its verified gross mass and gets a goods certificate.

Sends

    In it as SenderParty, BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Forecast Revision to Selanu Cotton Ginnery: Demand for clothing is up; the mill revises its forecast upwards. See the example document.

  • Order to Selanu Cotton Ginnery: Bales of grade cotton. Delivery terms FOB the island port: the mill arranges the sea freight. See the example document.

Packaging for the benches, clothing for the store

The packaging maker supplies the manufacturer in Urvania and the textile mill at home; the textile mill sells clothing to the department store, delivered duty paid (DDP) at the store.

Sends

    In it as BuyerCustomerParty, Delivery/DeliveryParty.
