Kelaro Air Cargo

#12 Air Carrier · Lisavu (XL) · Logistics

The island's airline; flies urgent cargo and uses road feeder services on the mainland.

In Lisavu Currency: Coral (XLC). Taxes: An island without a general sales tax or VAT: the state is financed by customs duty and an import consumption tax, both collected by customs at import. Domestic sales carry no tax, and the invoice says so.

How UBL helps

The air carrier flies urgent shipments and uses road feeder services on the mainland. UBL waybills can name the road carrier as substitute carrier, so the whole route, by truck and by plane, stays under one transport document.

In this world it sends 1 and receives 1 documents, and is named in 3 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

An urgent part of the order by air (Bayvarra → island Lisavu)

The buyer needs part of the quantity before the ship arrives. It changes the order: that part goes by air, carriage paid to (CPT) the island's airport. The forwarder books the airline, whose road feeder service (the road carrier) trucks the cargo to the airport.

Sends

  • Waybill to Bayforth Forwarding: The air waybill; the airline's road feeder service, run by the road carrier, is named as substitute carrier for the truck leg to the airport.

    In it as SenderParty, CarrierParty.

    Also mentions: Lumeway Distribution as ConsignorParty; Velumi Trading as Shipment/Consignment/ConsigneeParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/SubstituteCarrierParty.

Receives

  • Transportation Status from Lumeport Airport Cargo Terminal: The air cargo terminal reports the cargo accepted and built up for the flight.

    In it as ReceiverParty, Consignment/CarrierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Despatch Advice from Lumeway Distribution to Nalevi Warehousing: The distributor announces the air part to the buyer's 3PL, with the airline as carrier.

    In it as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as BuyerCustomerParty.

  • Transportation Status from Selanu Airport Cargo to Bayforth Forwarding: The island's air cargo terminal reports the cargo arrived and ready for customs clearance.

    In it as Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).

    Also mentions: Velumi Trading as Consignment/ConsigneeParty.

  • Invoice from Bayforth Forwarding to Lumeway Distribution: The forwarder invoices the air freight, including the road feeder leg, to the distributor, who sold carriage paid to the island airport.

    In it as Delivery/Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty (The Party who provides the transport of goods between named points).
