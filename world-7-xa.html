How UBL helps

Waste movements must be announced and tracked before the waste leaves the site. UBL's waste notification and waste movement documents describe the waste, its producer and the facility that recovers or disposes of it, and the same parties then appear on the invoice for the removal service.

In this world it sends 2 and receives 0 documents, and is named in 1 documents between other parties. They are listed per flow below.

Production waste to recovery (domestic, Country A)

The factory's hazardous production waste may only move with a notification. In Country A one administration acts as customs and as competent authority for waste permits. The waste processor recovers the material and invoices the service.

Sends

  • Waste Movement to Customs Administration of Country A: The recovery facility reports each movement received to the competent authority.

    In it as SenderParty, RecoveryFacilityParty.

    Also mentions: Valdara Industries as NotifierParty; Valdara Industries as WasteProducerParty; Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.

  • Invoice to Valdara Industries: The waste processor invoices collection and recovery.

    In it as AccountingSupplierParty.

Is named in documents between others

  • Waste Notification from Valdara Industries to Customs Administration of Country A: The manufacturer, as producer and notifier, notifies the planned movements to the competent authority.

    In it as RecoveryFacilityParty (The Party recovering the waste material).

    Also mentions: Trasvia Haulage as Shipment/Consignment/CarrierParty.
